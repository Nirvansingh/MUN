ISLAMIC REPUBLIC OF IRAN

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Iran is a major Middle Eastern power with significant influence across the region through its network of allied militias and governments. It is a key player in global energy markets and nuclear non-proliferation debates, maintaining a complex relationship with the West.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Tehran
Largest City: Tehran
Official Name: Islamic Republic of Iran
Government Type: Unitary Islamic theocratic republic
Head of State: Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei
Head of Government: President Masoud Pezeshkian
Official Languages: Persian (Farsi)
Population: ~89 million
Area: 1,648,195 km² (17th largest)
Currency: Iranian Rial (IRR)
GDP (Nominal): ~$410 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$4,600

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Oil and gas, petrochemicals, agriculture (pistachios, saffron, caviar), automotive, steel, manufacturing, carpets
Major Exports: Crude oil, petrochemicals, natural gas, fruits, nuts, carpets, cement, steel
Major Imports: Machinery, grains, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, electronics, soybeans, rice, livestock
Major Trade Partners: China, Turkey, UAE, India, Iraq, Russia, Pakistan, Afghanistan

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: One of the largest militaries in the Middle East (~575,000 active personnel). Powerful paramilitary force (IRGC). Significant missile and drone capabilities. Network of allied militias across the Middle East.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT signatory); IAEA monitors enrichment program — enrichment capability at near-weapons grade (60%). No confirmed nuclear weapon.
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: Not on Council (last served 2021-22)
Major International Organizations: UN, OPEC, OIC, ECO, NAM, SCO (since 2024), BRICS (since 2024)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Major power in the Middle East — influence extends to Iraq, Syria, Lebanon (Hezbollah), Yemen (Houthis), Palestine (Hamas), Afghanistan
Neighbouring Countries: Iraq, Turkey, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Turkmenistan, Afghanistan, Pakistan
Strategic Importance: Controls Strait of Hormuz (chokepoint for ~20% of global oil); major energy producer; key player in Middle East security; nuclear program
Important Maritime Routes: Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, Gulf of Oman, Caspian Sea
Important Geography: Zagros Mountains, Dasht-e Lut desert, Persian Gulf coastline, Caspian Sea coast

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Holds world's second-largest natural gas reserves (South Pars); fourth-largest oil reserves; significant refining capacity but constrained by sanctions
Natural Resources: Oil, natural gas, coal, copper, iron ore, zinc, lead, chromium, salt, limestone
Technology Sector: Growing domestic tech sector (digital services, ride-sharing); nuclear technology; drone and missile development; restricted by international sanctions
Major Companies: National Iranian Oil Co., NIOC, Iran Khodro (automotive), Mobarakeh Steel, Golgohar Mining, Petropars

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High literacy rate (~89%); strong STEM education; high university enrollment (especially women)
Internet Penetration: ~85% of population; heavily filtered and monitored
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.780 (High, ranked 78th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 24/100 (ranked 147th)
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — state-controlled media; strict censorship; journalists frequently detained
Human Rights Overview: Severe restrictions on political dissent, women's rights (hijab laws), LGBTQ+ rights, religious minorities (Bahá'í), and freedom of expression; high use of capital punishment

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Economic survival under sanctions, nuclear program advancement, regional influence expansion, resistance to Israeli normalization, strengthening ties with Russia and China
Foreign Policy Overview: Anti-Western, anti-Israel; support for proxy forces across Middle East; axis of resistance against US-Israel influence; strategic partnership with Russia and China
Relations With:
  USA: Adversarial — no diplomatic relations since 1980; sanctions; nuclear tensions; proxy conflicts
  China: Strategic partner — largest trading partner; oil exports; Belt and Road; arms cooperation
  Russia: Strategic partner — military cooperation (drones, Syria); nuclear energy (Bushehr); aligned against US
  India: Historically friendly; energy imports; Chabahar Port development; but tensions over Afghanistan and Pakistan ties
  Pakistan: Mixed — border tensions (Balochistan); trade limited; historical rivalry for Islamic world leadership
  EU: Strained — nuclear deal collapse; sanctions; human rights concerns; limited trade
  Middle East: Supports Assad (Syria); Hezbollah (Lebanon); Houthis (Yemen); Hamas (Palestine); rivals Saudi Arabia and Israel
  Neighbouring Countries: Dominant influence in Iraq; supports Assad (Syria); tensions with Afghanistan (water, refugees)

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: Russia (strategic partner), China (strategic partner), Syria, Hezbollah (Lebanon), Houthis (Yemen), Hamas
Major Rivals: USA, Israel, Saudi Arabia, UAE

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Nuclear program advancement, regional influence, sanctions relief, Strait of Hormuz control, energy exports, axis of resistance support
Current Major Challenges: Severe economic sanctions; inflation (40%+); unemployment; water scarcity; environmental degradation; protests (Woman, Life, Freedom); ageing leadership succession

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Iran has the world's second-largest natural gas reserves after Russia.
• The Strait of Hormuz (controlled by Iran) carries ~20% of global oil supply.
• Iran is one of the world's oldest continuous civilizations (Persia, ~2,500 years).
• Iran has the largest Shia Muslim population globally.
