NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) export corridors in the Persian Gulf.
• Protecting shipping lanes through the Strait of Hormuz and Bab-el-Mandeb.
• Acting as a neutral mediator to resolve regional maritime and trade conflicts.
• Expanding global sovereign wealth investments through the Qatar Investment Authority (QIA).
• Promoting national food security through diversified supply chains.
• Developing Hamad Port as a major regional shipping and logistics hub.
• Supporting political stability in the Middle East to secure trade lanes.
• Defending critical offshore gas fields (North Field) from security threats.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Prioritizes securing global LNG supply chains. Promotes Hamad Port connectivity and alternative shipping routes.
• Maritime Security: Supports freedom of navigation. Promotes diplomatic dialogue to secure shipping lanes rather than military coalitions.
• Free Trade: Strong advocate for open energy trade and WTO compliance.
• Sanctions: Opposes unilateral economic sanctions, especially those affecting energy transit.
• International Cooperation: Coordinates via GCC, OIC, Arab League, and UN.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ World's leading exporter of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG).
✓ Vast financial reserves and global investments (QIA).
✓ Trusted neutral mediator role, maintaining dialogue with all major actors.
✓ Modern port infrastructure (Hamad Port) with high efficiency.
✓ Strategic alliance with the US (Major Non-NATO Ally status).
✓ Close defense and economic cooperation with Turkey.
✓ Host to major international diplomatic forums.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High dependency on imported food and raw materials.
✗ Small military force, relying on foreign security guarantees.
✗ Geographic exposure to Strait of Hormuz shipping bottlenecks.
✗ Vulnerability of energy export terminals to regional escalations.
✗ Economic susceptibility to changes in global energy prices.
✗ Dependency on a single export commodity (gas/oil).

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Qatar's LNG exports are crucial for global energy stability and supply chains.
• Neutral mediation is the most effective tool to resolve maritime security disputes.
• Energy contracts are commercial agreements and must be protected from politics.
• Hamad Port operations comply with the highest international security standards.
• Qatar supports freedom of navigation and UNCLOS.
• Unilateral sanctions harm developing states and worsen global supply chains.
• Regional security must be built on dialogue and respect for sovereignty.
• Food security must be protected through unrestricted access to agricultural imports.
