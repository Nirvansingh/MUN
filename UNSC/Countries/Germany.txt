================================================================
GERMANY
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: Federal Republic of Germany
Capital: Berlin
Government: Federal Parliamentary Republic
Current Leader: Chancellor Olaf Scholz
Population: ~84 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent Member)
Major International Organizations: UN, EU, NATO, G7, OECD, WTO, IMO

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• France
• United States
• United Kingdom
• Italy
• EU Member States
• NATO Members
• Japan

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• Russia
• Iran
• North Korea

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing export routes for high-value industrial manufacturing (automotive, machinery).
• Diversifying energy supply chains away from Russian natural gas.
• Protecting land and sea trade corridors within the European Single Market.
• Securing critical maritime infrastructure in the North and Baltic Seas.
• Enforcing international law and UNCLOS in global transit choke points.
• Implementing the Supply Chain Due Diligence Act to enforce human rights.
• Supporting EU-wide maritime security operations in the Red Sea.
• Protecting digital supply chain infrastructure from state-sponsored cyber threats.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Promotes supply chain diversification and resilience. Enacts laws (Due Diligence Act) to monitor global supplier standards.
• Maritime Security: Strongly supports UNCLOS. Deploys naval assets in NATO and EU operations (Operation Aspides) to protect trade lanes.
• Free Trade: Strong advocate for open, rules-based free trade within the EU framework.
• Sanctions: A leading supporter of EU and G7 sanctions against Russia and Iran.
• International Cooperation: Focuses on cooperation within the EU, G7, NATO, and UN to maintain trade security.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Energy transition and securing LNG supply lines.
• Security of undersea pipelines and communication cables in the Baltic Sea.
• Impact of Red Sea shipping delays on German industrial production.
• Implementation of the Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (Lieferkettengesetz).
• EU-China trade relationships and automotive tariffs.
• Cybersecurity of port management and logistics databases.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Largest economy in Europe, central to EU trade and industrial supply chains.
✓ High level of digital and logistical infrastructure efficiency.
✓ Leading global exporter of machinery, automobiles, and chemical products.
✓ Active participant in EU-led maritime security operations.
✓ Strong cybersecurity defense framework coordinated by BSI.
✓ High political influence within the European Union.
✓ Close partnership with France, driving European policy.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Extreme dependency on imported raw materials and critical minerals.
✗ Stretched naval capacity, limiting large-scale global power projection.
✗ High energy costs impacting domestic industrial competitiveness.
✗ Susceptibility of domestic manufacturing to container transport bottlenecks.
✗ Regulatory bottlenecks in adapting to rapid technology shifts.
✗ Trade dependency on the Chinese consumer market.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Supply chain due diligence laws protect human rights and are not trade barriers.
• Energy diversification is necessary for national sovereignty and economic security.
• German naval deployments are defensive and comply with international law.
• Germany supports multilateralism and the WTO dispute settlement framework.
• Port cybersecurity measures comply with EU-wide safety standards.
• Sanctions are target-oriented and comply with international law.
• Critical maritime infrastructure requires coordinated European protection.
• Germany seeks open markets based on fair and reciprocal standards.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How do Germany's supply chain due diligence laws affect exporters in developing nations?
Q2. Why has Germany delayed expanding its naval presence in the Indo-Pacific?
Q3. How does Germany reconcile its energy transit needs with the phase-out of Russian gas?
Q4. How do EU tariffs on Chinese EVs align with Germany's free trade advocacy?
Q5. What checks prevent German technology from being used in foreign surveillance systems?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Russia: Will Russia support an independent investigation into Nord Stream pipeline sabotage?
• China: How does China guarantee fair market access for foreign automotive firms?
• Iran: Will Iran stop actions threatening commercial trade routes in the Persian Gulf?
• USA: How does the US intend to align its domestic inflation reduction laws with WTO rules?
• Egypt: How can we cooperate to mitigate Suez Canal trade bottlenecks?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Any dilution of environmental or human rights standards in trade treaties.
• Resolutions that restrict EU-wide trade policy decisions.
• Rejection of UNCLOS as the core of maritime law.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: France, EU members, US, UK, G7 states, Japan.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: China (seek cooperation on climate and trade rules first).
• Possible compromises: Support technical assistance for developing states' port security.
• Main objectives: Promote European-led maritime initiatives, defend UNCLOS, secure supply routes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with structured, logical, and policy-focused precision.
✓ Emphasize the importance of the European Union Single Market.
✓ Highlight Germany's transition to green energy supply chains.
✓ Frame supply chain security around human rights and sustainability.
✓ Support French statements on European strategic autonomy.
