================================================================
UNSC RESOURCE GUIDE: USEFUL SOURCES & TRADE WATCHDOGS
================================================================

This document lists the most reliable organizations and databases to monitor for real-time shipping and trade data during committee.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTERGOVERNMENTAL MARITIME BODIES
----------------------------------------------------------------
• International Maritime Organization (IMO):
  - Website: https://www.imo.org
  - Focus: The specialized UN agency responsible for the safety and security of shipping and prevention of marine pollution.
• UN Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD):
  - Website: https://unctad.org
  - Focus: Publishes the annual Review of Maritime Transport, the definitive statistical analysis on global fleet sizes, container volumes, and transit costs.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. TRADE & ECONOMIC WATCHDOGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• World Trade Organization (WTO):
  - Website: https://www.wto.org
  - Focus: Tracks global trade disputes, tariff changes, and trade restrictive measures under national security exceptions.
• World Bank Group - Global Trade Portal:
  - Website: https://www.worldbank.org
  - Focus: Tracks food security indices, fertilizer supply chains, and infrastructure development loans.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. INDUSTRY INTELLIGENCE & RESEARCH ORGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Lloyd's List Intelligence:
  - Website: https://lloydslistintelligence.com
  - Focus: Provides real-time tracking of merchant shipping, vessel registrations, insurance status, and port delays.
• ICC International Maritime Bureau (IMB):
  - Website: https://www.icc-ccs.org/icc/imb
  - Focus: Tracks global piracy incidents and acts of armed robbery against ships in real-time.
