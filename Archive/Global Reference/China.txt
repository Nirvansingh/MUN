PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
China is the world's second-largest economy and a rising global power challenging US hegemony across trade, technology, and military domains. As a P5 member and the world's largest manufacturer, it plays a decisive role in几乎所有 international issues.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Beijing
Largest City: Shanghai
Official Name: People's Republic of China
Government Type: Single-party socialist republic
Head of State: President Xi Jinping
Head of Government: Premier Li Qiang
Official Languages: Standard Chinese (Mandarin)
Population: ~1.41 billion
Area: 9,596,960 km² (4th largest)
Currency: Renminbi (CNY)
GDP (Nominal): ~$19.5 trillion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$13,800

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Manufacturing, technology, construction, real estate, automotive, electronics, steel, consumer goods
Major Exports: Broadcasting equipment, computers, integrated circuits, telephones, machinery, textiles
Major Imports: Crude oil, natural gas, integrated circuits, iron ore, soybeans, machinery, aircraft
Major Trade Partners: USA, Japan, South Korea, Vietnam, Germany, Australia, Russia, Taiwan

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Largest active military in the world (~2 million personnel). Rapidly modernizing navy and air force. Major investments in anti-access/area denial (A2/AD) capabilities, hypersonic weapons, and space-based systems.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Recognized nuclear weapon state (NPT). ~500 warheads, rapidly expanding arsenal.
UN Membership: Founding member (1945); took ROC seat in 1971
UNSC Status: P5 (permanent member with veto power)
Major International Organizations: UN, G20, BRICS, SCO, APEC, WTO, Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Dominant power in East Asia; growing influence in Africa, Middle East, Latin America via Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)
Neighbouring Countries: India, Russia, Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Afghanistan, Pakistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, Laos, Vietnam, North Korea
Strategic Importance: Controls South China Sea trade routes; key player in global supply chains; largest trading partner for 120+ countries
Important Maritime Routes: South China Sea, East China Sea, Malacca Strait (critical import chokepoint)
Important Geography: Himalayas (southwest border with India), Taklamakan and Gobi Deserts, Yangtze River economic belt

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: World's largest coal producer and consumer; largest renewable energy producer (solar, wind, hydro); largest electricity grid globally
Natural Resources: Coal, rare earth elements (dominant global supplier), iron ore, gold, copper, lead, zinc, uranium
Technology Sector: Rapidly advancing — major AI, 5G, and semiconductor industries (Huawei, Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu, ByteDance, SMIC)
Major Companies: ICBC, China Construction Bank, Tencent, Alibaba, PetroChina, Sinopec, Huawei, BYD, CATL

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High literacy rate (~97%); increasingly high tertiary enrollment
Internet Penetration: ~78% — world's largest online population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.788 (High, ranked 75th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 42/100 (ranked 65th)
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — state-controlled media, strict censorship, Great Firewall
Human Rights Overview: Severe restrictions on political expression, religious freedom (Xinjiang), and digital privacy; mass surveillance via social credit system; concerns over treatment of Uyghurs and Tibetans

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: National rejuvenation (Chinese Dream), economic self-sufficiency, technological sovereignty, reunification with Taiwan, countering US influence
Foreign Policy Overview: Assertive expansionism — South China Sea claims, Belt and Road infrastructure lending, strategic partnership with Russia, competition with US and India, deepening Africa ties
Relations With:
  USA: Strategic competitor; trade war, technology decoupling, Taiwan tensions
  China: —
  Russia: Strategic partnership; aligned against US hegemony; energy and military cooperation
  India: Border tensions (Doklam, Galwan); strategic rivalry in South Asia and Indian Ocean
  Pakistan: All-weather ally; major infrastructure investment (CPEC); military and nuclear cooperation
  EU: Economic partner but strategic competitor; tensions over human rights, trade imbalances
  Middle East: Growing engagement — energy imports from Gulf, diplomacy with Iran and Saudi Arabia
  Neighbouring Countries: Mixed — dominant economic partner; territorial disputes with India, Vietnam, Philippines

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: Pakistan, Russia (strategic partnership), North Korea, Cambodia, Myanmar (junta), Iran (strategic partner)
Major Rivals: USA, India, Japan, Australia, European Union, NATO

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Economic growth, technological self-sufficiency, energy security, reunification with Taiwan, securing trade routes, countering US influence
Current Major Challenges: Economic slowdown and property crisis; demographic decline (aging population); technology sanctions from US; debt trap diplomacy backlash; Xinjiang and Tibet international criticism; tensions over Taiwan

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• China builds more skyscrapers in a year than any other country.
• China controls over 60% of global rare earth element production.
• The South China Sea is the site of disputed island militarization.
• China's Belt and Road Initiative spans over 140 countries.
