NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing and re-opening Black Sea shipping lanes for commercial exports.
• Defending against Russian naval blockades and missile attacks on ports.
• Protecting and expanding agricultural grain exports (Black Sea Grain Corridor).
• Securing international military and cyber aid to protect maritime infrastructure.
• Sanctioning the Russian shadow fleet and restricting Russian shipping.
• Developing and protecting Danube River trade corridors as alternative transit.
• Clearing sea mines from territorial and neutral Black Sea waters.
• Defending sovereign territorial claims over Crimea and surrounding EEZs.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Emphasizes the critical role of Ukrainian grain in global food security. Accuses Russia of weaponizing global food supply chains.
• Maritime Security: Strongly supports UNCLOS and freedom of navigation. Opposes Russia's use of sea mines and blockades in the Black Sea.
• Free Trade: Advocates for open, secure trade lanes, free from military aggression.
• Sanctions: A leading advocate for comprehensive sanctions on Russian energy, shipping, and the shadow fleet.
• International Cooperation: Promotes cooperation via the UN, EU, NATO, and bilateral security agreements.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Security and safety of the Black Sea grain shipping routes.
• Russian missile attacks on port infrastructure in Odesa and Chornomorsk.
• Use of maritime naval drones for harbor and coastal defense.
• Security of the Danube River trade routes as alternative corridors.
• Drift mines in the Black Sea and international de-mining coordination.
• Enforcing sanctions against shipping companies facilitating Russian oil exports.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ World-leading producer and exporter of grain, sunflower oil, and steel.
✓ High levels of international support and military aid from Western states.
✓ Innovative use of asymmetric naval warfare (naval drones) for defense.
✓ Strategic Danube River ports, offering alternative logistics.
✓ Broad alignment with the EU Single Market and transport networks.
✓ Moral standing as a state defending its sovereignty under the UN Charter.
✓ High level of digital administration supporting logistics coordination.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Black Sea naval assets highly damaged or destroyed.
✗ Ports and shipping terminals subject to constant Russian air strikes.
✗ High economic dependency on foreign financial and military assistance.
✗ Black Sea routes remain high-risk, raising shipping insurance costs.
✗ Domestic labor shortage due to mobilization and displacement.
✗ Dependency on regional neighbors (Poland, Romania) for transit.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Ukraine's naval drone deployments are defensive actions against aggression.
• Ukrainian grain exports are vital for preventing global food crises.
• Sea mines were deployed defensively to prevent amphibious invasions.
• Russia's attacks on ports are deliberate attempts to damage global food chains.
• Ukraine coordinates with Black Sea neighbors (Turkey, Romania) to secure transit.
• Economic sanctions on Russia protect the rules-based international order.
• Ukraine supports a transparent, international monitoring framework for Black Sea ports.
• Territorial sovereignty over Crimea is non-negotiable under the UN Charter.

• Lifting of maritime sanctions on Russia without complete troop withdrawal.
• Any framework that fails to recognize Ukraine's sovereign waters.
