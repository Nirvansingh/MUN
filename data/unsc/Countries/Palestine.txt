NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing sovereign maritime rights off the coast of the Gaza Strip.
• Developing and accessing the Gaza Marine offshore natural gas field.
• Ending the Israeli blockade of Gaza to establish open trade portals.
• Rebuilding destroyed civilian infrastructure and supply chain terminals.
• Securing unhindered international humanitarian supply corridors (food, medicine).
• Opposing Israeli security controls over Palestinian imports and exports.
• Promoting international legal accountability for disruptions to civilian trade.
• Establishing sovereign port and shipping infrastructure in the Mediterranean.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Highlights the complete vulnerability of Palestinian supply chains to Israeli control. Calls for unhindered humanitarian access and sovereign control of borders.
• Maritime Security: Strongly opposes Israel's naval blockade of the Gaza Strip, framing it as a collective punishment and violation of international law.
• Free Trade: Advocates for the right of all peoples to engage in global trade free from military occupations.
• Sanctions: Rejects unilateral sanctions that exacerbate humanitarian crises or limit the flow of essential civilian goods.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via the UN, Arab League, OIC, and G77 to advocate for digital and trade rights.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Ownership and development rights of the Gaza Marine gas field.
• Impact of the Israeli maritime blockade on Gaza's economy and logistics.
• Security and operation of humanitarian corridors by land and sea.
• Destruction of civilian logistics infrastructure (ports, warehouses, roads).
• Palestinian representation and rights under the UNCLOS framework.
• Trade restrictions on dual-use items imported into Palestinian territories.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Broad international recognition in the UN General Assembly and G77.
✓ Strong moral arguments based on international humanitarian law and UNCLOS.
✓ Strategic support and aid from wealthy Arab League and OIC states.
✓ Diplomatic backing from permanent UNSC members (China, Russia).
✓ High youth literacy and active diaspora supporting digital reconstruction.
✓ Undersea gas resources (Gaza Marine) representing future economic sovereignty.
✓ Strong documentation of human rights violations by local CSOs.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Complete lack of physical control over territorial waters and borders.
✗ Division of administrative control between West Bank and Gaza.
✗ Severe dependency on foreign aid and Israeli-controlled tax transfers.
✗ Extreme destruction of local manufacturing and logistical capacity.
✗ No active navy or sovereign merchant shipping fleet.
✗ Inability to secure direct trade agreements due to occupied status.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• The maritime blockade of Gaza violates Article 33 of the Fourth Geneva Convention.
• Palestine has sovereign rights to natural resources within its maritime EEZ.
• Restrictions on import of civilian dual-use items represent collective punishment.
• Palestine coordinates with international agencies to ensure aid is distributed peacefully.
• Access to open shipping lanes is a prerequisite for sovereign economic survival.
• International support is directed toward peaceful, commercial port development.
• The Gaza Marine gas field belongs to the Palestinian people under international law.
• State building requires sovereign control over national maritime borders.
