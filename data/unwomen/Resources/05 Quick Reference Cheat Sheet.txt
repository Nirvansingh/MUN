==================================================
RED LINES FOR THE PHILIPPINES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
==================================================

1. NO NATIONALITY-BASED WAGE DISCRIMINATION: Any resolution clause permitting sub-minimum wages for foreign domestic workers will be vigorously opposed.
2. NO RECRUITMENT FEES LEVIED ON WORKERS: The "Employer-Pays Principle" must be absolute. Care workers must not begin employment in debt bondage.
3. UNCONDITIONAL PASSPORT RETENTION: Domestic workers must retain sole physical custody of their passports, identity documents, and mobile phones at all times.
4. PROHIBITION OF FORCED FAMILY SEPARATION: We reject long-term care worker visa regimes that systematically ban workers from accompanied family life.
5. NO EROSION OF ILO CONVENTION 189 STANDARDS: We will veto any compromise language that waters down statutory rest periods, minimum wage floors, or written contracts.

TOP 10 LAWS & LEGAL CITATIONS
==================================================

1. Article II, Section 14, 1987 Philippine Constitution: "The State recognizes the role of women in nation-building, and shall ensure the fundamental equality before the law of women and men."
2. Article XIII, Section 14, 1987 Philippine Constitution: Protects working women taking into account their maternal and economic functions.
3. Republic Act No. 10361 (Batas Kasambahay, 2013): Landmark Philippine domestic workers act mandating written contracts, minimum wage, rest periods, and SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG.
4. Republic Act No. 9710 (Magna Carta of Women, 2009): Mandatory 5% Gender and Development (GAD) budget allocation across all government agencies.
5. Republic Act No. 11210 (Expanded Maternity Leave Act, 2019): 105 days fully paid maternity leave with 7 days transferable to the father.
6. Republic Act No. 11641 (Department of Migrant Workers Act, 2021): Dedicated cabinet department governing diaspora protection and bilateral agreements.
7. ILO Convention No. 189 (Domestic Workers Convention, 2011): Global gold standard for domestic labor rights, ratified first in Asia by the Philippines.
8. ILO Convention No. 156 (Workers with Family Responsibilities Convention, 1981): Non-discrimination based on family care obligations.
9. CEDAW Article 11 & General Recommendation No. 17: Eliminating workplace discrimination and measuring the economic valuation of unpaid domestic work.
10. UN 2030 Agenda SDG Target 5.4: Universal mandate to recognize, reduce, and redistribute unpaid care and domestic work.

==================================================
TOP 10 RESOLUTION CLAUSE SNIPPETS
==================================================

1. The "Pasningsgaranti" Childcare Clause:
   "Calls upon all Member States to establish a statutory legal guarantee to public or publicly-subsidized early childhood education and care for all children from the end of statutory parental leave, capping out-of-pocket household expenditures at no more than 10% of net family earnings;"

2. The Father's Quota Clause:
   "Urges the implementation of individualized, non-transferable parental leave allocations reserved exclusively for fathers or second parents on a 'use-it-or-lose-it' basis, benchmarked at a minimum of 8 to 12 weeks with adequate income-replacement rates;"

3. Pension Care Credits Clause:
   "Recommends that Member States incorporate mandatory statutory Care Credits into national public pension calculations, ensuring that periods of unpaid childcare, eldercare, or disability care are credited as full contributory working years;"

4. Superannuation on Leave Clause:
   "Encourages Member States to mandate the payment of statutory retirement pension contributions and superannuation guarantees on all government-funded and employer-provided paid maternity and parental leave benefits;"

5. Abolition of Exit Visas and Tied Sponsorship Clause:
   "Demands that all destination countries eliminate exit-permit requirements and tied-employer sponsorship regulations for domestic and care workers, ensuring the universal right to change employers freely and retain sole custody of travel documents;"

6. Digital Wage Protection (WPS) Clause:
   "Mandates the universal adoption of auditable electronic wage payment systems for all domestic and household employees, monitored by national labor authorities to completely eradicate wage theft and delayed remuneration;"

7. "Triple Win" Ethical Recruitment Clause:
   "Establishes a UN Multilateral Framework for Ethical Care Migration, requiring recipient nations recruiting health and care personnel to co-invest directly in the educational institutions, faculty development, and healthcare infrastructure of origin countries;"

8. Frontline Health Worker Recognition Clause:
   "Recommends the formal legal recognition of community health and nutrition workers (such as ASHA and Anganwadi personnel) as statutory employees entitled to statutory minimum wage floors, occupational injury protections, and contributory social insurance;"

9. Time-Use Satellite Accounting Clause:
   "Requests the United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD) to assist all Member States in conducting standardized biennial National Time-Use Surveys to quantify unpaid care work and integrate satellite care accounts into official national GDP reporting;"

10. UN Global Care Infrastructure Fund Clause:
    "Decides to establish the UN Global Care Infrastructure Fund under the joint administration of UN Women and the World Bank, providing concessional financing to developing nations to construct universal community childcare and clean water infrastructure."

TOP 10 PHILIPPINE RAPID DEFENCES
==================================================

1. On Remittance Dependency: "Migration from the Philippines is an exercise of constitutional freedom of movement backed by the Department of Migrant Workers (RA 11641). Destination states must reform their restrictive sponsorship laws."
2. On Low Kasambahay Wages: "Batas Kasambahay (RA 10361) created an enforceable legal floor where none existed, with mandatory SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG contributions continuously raised by regional wage boards."
3. On Low Female Labor Participation: "The Philippines ranks #2 in East Asia on gender equality. We are tackling unpaid care through 105 days of paid maternity leave, universal barangay daycare, and mandatory 5% GAD gender budgeting."
4. On Nurse Migration ("Care Drain"): "The Philippines trains over 30,000 nurses annually. Destination economies must adopt our 'Triple Win' model and co-invest in our training institutions rather than poaching talent for free."
5. On SEnA Conciliation: "Our Single Entry Approach (SEnA) resolves over 90% of labor disputes within 30 days without expensive litigation."
6. On OFW Abuse Abroad: "Vulnerabilities abroad occur because host nations deny domestic workers labor code rights. The Philippines freezes deployment whenever a receiving state fails to guarantee worker safety."
7. On Barangay Daycare Hours: "Over 42,000 barangays have legally mandated ECCD centers under RA 10410, and municipal GAD budgets are expanding them into full-day facilities."
8. On Gender Parity Leadership: "The Philippines has achieved 100% parity in secondary and tertiary education, with women holding equal senior corporate and civil service management roles."
9. On ILO Convention 189: "The Philippines was the first nation in Asia to ratify ILO C189 in 2012, setting the standard for the entire continent."
10. On Constitutional Equality: "Article II, Section 14 of our 1987 Constitution enshrines the fundamental equality of women and men as an unalterable principle of state policy."
