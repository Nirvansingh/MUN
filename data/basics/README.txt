MUN RESEARCH WORKSPACE: README & PROJECT GUIDE
================================================================

This workspace contains a comprehensive research library and debate-ready resources for two distinct committees:

1. UNHRC (United Nations Human Rights Council)
   Agenda: Human Rights Implications of Artificial Intelligence, Mass Digital Surveillance, and Data Privacy.

2. UNSC (United Nations Security Council)
   Agenda: Impact of Ongoing Geopolitical Conflicts on Global Supply Chains, Maritime Security, and Free Trade.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE THESE RESOURCES IN COMMITTEE
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. DURING THE GENERAL SPEAKER'S LIST (GSL):
   - Refer to [Countries/] directories for your country's GSL outline.
   - Use the [Speeches/General GSL.txt] templates to quickly adapt your opening remarks to the flow of debate.

2. DURING MODERATED CAUCUSES:
   - Go to [Speeches/Moderated Caucus.txt] under your committee's folder for pre-written arguments, speaking notes, and statistics tailored to specific sub-issues (e.g., Red Sea corridors, facial recognition, or data localization).

3. DURING UNMODERATED CAUCUSES:
   - Use [Resolutions/Working Paper Clauses.txt] and [Solutions.txt] to guide your bloc's drafting process. Copy and adapt these pre-formatted clauses directly into your working papers.

4. DURING QUESTIONS & ANSWERS (Q&A):
   - Check the "LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY" in your country file to prepare defensive arguments.
   - Use the "QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK" section to challenge and lobby opposing blocs.
