ISLAMIC EMIRATE OF AFGHANISTAN

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Afghanistan is a landlocked Central-South Asian country under Taliban rule since 2021, facing severe humanitarian, economic, and human rights crises. Its strategic location has made it a focal point of great power competition for decades.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Kabul
Largest City: Kabul
Official Name: Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan (de facto); Islamic Republic of Afghanistan (constitutional)
Government Type: Unitary Islamic theocratic emirate (de facto)
Head of State: Supreme Leader Haibatullah Akhundzada
Head of Government: Prime Minister Mohammad Hassan Akhund (acting)
Official Languages: Pashto, Dari (Persian)
Population: ~40 million
Area: 652,230 km² (41st largest)
Currency: Afghan Afghani (AFN)
GDP (Nominal): ~$14.5 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$360

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Agriculture (opium poppy, wheat, fruits), carpets, textiles, small-scale manufacturing, mining (limited)
Major Exports: Opium (illicit), dried fruits, carpets, saffron, cotton, hides and skins, precious stones
Major Imports: Wheat, machinery, vehicles, petroleum products, pharmaceuticals, electronics, clothing
Major Trade Partners: Pakistan, Iran, China, UAE, India, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Taliban defence forces (estimated 170,000+ fighters). Limited air capability (captured US equipment largely inoperable). Ongoing counter-insurgency operations against ISIS-K. No conventional navy or air force.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). No nuclear program.
UN Membership: 1946
UNSC Status: Not on Council; Taliban government not officially recognized
Major International Organizations: UN (seat vacant — Taliban not recognized); OIC, SAARC, SCO (observer)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Limited internationally due to non-recognition; significant influence only within South and Central Asia via refugee flows and security concerns
Neighbouring Countries: Pakistan (south/east), Iran (west), Turkmenistan (northwest), Uzbekistan (north), Tajikistan (northeast), China (northeast — Wakhan Corridor)
Strategic Importance: Historic crossroads of Central and South Asia; key to regional connectivity projects; counter-terrorism focal point; significant mineral resources; water source for Central Asia
Important Maritime Routes: Landlocked — no coastline; relies on Pakistan (Karachi, Gwadar) and Iran (Chabahar) for trade routes
Important Geography: Hindu Kush mountain range, Khyber Pass, Helmand River, Amu Darya river, Wakhan Corridor, extensive deserts

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Limited — significant hydroelectric potential (unexploited); heavily dependent on energy imports from Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Iran
Natural Resources: Copper (Aynak — one of world's largest), iron ore, lithium, rare earth elements, gold, natural gas, petroleum, marble
Technology Sector: Minimal — basic mobile networks; limited internet; no significant tech industry
Major Companies: None significant — economy dominated by informal and illicit sectors

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: ~37% literacy (one of lowest globally); severe gender disparity — girls banned from secondary and higher education
Internet Penetration: ~18% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.462 (Low, ranked 180th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: Not ranked
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — independent media largely shut down; journalists arrested; women banned from media work
Human Rights Overview: Severe under Taliban — women and girls banned from education and public life; restrictions on freedom of movement, employment, and dress; minorities at risk (Hazaras, Sikhs); torture and extrajudicial killings reported

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: International recognition, sanctions relief, counter-ISIS-K, economic survival, maintaining internal control, managing refugee crisis
Foreign Policy Overview: Seeks international legitimacy without compromising ideological principles; balances relations with Pakistan (Taliban's historic patron), China, Iran, and Central Asian states
Relations With:
  USA: Hostile — no diplomatic relations; frozen assets; sanctions; counter-terrorism over-the-horizon operations
  China: Primary diplomatic and economic partner — Belt and Road; investment in mining (Aynak copper); recognition talks
  Russia: Cautious engagement — counter-terrorism concerns; ISIS-K threat; potential recognition discussions
  India: Strained — India supported former government; limited humanitarian engagement; security concerns over Pakistan and terrorism
  Pakistan: Complex — Taliban historic ally; sanctuary provided during insurgency; but border tensions (Durand Line) and refugee issues
  EU: Humanitarian aid only; no recognition; conditions on women's rights and counter-terrorism
  Middle East: Qatar hosts Taliban political office; UAE and Saudi Arabia cautious; Iran complex (shared border, water disputes, sectarian differences)
  Neighbouring Countries: Pakistan primary gateway; Iran economic link; Central Asian states concerned about extremism

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: None formally; Pakistan (primary supporter), Qatar (diplomatic facilitator)
Major Rivals: ISIS-K (internal), remnants of former government, anti-Taliban resistance (National Resistance Front)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: International recognition, economic survival, trade access, counter-ISIS-K, maintaining internal unity, water rights (Helmand River)
Current Major Challenges: Humanitarian crisis (famine); economic collapse; international isolation; women's rights international pressure; ISIS-K insurgency; refugee repatriation; drought and climate change; opium ban impact on rural economy

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Afghanistan is one of the world's largest producers of opium poppy (illicit).
• The Hindu Kush mountain passes have historically been invasion routes.
• Afghanistan has one of the youngest populations globally (~60% under 25).
• The Aynak copper mine is one of the world's largest untapped copper deposits.
