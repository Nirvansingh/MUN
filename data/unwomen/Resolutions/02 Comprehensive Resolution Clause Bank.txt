SECTION 1: PREAMBULATORY CLAUSE BANK
==================================================

• PP1 (Constitutional & UN Charter Authority):
  "Reaffirming the fundamental purposes and principles enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, particularly Article 55, which obligates all Member States to promote higher standards of living, full employment, and conditions of economic and social progress without distinction as to sex,"

• PP2 (UDHR Social Protection Authority):
  "Recalling Articles 22, 23, and 25 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, which establish the universal human right to social security, just and favorable conditions of work, equal remuneration for work of equal value, and special protection for motherhood and childhood,"

• PP3 (CEDAW & General Recommendations):
  "Reiterating the binding legal obligations under the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (CEDAW), particularly Article 11, General Recommendation No. 17 on the measurement and valuation of unremunerated domestic activities, and General Recommendation No. 26 on women migrant workers,"

• PP4 (Sustainable Development Goals / SDG 5.4):
  "Guided by the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, in particular Sustainable Development Goal Target 5.4 to recognize, reduce, and redistribute unpaid care and domestic work through the provision of public services, infrastructure, and social protection policies,"

• PP5 (ILO Conventions & Decent Work):
  "Acknowledging the core international conventions of the International Labour Organization (ILO), including Convention No. 189 (Domestic Workers Convention, 2011), Convention No. 156 (Workers with Family Responsibilities, 1981), and Convention No. 100 (Equal Remuneration, 1951),"

• PP6 (The 16.4 Billion Hours Macroeconomic Reality):
  "Conscious that women and girls globally perform over 76% of all unpaid care work, totaling 16.4 billion hours daily—the economic equivalent of 2 billion individuals working full-time without remuneration—representing an invisible annual subsidy of at least eleven trillion United States dollars ($11,000,000,000,000) to the global economy,"

• PP7 (Time Poverty & Labor Participation):
  "Deeply concerned that the unequal distribution of care responsibilities generates severe female time poverty, depresses global female labor force participation to approximately 47%, and restricts women's access to continuous education, formal employment, and public leadership,"

• PP8 (The Lifetime Gender Pension Deficit):
  "Alarmed that career interruptions and wage penalties associated with unpaid caregiving result in an alarming lifetime gender pension gap exceeding 30% to 38% in many Member States, leaving elderly women disproportionately vulnerable to extreme poverty,"

• PP9 (Global Care Chains & Migrant Care Vulnerabilities):
  "Recognizing the indispensable contribution of millions of transnational migrant domestic and healthcare workers who sustain family and healthcare infrastructures across destination societies, while expressing grave concern regarding persistent vulnerabilities under restrictive sponsorship regimes, passport confiscation, and lack of social security portability,"

• PP10 (Care as a Public Good):
  "Convinced that the care economy cannot be treated as an uncompensated private favor or a deregulated market commodity, but must be institutionalized as an essential public good and foundational macroeconomic pillar,"

SECTION 2: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — MACROECONOMICS & TIME USE
==================================================

• OP1 (Biennial National Time-Use Surveys):
  "Urges all Member States, in technical partnership with the United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD) and UN Women, to conduct institutionalized, publicly accessible National Time-Use Surveys at least once every three years to quantify the volume and demographic distribution of unpaid care and domestic labor;"

• OP2 (Satellite National Care Accounts):
  "Recommends that Member States incorporate Satellite Care Accounts into their national accounting frameworks, monetizing unpaid childcare, eldercare, and household services to establish their proportional contribution to Gross Domestic Product (GDP);"

• OP3 (Gender-Responsive Budgeting / 5% Benchmark):
  "Encourages Member States to adopt statutory Gender-Responsive Budgeting mechanisms, benchmarked at a minimum allocation of 5% of national and municipal agency budgets specifically dedicated to Gender and Development (GAD) and public care infrastructure (modeled after the Republic of the Philippines' Magna Carta of Women, Republic Act No. 9710);"

SECTION 3: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — CHILDCARE INFRASTRUCTURE
==================================================

• OP4 (Universal Public Childcare Guarantee / Pasningsgaranti):
  "Calls upon all Member States to establish a statutory 'Public Childcare Guarantee' (modeled after the Kingdom of Denmark's Pasningsgaranti), ensuring that every child is legally guaranteed a licensed, high-quality public or publicly subsidized early childhood education and care slot from the conclusion of statutory parental leave up to primary school entry age;"

• OP5 (Statutory 10% Household Fee Ceiling):
  "Recommends that national legislation establish progressive fee caps ensuring that out-of-pocket childcare expenditures do not exceed 10% of net household earnings, with completely free early childhood education guaranteed for lower-income families;"

• OP6 (Corporate Workplace Crèche Mandate):
  "Urges Member States to mandate that all commercial, industrial, and public-sector enterprises employing 50 or more workers establish on-site or consortium-based childcare facilities, or provide equivalent direct employer childcare subsidies to working parents, accompanied by progressive fines for non-compliance that exceed the cost of childcare facility maintenance;"

• OP7 (Early Childhood Educator Wage Dignity):
  "Requests Member States to establish professional certification standards and statutory wage parity for Early Childhood Educators (ECEs) with primary school teachers to resolve structural recruitment deficits and professionalize the childcare workforce;"

==================================================
SECTION 4: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — PARENTAL LEAVE & RETIREMENT
==================================================

• OP8 (Social Insurance Paid Maternity Leave):
  "Calls upon Member States to provide a minimum of 18 to 24 weeks of fully paid maternity leave at high income-replacement rates, funded through tripartite state social security pools rather than direct employer liability, completely eliminating female hiring disincentives in the private sector;"

• OP9 (Non-Transferable Father's Quota):
  "Strongly encourages the implementation of individualized, non-transferable parental leave allocations reserved exclusively for fathers or second parents on a 'use-it-or-lose-it' basis, benchmarked at a minimum of 8 to 12 weeks at high wage-replacement rates, to dismantle corporate motherhood penalties and promote shared parenting from infancy;"

• OP10 (Superannuation on Paid Parental Leave):
  "Urges all Member States to enact statutory 'Superannuation on Parental Leave' frameworks (modeled after the Commonwealth of Australia's historic 2025 reform), mandating that statutory retirement superannuation and pension contributions be paid on all government-funded and employer-provided paid maternity and parental leave benefits;"

• OP11 (Statutory Pension Care Credits):
  "Recommends that Member States incorporate statutory 'Care Pension Credits' into national public pension systems, guaranteeing that periods of full-time unpaid caregiving for children under age 6, elderly dependents, or persons with disabilities are credited as full contributory working years toward retirement pension calculations;"

---------------------------------------------------------------

==================================================
SECTION 5: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — MULTI-GENERATIONAL & ELDERCARE
==================================================

• OP12 (Grandparent Childcare Allowances):
  "Commends innovative multi-generational family support policies, specifically Hungary's Grandparent Childcare Allowance (Nagyszülői GYED), and encourages Member States to establish statutory frameworks extending state-funded childcare allowances, tax relief, and pension credits to grandparents and extended family members providing daily in-home care for young children;"

• OP13 (Public Long-Term Care Insurance):
  "Urges Member States facing rapid demographic aging to establish universal, socialized Long-Term Care Insurance systems (modeled on the frameworks of Japan and the Federal Republic of Germany), providing universal home-nursing assistance and family respite allowances to eliminate unassisted eldercare resignations (Kaigo Risyoku);"

---------------------------------------------------------------

==================================================
SECTION 6: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — DOMESTIC WORKERS & ILO C189
==================================================

• OP14 (Universal Ratification of ILO Convention 189):
  "Urges all Member States to accelerate the ratification and statutory domestic codification of ILO Convention No. 189 (Domestic Workers Convention, 2011), ensuring that in-home domestic workers enjoy equal rights under general national labor codes, including statutory minimum wage floors and overtime compensation;"

• OP15 (Standard Written Contracts & Rest Periods):
  "Mandates that all domestic employment relationships be governed by standard written employment contracts in understandable languages, guaranteeing at least 8 consecutive hours of daily rest, a minimum of 24 consecutive hours of weekly rest, and statutory annual paid vacation;"

• OP16 (Fast-Track SEnA Labor Conciliation):
  "Recommends the institutionalization of fast-track administrative conciliation mechanisms for domestic labor grievances (modeled after the Republic of the Philippines' Single Entry Approach / SEnA), mandating that labor disputes involving domestic staff be mediated and resolved within a strict 30-day timeline without filing fees;"

• OP17 (Inclusion in Workplace Injury Compensation):
  "Calls upon Member States to include domestic and household workers in statutory national social insurance schemes covering occupational injuries, diseases, and workplace mortality (modeled on the Republic of South Africa's Mahlangu precedent and the Philippine Batas Kasambahay SSS coverage);"

---------------------------------------------------------------

==================================================
SECTION 7: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — MIGRANT CARE WORKERS & ETHICAL MOBILITY
==================================================

• OP18 (Employer-Pays Principle / Zero Placement Fees):
  "Reaffirms the absolute necessity of enforcing the 'Employer-Pays Principle,' strictly prohibiting recruitment agencies, employers, and intermediaries from charging recruitment fees, placement fees, or debt obligations directly or indirectly to domestic and care workers;"

• OP19 (Universal Digital Wages Protection Systems / WPS):
  "Recommends that destination countries implement auditable electronic Wages Protection Systems for all domestic employees (modeled on the United Arab Emirates WPS framework), requiring mandatory electronic salary deposits to verified bank or payroll accounts to completely eradicate wage theft and arbitrary deductions;"

• OP20 (Unconditional Passport Retention):
  "Affirms that domestic and care workers must retain sole, unrestricted physical custody of their passports, travel identity documents, and personal mobile communication devices at all times, urging Member States to establish mandatory criminal penalties for employer document confiscation;"

• OP21 (Abolition of Tied Sponsorship & Exit Visas):
  "Calls upon all destination countries to eliminate exit-permit requirements ('khuruj') and tied-employer sponsorship restrictions for domestic workers, guaranteeing the universal right to change employers freely in cases of labor contract violation and exit the host territory without employer permission;"

• OP22 ('Triple Win' Ethical Healthcare Recruitment):
  "Adopts the 'Triple Win Framework for Ethical Care Migration' (modeled on the Federal Republic of Germany-Republic of the Philippines Bilateral Agreement), requiring recipient Member States recruiting international healthcare personnel to co-invest directly in the training academies, medical universities, and hospital infrastructure of origin nations;"

• OP23 (Prohibition of Forced Family Separation):
  "Strongly condemns immigration regulations that systematically prohibit foreign care workers from accompanied family life, urging destination states to provide accessible legal pathways for family reunification and permanent residency for long-term care personnel (modeled on Canada's Caregiver Pilots);"

---------------------------------------------------------------

==================================================
SECTION 8: OPERATIVE CLAUSES — FRONTLINE WORKERS & UN INFRASTRUCTURE FUND
==================================================

• OP24 (Formal Recognition of Community Health Workers):
  "Urges Member States to transition frontline female community healthcare and nutrition providers (such as India's ASHA and Anganwadi personnel) from 'honorary volunteers' to formal statutory employees entitled to statutory minimum wages, workplace injury compensation, and contributory pensions;"

• OP25 (Establishment of the UN Global Care Infrastructure Fund):
  "Decides to establish the UN Global Care Infrastructure Fund (UNGCIF) under the joint management of UN Women, the World Bank, and regional development institutions, dedicated to mobilizing concessional financing and development grants for low- and middle-income countries to construct municipal early childhood centers, expand rural piped water and clean energy grids, and develop digital informal worker social security portals;"

• OP26 (Annual Progress Reporting):
  "Requests the Executive Director of UN Women, in consultation with the Director-General of the International Labour Organization, to submit an annual progress report to the Commission on the Status of Women and the UN General Assembly on the implementation of this resolution and the global advancement of the care economy."

SECTION 9: STRATEGIC COMPROMISE CLAUSES FOR RESISTANT DELEGATIONS
==================================================

• Compromise Clause for USA / Free-Market States (Corporate Tax Incentives):
  "Recognizes the valuable contribution of private-sector innovation and employer-sponsored care initiatives, encouraging Member States to provide progressive corporate tax credits and public-private partnership (PPP) co-financing for enterprises that establish licensed daycare centers or flexible remote-work options for caregivers;"

• Compromise Clause for Gulf / GCC States (Sovereign Phased Modernization):
  "Acknowledges the sovereign jurisdiction of Member States in modernizing domestic labor governance, commending regional initiatives that introduce electronic wage systems, regulated recruitment centers, and standard digital contracts in progressive alignment with international standards;"

• Compromise Clause for Developing / Debt-Distressed States (Debt-for-Care Swaps):
  "Encourages international financial institutions and bilateral creditors to explore 'Debt-for-Care Infrastructure Swaps,' allowing developing Member States to convert external debt service payments into verifiable domestic investments in rural early childhood centers and clean water infrastructure."
