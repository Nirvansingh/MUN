================================================================
TÜRKIYE
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: Republic of Türkiye
Capital: Ankara
Government: Unitary Presidential Constitutional Republic
Current Leader: President Recep Tayyip Erdogan
Population: ~85 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent Member)
Major International Organizations: UN, NATO, WTO, BSEC, OIC, ECO, G20

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• Ukraine (Defense Partner)
• Qatar
• Pakistan
• Azerbaijan
• Central Asian States
• Saudi Arabia
• United Arab Emirates
• Northern Cyprus

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• Greece (Maritime Boundary Disputes)
• Russia (Complex Partnership/Geopolitical Rivalry)
• Syria (Border Security Friction)

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Enforcing the Montreux Convention strictly to regulate transit through the Turkish Straits.
• Promoting the Middle Corridor (Trans-Caspian East-West-Middle Corridor) trade route.
• Protecting Turkey's maritime rights in the Aegean and Eastern Mediterranean ("Blue Homeland").
• Mediating regional conflicts (e.g., Black Sea grain corridor negotiations).
• Securing and diversifying energy imports (natural gas pipelines, nuclear projects).
• Expanding domestic defense industry exports, including unmanned systems (UAVs) and warships.
• Supporting economic and transport connectivity with Central Asian Republics.
• Countering terrorism along national borders to protect transit stability.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Focuses on positioning Turkey as a central logistics hub linking Asia and Europe. Promotes the Middle Corridor to bypass Russian and Iranian routes.
• Maritime Security: Strictly implements the Montreux Convention, regulating Black Sea transit. Opposes external naval deployments in the Black Sea.
• Free Trade: Supports free trade, maintaining a customs union with the European Union while seeking trade diversification.
• Sanctions: Does not enforce unilateral Western sanctions on Russia, maintaining energy and commercial trade.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via BSEC, OIC, ECO, NATO, and UN channels.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Enforcement of the Montreux Convention during the Ukraine conflict.
• Revival of Black Sea grain corridor mechanisms.
• Eastern Mediterranean drilling activities and maritime border disputes with Greece.
• Development of the Middle Corridor transit infrastructure.
• Export of Turkish defense technologies (Bayraktar UAVs) to secure partner trade lanes.
• Impact of Red Sea shipping shifts on Turkish port logistics.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Sovereign control over the Turkish Straits (Bosporus, Dardanelles) under Montreux.
✓ Strategic geopolitical bridge between Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.
✓ Powerful military and modern, expanding navy (Milgem Project).
✓ High-value defense exporter, producing tactical drones used in regional conflicts.
✓ Active mediator role, trusted by both Ukraine and Russia.
✓ Custom Union agreement with the EU, securing trade access.
✓ Diversified energy transit hub (host to major gas pipelines).

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Domestic inflation and currency volatility affecting trade costs.
✗ Dependency on imported energy and raw materials.
✗ Border conflicts with Syria and Iraq, straining national resources.
✗ Ongoing maritime boundary disputes with Greece, causing regional tension.
✗ Geopolitical balancing act between NATO commitments and Russia relations.
✗ Logistics routes vulnerable to regional escalation in the Middle East.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Turkish Straits regulations under the Montreux Convention are non-negotiable.
• The Blue Homeland doctrine protects legitimate maritime rights under international law.
• Turkey's mediation is vital for securing global grain supply chains.
• Turkey opposes unilateral sanctions that disrupt global trade and food security.
• Transit corridor investments are commercial agreements that benefit global trade.
• Naval deployments in the Aegean are defensive and protect national sovereignty.
• Turkey supports the territorial integrity of Ukraine.
• Economic relations with Russia comply with Turkey's national interests and international law.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does Turkey reconcile NATO membership with refusal to enforce sanctions on Russia?
Q2. How do Turkish drilling operations in the Eastern Mediterranean align with UNCLOS?
Q3. What measures protect Black Sea commercial transit from drifting sea mines?
Q4. How does the Blue Homeland doctrine affect maritime safety in the Aegean Sea?
Q5. How does Turkey prevent sanctioned goods from being transshipped through its territory?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Greece: Will Greece engage in bilateral dialogue to resolve Aegean boundaries without external intervention?
• Russia: How can Russia support the revitalization of the Black Sea grain corridor?
• Ukraine: What steps is Ukraine taking to clear its drifting sea mines in the Black Sea?
• USA: How do unilateral trade sanctions affect economic stability in the Middle East?
• Egypt: How can we coordinate to secure Eastern Mediterranean shipping safety?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Any amendments or challenges to the Montreux Convention.
• UN resolutions that invalidate Turkey's Eastern Mediterranean maritime claims.
• Internationalization of Turkish port security audits.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: Ukraine, Qatar, Azerbaijan, Pakistan, OIC, Central Asian states.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Russia, US (maintain balanced diplomatic dialogue).
• Possible compromises: Support UN-led Black Sea shipping safety monitors.
• Main objectives: Enforce Montreux, promote Middle Corridor, protect maritime boundaries.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high assertiveness and pride in Turkey's strategic mediator role.
✓ Defend the Montreux Convention strictly, framing it as the key to Black Sea stability.
✓ Emphasize Turkey's role in the Black Sea Grain Initiative, which fed millions.
✓ Use terms like "regional ownership," "Middle Corridor connectivity," and "constructive dialogue."
✓ Deflect Greek criticisms by calling for direct, bilateral negotiations.
