IRAN
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: Islamic Republic of Iran
Capital: Tehran
Government: Islamic Theocratic Republic
Current Leader: Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei
Population: ~89 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent Voice)
Major International Organizations: UN, BRICS, SCO, OIC, ECO, OPEC

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Controlling and securing the Strait of Hormuz to protect sovereign waters.
• Countering US and Western naval deployments in the Persian Gulf.
• Supporting the Axis of Resistance to counter Western influence in the Middle East.
• Bypassing Western unilateral sanctions to export oil and petrochemicals.
• Developing the International North-South Transport Corridor (INSTC) with Russia and India.
• Expanding domestic missile and unmanned systems technology.
• Securing agricultural and food import corridors from regional partners.
• Protecting critical energy infrastructure from foreign cyber sabotage.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Promotes the INSTC as a secure corridor bypassing Western-controlled shipping lanes. Opposes Western supply chain blocks.
• Maritime Security: Asserts sovereign control over the Strait of Hormuz. Rejects Western-led maritime coalitions as escalatory and illegal.
• Free Trade: Advocates for trade among non-Western states, utilizing local currencies to bypass dollar dominance.
• Sanctions: Declares all Western unilateral economic sanctions as economic warfare and illegal.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via BRICS, SCO, OIC, and bilateral agreements to build secure trade routes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Security of the Strait of Hormuz and encounters with US Navy vessels.
• Yemen conflict, Houthi attacks in the Red Sea, and allegations of Iranian support.
• Development of the INSTC to connect Russian and Indian markets.
• Shadow fleet operations and oil export routes to Asian markets.
• Cyber security of national energy grids and petrochemical terminals.
• Impact of Western sanctions on Iranian civilian medicine imports.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Sovereign control over the Strait of Hormuz (choke point for 20% of global oil).
✓ Highly developed asymmetric naval warfare capabilities (fast attack boats, mines).
✓ Strategic partnerships with Russia and China.
✓ Central node of the INSTC, linking Russia and India.
✓ Strong domestic missile and UAV manufacturing base.
✓ Centralized control over national maritime ports.
✓ Active influence over regional political and security actors.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Extreme economic damage caused by long-term Western sanctions.
✗ High domestic inflation and currency depreciation.
✗ Infrastructure bottlenecks in domestic shipping and rail networks.
✗ Vulnerability of energy infrastructure to cyber attacks (e.g., terminal disruptions).
✗ Limited access to advanced Western technology and port hardware.
✗ High dependence on oil revenues for national budgets.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Iran guarantees the safety of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.
• US military presence in the Persian Gulf is the primary source of regional instability.
• Western sanctions are illegal, violating WTO rules and human rights.
• Support for regional allies is political; actions are independent.
• The INSTC promotes sovereign connectivity for developing nations.
• Maritime defense measures protect territorial waters from foreign espionage.
• Iran is a victim of state-sponsored cyber sabotage targeting civilian infrastructure.
• De-dollarization of trade is necessary to protect national sovereignty.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does Iran justify the export of drones and missiles to groups targeting Red Sea shipping?
Q2. What measures protect commercial ships from arbitrary seizure in the Strait of Hormuz?
Q3. How does Iran respond to allegations of facilitating illicit oil transshipments?
Q4. How do Iran's naval drills in the Persian Gulf align with international maritime safety?
Q5. What checks prevent Iranian cyber units from targeting foreign maritime databases?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• USA: How does the US justify military interventions that destabilize regional trade?
• Israel: Will Israel cease attacks on commercial tankers in the Mediterranean?
• Saudi Arabia: How can we coordinate to secure regional shipping without foreign intervention?
• China: How can we accelerate INSTC transport routes to secure trade corridors?
• Russia: What steps are being taken to integrate our banking networks to bypass SWIFT?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Resolutions that authorize international inspections of Iranian flag vessels.
• Any UN mandate that restricts Iran's sovereign authority in the Strait of Hormuz.
• Restrictions on technology exports or military development.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: Russia, China, Syria, BRICS members, SCO states.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Saudi Arabia (maintain diplomatic engagement under the Beijing agreement).
• Possible compromises: Support UN-led maritime safety standards for merchant vessels.
• Main objectives: Oppose Western sanctions, promote INSTC, reject US naval presence.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high defiance and national pride, calling out Western double standards.
✓ Frame Iran as a defender of regional sovereignty against US intervention.
✓ Emphasize the illegality of Western economic sanctions.
✓ Promote the INSTC as a peaceful trade corridor for global development.
✓ Coordinate arguments closely with Russia and China.
