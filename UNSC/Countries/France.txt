FRANCE
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: French Republic
Capital: Paris
Government: Semi-Presidential Constitutional Republic
Current Leader: President Emmanuel Macron
Population: ~68 Million
UNSC Status: Permanent Member (P5)
Major International Organizations: UN, EU, NATO, G7, WTO, IMO, OECD, La Francophonie

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing maritime trade routes linking Europe to the Indo-Pacific.
• Protecting French sovereign waters, including the world's second-largest Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ).
• Maintaining the safety of shipping lanes through the Red Sea and Bab-el-Mandeb.
• Opposing coercive trade practices and protecting European technological sovereignty.
• Enforcing international law and UNCLOS across all maritime zones.
• Strengthening European strategic autonomy in logistics and key industries.
• Protecting undersea critical infrastructure (cables and pipelines) in European waters.
• Supporting the security of the Black Sea and the Danube trade routes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Promotes European strategic autonomy. Advocates for diversifying supply chains, near-shoring, and reducing dependency on strategic adversaries.
• Maritime Security: Strongly supports UNCLOS. Deploys naval assets independently or within European frameworks (like Operation Aspides) to protect shipping.
• Free Trade: Supports fair and open trade within the EU framework. Opposes protectionism but advocates for strict enforcement of trade reciprocity.
• Sanctions: Supports EU-wide sanctions against Russia, Iran, and entities disrupting maritime trade. Focuses on enforcing shipping transparency.
• International Cooperation: Prefers multilateral, UN-led, or European-led security initiatives to maintain global trade stability.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Deployment of Operation Aspides in the Red Sea and French naval engagement.
• Defense of undersea cables and energy pipelines in the Mediterranean and Atlantic.
• Enforcement of sanctions against the Russian shadow fleet in European ports.
• European strategic autonomy and supply chain resilience for critical components.
• Maritime security in the Indo-Pacific, focusing on France's territories.
• Cyber security for port management and supply chain databases.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Second-largest Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) globally, providing massive maritime leverage.
✓ Advanced blue-water navy (Marine Nationale) with a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier.
✓ Permanent member of the UNSC and leading voice in the European Union.
✓ Leading role in European maritime security operations (e.g., Operation Aspides).
✓ Strong domestic industries in aerospace, defense, and high-value logistics.
✓ Strategic territories globally (Indo-Pacific, Caribbean), offering naval bases.
✓ Highly developed cybersecurity defense systems coordinated by ANSSI.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Dependency on imports for energy, raw materials, and electronics.
✗ Navy is highly capable but stretched thin across multiple global territories.
✗ Economic challenges and budget constraints limiting defense hardware expansion.
✗ Susceptibility of domestic industries to energy transit cost shocks.
✗ Domestic protests and political alignment challenges regarding trade policies.
✗ Heavy reliance on the Suez Canal route for trade with Asia.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Operation Aspides is a defensive, non-escalatory operation protecting civilian ships.
• France's actions comply with UNCLOS and international maritime law.
• Strategic autonomy is not protectionism; it secures vital supply chain links.
• French naval deployments protect freedom of navigation for all states.
• Sanctions are necessary tools to defend sovereignty and international law.
• Critical maritime infrastructure requires coordinated European protection.
• France seeks a balanced, diplomatic resolution to regional maritime disputes.
• Trade policies must protect environmental and labor standards globally.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does France reconcile its call for European autonomy with its dependency on NATO/US security?
Q2. What measures is France taking to enforce sanctions against the shadow fleet in its waters?
Q3. How does France justify the defensive limits of Operation Aspides compared to Operation Prosperity Guardian?
Q4. Does France's support for agricultural tariffs within the EU undermine global free trade?
Q5. How does France protect its vast EEZ from illegal fishing and security breaches?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Russia: Will Russia support an independent investigation into Nord Stream infrastructure sabotage?
• China: How does China ensure its South China Sea claims do not impede global free trade?
• Iran: What steps is Iran taking to de-escalate maritime tensions in the Strait of Hormuz?
• India: How can France and India deepen technology cooperation to secure Indo-Pacific lanes?
• Egypt: What measures is Egypt taking to coordinate with European security operations?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Any restrictions on French naval deployments in its global EEZ.
• Resolutions that dilute European Union trade decision-making powers.
• Any framework that fails to recognize UNCLOS as the core of maritime law.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: Germany, Italy, EU members, US, UK, Japan, India.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: China (seek cooperation on climate and trade rules first).
• Possible compromises: Support technical assistance for developing states' port security.
• Main objectives: Promote European-led maritime initiatives, defend UNCLOS, secure supply routes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high diplomatic poise, balancing French national interests with European unity.
✓ Highlight France's unique position as both a European and an Indo-Pacific power.
✓ Focus on terms like "strategic autonomy," "rules-based order," and "multilateral consensus."
✓ Differentiate European-led initiatives (like Aspides) from other coalitions when appropriate.
✓ Reference France's vast maritime territory and responsibilities in global waters.
