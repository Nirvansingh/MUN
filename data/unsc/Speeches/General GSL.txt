TEMPLATE 1: P5 & WESTERN MARITIME ALLIANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] addresses this council to confront an immediate threat to international peace and security: the systemic targeting of commercial shipping in global transit corridors. From the Red Sea to the Black Sea, civilian seafarers and merchant vessels are subjected to lawless attacks and blockades. 

These actions violate the fundamental principles of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS). They disrupt vital supply chains, raise transportation costs, and fuel global inflation. 

[Country Name] calls upon this council to take decisive, united action. We must condemn maritime terrorism, support defensive coalitions that protect transit corridors, and enforce sanctions against those state actors who fund and arm disruptive proxy forces. Freedom of navigation is a universal right that this council is mandated to protect. Thank you."

TEMPLATE 2: SOVEREIGN & EURASIAN BLOC
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] notes that while some member states claim to defend free trade, their own policies are the primary source of global supply chain disruption. Unilateral, illegal economic sanctions and technology blocks fragment international markets, violating WTO principles and the UN Charter.

Furthermore, we reject attempts to use the Security Council to validate foreign naval deployments in regions where they only serve to escalate tensions. Maritime security must respect the sovereign territorial boundaries and exclusive economic zones of coastal states. 

We advocate for regional cooperative security frameworks and the development of secure, non-Western logistics corridors, such as the [mention Northern Sea Route or Middle Corridor]. True trade security requires the ending of unilateral economic warfare. Thank you."

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 3: TRANSIT & DEVELOPING NATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] represents a nation whose economy is directly exposed to maritime transit disruptions. For developing countries, the closure of shipping routes or spike in transport insurance rates translates directly into food insecurity, fertilizer shortages, and energy inflation.

We do not seek military escalation or naval confrontations in our regional waters. What we require is immediate, non-military diplomatic mediation to secure trade corridors. We call on this council to coordinate funding to assist developing states in modernizing port cybersecurity and safeguarding shipping lanes.

Let us prioritize regional stability, open commerce, and humanitarian grain corridors. Thank you."
