================================================================
EGYPT
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: Arab Republic of Egypt
Capital: Cairo
Government: Unitary Semi-Presidential Republic
Current Leader: President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi
Population: ~110 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent Member)
Major International Organizations: UN, Arab League, African Union, OIC, G77, BRICS, WTO, IMO

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• Saudi Arabia
• United Arab Emirates
• United States
• France
• Germany
• Jordan
• Greece
• China (Strategic Partner)
• Russia (Strategic Partner)

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• Turkey (Complex Diplomacy/Regional Rivalry)
• Ethiopia (Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam Dispute)

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Protecting Suez Canal transit fees, which generate 10% of state revenue.
• Securing Red Sea shipping lanes from Houthi missile and drone disruptions.
• Developing the Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZONE) as a global logistics hub.
• Maintaining national food security as the world's largest wheat importer.
• Securing maritime energy transit corridors in the Eastern Mediterranean.
• Protecting critical undersea internet cables routed through the Suez land bridge.
• Fighting maritime piracy and arms smuggling in the Red Sea.
• Stabilizing regional conflicts that impact transit revenues and trade corridors.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Emphasizes the central role of the Suez Canal in global supply chains. Prioritizes food security and wheat imports.
• Maritime Security: Supports freedom of navigation and UNCLOS. Actively patrols the Red Sea but avoids offensive coalitions to prevent regional escalation.
• Free Trade: Promotes open maritime trade and investment in the SCZONE.
• Sanctions: Opposes unilateral sanctions that impact transit revenues or food imports.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via the Arab League, African Union, OIC, BRICS, and UN.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Loss of Suez Canal transit revenues due to Houthi attacks in the Red Sea.
• Food security and wheat supply chain shocks from the Ukraine conflict.
• Development of the Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZONE).
• Undersea cable security on the Egyptian land bridge.
• Maritime boundary delimitation and gas exploration in the Eastern Mediterranean.
• Border security and transit stability near Libya and Gaza.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Sovereign control over the Suez Canal (corridor for 12% of global trade).
✓ Powerful military and modern navy (largest in Africa/Middle East).
✓ Strategic bridge between Africa, Asia, and European trade networks.
✓ Active member of BRICS and Arab League, offering high diplomatic leverage.
✓ Host to the Suez Canal Economic Zone, attracting global manufacturing.
✓ Major recipient of security aid from the US and Gulf states.
✓ Close partnership with both Western powers and Russia/China.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Extreme dependency on imported wheat for domestic bread subsidies.
✗ High dependency of national budget on Suez Canal transit fees.
✗ Severe domestic currency and fiscal challenges.
✗ Stretched military resources due to border security threats.
✗ Undersea cables are concentrated in a single land bridge, raising vulnerability.
✗ Exposure to regional escalations in Gaza, Yemen, and Libya.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Suez Canal operations comply strictly with the Constantinople Convention.
• Egypt acts as a responsible guardian of global maritime trade.
• Food security requires international protection of grain corridors.
• Egypt opposes unilateral sanctions that disrupt global trade and food supply.
• Port and SCZONE security measures comply with international standards.
• Undersea cable security is a vital national interest.
• Egypt seeks peaceful resolutions to regional maritime disputes.
• The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam dispute requires a legally binding agreement.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How is Egypt mitigating the economic loss of Suez Canal revenues without escalating conflicts?
Q2. What measures protect Suez Canal database networks from cyber sabotage?
Q3. How does Egypt address the environmental impact of Suez Canal expansions?
Q4. Does Egypt's Eastern Mediterranean maritime agreement with Greece escalate regional tensions?
Q5. What checks prevent arms smuggling through the Sinai and Gaza border?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Yemen (Houthi Authorities): How do your attacks on civilian shipping lanes serve any regional peace?
• USA: How can the US assist Egypt in securing Red Sea maritime lanes from threats?
• Russia: Will Russia guarantee stable wheat exports to Egypt at fair prices?
• China: How can China expand investments in the SCZONE to boost trade?
• Ukraine: How can we secure stable Black Sea grain transit lanes?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Any challenge to Egypt's sovereignty over the Suez Canal.
• Unilateral military interventions in the Red Sea.
• Restrictions on grain and food imports.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Gulf states, United States, France, G77 states.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Turkey (maintain diplomatic engagement).
• Possible compromises: Support international funding for Suez Canal safety upgrades.
• Main objectives: Secure Suez Canal transit, protect grain imports, resist unilateral sanctions.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high authority, highlighting Egypt's control over the Suez Canal.
✓ Frame Egypt's transit revenues as a global asset that must be protected.
✓ Focus on terms like "Constantinople Convention," "Suez Canal Economic Zone," and "food security."
✓ Highlight Egypt's naval contributions to Red Sea safety.
✓ Coordinate arguments closely with Saudi Arabia and UAE.
