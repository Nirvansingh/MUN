This document details four debate-ready solutions for resolutions addressing maritime safety and global supply chains.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 1: UN MARITIME SECURITY COORDINATION MECHANISM
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Establishing a centralized, UN-coordinated system to bridge communications between different naval coalitions (e.g., Operation Prosperity Guardian, Operation Aspides, and non-Western patrols) and commercial shipping.
• Key Elements:
  - Real-time transit tracking and threat alerts.
  - Joint protocols for responding to distress signals in conflict zones.
  - De-confliction zones near major choke points.
• Bloc Support: Supported by transit states and Western allies.
• Opposed by: States concerned with foreign intelligence access to shipping databases.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 2: SHADOW FLEET SAFETY MORATORIUM
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Requiring all vessels carrying hazardous cargo (such as crude oil) through international straits to verify P&I insurance and compliance with IMO structural safety rules (double-hull requirements).
• Key Elements:
  - Coastal state inspections of non-compliant vessels at strait entrances.
  - Centralized UN registry of verified, insured tankers.
  - Establishing environmental response teams in high-risk zones.
• Bloc Support: Sanctions-enforcing Western states.
• Opposed by: Russia and states operating shadow fleet logistics.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 3: UNDERSEA INFRASTRUCTURE PROTECTION PROTOCOLS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Creating a joint monitoring framework to protect undersea fiber-optic communication cables and gas pipelines in international waters.
• Key Elements:
  - Sound sensor arrays and satellite tracking of suspicious vessels lingering over cables.
  - Rapid-repair agreements to deploy technicians during sabotage events.
  - Sovereign control over maintenance remains within EEZs.
• Bloc Support: Broad consensus, though states reject sharing sensitive naval operations data.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 4: TRADE CORRIDOR DIVERSIFICATION AND CAPACITY BUILDING
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Supporting the development of alternative transport corridors (such as the Middle Corridor or North-South Corridor) to de-risk global trade from single choke point bottlenecks.
• Key Elements:
  - Financial support from the World Bank to modernize rail networks in Central Asia.
  - Simplification of border customs protocols through digital registries.
  - Strengthening port efficiency in developing transit states.
• Bloc Support: Supported by Global South, landlocked Central Asian states, and China.
