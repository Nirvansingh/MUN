----------------------------------------------------------------
1. BIOMETRIC SURVEILLANCE & FACIAL RECOGNITION
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Error Rates by Demographic: Research by NIST (2019) verified that some facial recognition algorithms have error rates up to 100 times higher for Asian and African American faces compared to Caucasian faces.
• Global Deployment: Over 70% of states globally are deploying facial recognition technology in police and border networks.
• Smart Cities: The global smart-city market is projected to deploy over 1 billion CCTV cameras globally by the end of 2026.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA HARVESTING & PRIVACY BREACHES
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Cost of Data Breaches: The average cost of a commercial data breach exceeded $4.4 million in 2023, driven by ransomware and cloud database leaks.
• Personal Profiles: Data brokers hold profiles containing over 3,000 data points on a single average internet user in developed economies.
• Encryption Protection: Over 80% of global internet traffic is now encrypted, though states are lobbying for law enforcement access.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. DIGITAL DIVIDE & INTERNET ACCESS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Unconnected Population: Approximately 33% of the world's population (~2.6 billion people) remains completely offline, mostly in the Global South.
• Mobile Penetration: While mobile phone penetration is high, only 35% of low-income countries have reliable access to high-speed broadband.
• Tech Concentration: Over 85% of global AI research and patents are held by the United States and China, raising concerns of technological neo-colonialism.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. CYBERSECURITY & THREATS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Infrastructure Attacks: Cyber attacks on public utility grids and healthcare databases rose by 150% between 2020 and 2023.
• State Espionage: Commercial spyware (like Pegasus) has been detected in over 45 states globally, targeting journalists and civil rights defenders.
