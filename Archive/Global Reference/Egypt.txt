ARAB REPUBLIC OF EGYPT

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Egypt is the most populous Arab country and a major regional power in North Africa and the Middle East. It controls the Suez Canal, a critical global trade chokepoint, and plays a central role in Arab, African, and Islamic diplomacy.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Cairo
Largest City: Cairo
Official Name: Arab Republic of Egypt
Government Type: Unitary semi-presidential republic (authoritarian)
Head of State: President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi
Head of Government: Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly
Official Languages: Arabic
Population: ~114 million
Area: 1,002,450 km² (29th largest)
Currency: Egyptian Pound (EGP)
GDP (Nominal): ~$450 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$3,900

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Tourism, agriculture, textiles, chemicals, petroleum refining, construction, manufacturing, Suez Canal revenues
Major Exports: Crude oil, refined petroleum, fertilizers, gold, textiles, fruits and vegetables, chemicals
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, wheat, iron and steel, pharmaceuticals, electronics, plastics, chemicals
Major Trade Partners: UAE, China, USA, Saudi Arabia, Italy, Turkey, Germany, India

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Largest military in Africa and Arab world (~440,000 active personnel). Highly capable air force and navy. Extensive US military aid (~$1.3 billion annually). Counter-terrorism operations in Sinai.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). Developing civilian nuclear program (El Dabaa plant with Russia).
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: Non-permanent member (most recently 2016-17)
Major International Organizations: UN, Arab League (headquarters), OIC, African Union, NAM, WTO, IMF, World Bank

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Leading Arab power; influential in African Union and Arab League; mediates between Israel and Palestinians; key US ally in Middle East
Neighbouring Countries: Sudan (south), Libya (west), Israel (northeast), Gaza Strip (northeast)
Strategic Importance: Controls Suez Canal (~12% of global trade transits); pivotal in Arab-Israeli peace; largest Arab military; moderating voice in Middle East
Important Maritime Routes: Suez Canal (critical chokepoint), Mediterranean Sea, Red Sea, Gulf of Aqaba
Important Geography: Nile River and Delta (lifeline of Egypt), Sahara Desert (96% of land), Sinai Peninsula, Suez Canal, Red Sea coral reefs

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Significant natural gas (Zohr field — largest in Mediterranean); limited oil; growing renewable energy (solar in Benban — one of world's largest solar parks)
Natural Resources: Oil, natural gas, phosphates, iron ore, manganese, limestone, gypsum, gold
Technology Sector: Growing — Cairo startup hub; fintech and e-commerce growth; outsourcing destinations; government digitalization push
Major Companies: El Sewedy Electric, Commercial International Bank, Orascom Construction, EFG Hermes, Telecom Egypt, Eastern Company

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: ~73% literacy; Al-Azhar University (prestigious Islamic institution); expanding higher education
Internet Penetration: ~72% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.731 (High, ranked 105th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 30/100 (ranked 108th)
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — state-controlled media; journalists arrested; independent outlets heavily censored
Human Rights Overview: Severe restrictions under Sisi — mass arrests (40,000+ political prisoners); torture in detention; restrictions on civil society; limited freedom of expression; but some economic rights progress

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Economic recovery (IMF program), foreign currency crisis, infrastructure megaprojects (New Administrative Capital), water security (Nile Dam), regional stability, counter-terrorism
Foreign Policy Overview: Pro-Western; strong US ally; peace with Israel (Camp David); balancing relations with Russia and China; active in Africa; opposes political Islam
Relations With:
  USA: Major ally — $1.3 billion annual military aid; strategic partnership; counter-terrorism cooperation
  China: Growing economic partner — Belt and Road; Suez Canal Economic Zone; trade and investment
  Russia: Energy cooperation (El Dabaa nuclear plant); arms purchases (pre-Ukraine); strained by Ukraine war
  India: Strong historical ties; growing trade and defence cooperation; civilizational connections (Nasser-Nehru era)
  Pakistan: Good relations; defence cooperation; trade growing; alignment on Islamic issues
  EU: Major trade partner; energy cooperation (natural gas exports to Europe); migration management
  Middle East: Mediates Israel-Palestine; rivals Turkey and Qatar (Muslim Brotherhood ties); coordinates with Saudi Arabia and UAE
  Neighbouring Countries: Libya (border security, proxy concerns); Sudan (Nile water dispute); Gaza (mediator); Israel (cold peace but security cooperation)

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: USA (strategic), Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Israel (peace treaty)
Major Rivals: Turkey, Qatar (historical tensions over Muslim Brotherhood)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Suez Canal security, water security (Nile/GERD), regional stability, counter-terrorism, economic growth, Sinai security, Arab leadership
Current Major Challenges: Economic crisis (inflation, debt, currency devaluation); population growth; water scarcity (Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam); unemployment; human rights international pressure; terrorism (Sinai)

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Egypt is the Arab world's most populous country.
• The Suez Canal generates over $9 billion in annual revenue.
• The Great Pyramids of Giza are the only surviving ancient Seven Wonders.
• Cairo is the largest city in the Arab world and Africa.
