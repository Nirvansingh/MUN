STATE OF PALESTINE

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Palestine is a non-member observer state at the UN, recognized by over 140 countries, seeking full sovereignty over the West Bank, Gaza Strip, and East Jerusalem. It remains at the heart of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict and is a central issue in Middle Eastern and international diplomacy.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: East Jerusalem (claimed); Ramallah (administrative centre)
Largest City: Gaza City (Gaza Strip)
Official Name: State of Palestine
Government Type: Semi-presidential republic (divided governance)
Head of State: President Mahmoud Abbas
Head of Government: Prime Minister Mohammad Mustafa
Official Languages: Arabic
Population: ~5.5 million (West Bank: ~3.3 million, Gaza: ~2.2 million)
Area: 6,020 km² (West Bank: 5,655 km², Gaza: 365 km²)
Currency: Israeli Shekel (ILS), Jordanian Dinar (JOD), Egyptian Pound (EGP)
GDP (Nominal): ~$20 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$3,600

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Agriculture (olives, citrus, dates), stone and marble quarrying, textiles, tourism (limited), construction, small-scale manufacturing
Major Exports: Olives, olive oil, fruits, vegetables, limestone, marble, furniture, textiles
Major Imports: Food products, machinery, vehicles, fuel, electronics, construction materials, chemicals
Major Trade Partners: Israel, Jordan, Egypt, UAE, Turkey, China, Germany, USA

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: No conventional military. Palestinian Authority maintains security forces in West Bank (coordination with Israel). Hamas maintains armed wing (Al-Qassam Brigades) in Gaza.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT signatory as State of Palestine).
UN Membership: Non-member observer state (2012)
UNSC Status: Not applicable; not a UN member state
Major International Organizations: UN (observer), Arab League, OIC, NAM, ICC (member since 2015)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Central symbol in Arab and Islamic world; moral authority in Global South; limited practical sovereignty
Neighbouring Countries: Israel (surrounds West Bank), Jordan (east), Egypt (southwest border with Gaza)
Strategic Importance: Religious significance (Jerusalem, Al-Aqsa Mosque, Bethlehem); heart of Arab-Israeli conflict; key to regional peace; humanitarian concern
Important Maritime Routes: Gaza coastline on Mediterranean Sea (limited access due to Israeli blockade)
Important Geography: West Bank (mountainous, fertile valleys), Gaza Strip (flat coastal plain), Jordan Valley, Dead Sea

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Very limited — Gaza has offshore natural gas fields (Gaza Marine, undeveloped); heavily dependent on energy imports from Israel
Natural Resources: Limestone, marble, Dead Sea minerals (access limited), arable land, stone
Technology Sector: Emerging tech sector in Ramallah; IT outsourcing; mobile app development; limited by movement restrictions
Major Companies: Paltel Group, Bank of Palestine, Padico (Palestine Development and Investment)

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High literacy rate (~96%) — one of the highest in the region; strong higher education sector (Birzeit, Al-Quds)
Internet Penetration: ~75% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.715 (High, ranked 106th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: Not separately ranked (part of PA governance concerns)
Press Freedom Status: Mixed — restrictions in both West Bank (PA) and Gaza (Hamas); journalists face harassment
Human Rights Overview: Severe challenges — Israeli occupation (movement restrictions, settlements, military raids); internal Palestinian political division (Fatah vs. Hamas); restricted access to resources and services

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Statehood recognition, ending occupation, Palestinian unity, international legal action (ICC), economic development, preserving refugee rights (UNRWA)
Foreign Policy Overview: Diplomatic-focused — seeking full UN membership; international criminal complaints against Israel; broad recognition campaign; Arab League and OIC support
Relations With:
  USA: Strained — supports two-state solution but vetoes UN membership; historically strong Israel bias; limited direct engagement
  China: Growing engagement — supports Palestinian cause; Belt and Road; hosted Palestinian factions for unity talks
  Russia: Supportive — traditional ally; diplomatic support at UN; Moscow hosting faction talks
  India: Historical support for Palestine; balancing with growing Israel ties; development assistance
  Pakistan: Strong political support; no diplomatic representation in Israel; advocacy at UN and OIC
  EU: Major donor; supports two-state solution; labels settlement products; divided on recognition
  Middle East: Support from Arab League; normalization deals (Abraham Accords) weakened Arab solidarity; Iran supports Hamas; UAE, Bahrain, Morocco normalized with Israel
  Neighbouring Countries: Coordination with Jordan on Jerusalem; Egypt mediates Gaza truces; relations with Israel fluctuating (security coordination vs. tensions)

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: Arab League members, OIC countries, NAM members, Russia, China, India (historical), South Africa, Turkey
Major Rivals: Israel (occupying power); internal: Hamas (political rival)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: End of occupation, full UN membership, East Jerusalem as capital, right of return for refugees, Palestinian unity, economic viability
Current Major Challenges: Israeli settlement expansion; Gaza blockade; internal division (Fatah-Hamas split); declining international focus; PA legitimacy crisis; humanitarian situation in Gaza; water shortages

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Palestine is recognized by over 140 UN member states.
• The Dead Sea lies between Palestine (West Bank) and Jordan.
• Bethlehem, one of the world's oldest cities, is in the West Bank.
• Palestine has one of the highest literacy rates in the developing world.
