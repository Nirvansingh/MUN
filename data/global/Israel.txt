STATE OF ISRAEL

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Israel is a technologically advanced democracy and a key US ally in the Middle East, with a highly capable military and a powerful high-tech economy. It is at the centre of regional geopolitical debates, particularly regarding the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Jerusalem (disputed)
Largest City: Jerusalem
Official Name: State of Israel
Government Type: Unitary parliamentary republic
Head of State: President Isaac Herzog
Head of Government: Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu
Official Languages: Hebrew (official), Arabic (special status)
Population: ~9.9 million
Area: 22,072 km²
Currency: Israeli New Shekel (ILS)
GDP (Nominal): ~$550 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$55,000

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Technology (cybersecurity, AI, fintech, medical devices), diamonds, pharmaceuticals, defence, agriculture (drip irrigation), tourism
Major Exports: Electronics, software, machinery, medical equipment, diamonds, chemicals, agricultural products, defence equipment
Major Imports: Crude oil, vehicles, machinery, electronics, grains, clothing, chemicals, precious stones
Major Trade Partners: USA, China, Germany, Switzerland, UK, Netherlands, India, Belgium

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Highly capable military (~170,000 active, ~465,000 reservists). Nuclear-capable. Advanced air force and intelligence services (Mossad, Shin Bet). Mandatory conscription. Significant experience in counter-terrorism and asymmetric warfare.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Widely believed to possess nuclear weapons (undeclared). Not NPT signatory.
UN Membership: 1949
UNSC Status: Not on Council (never served on UNSC)
Major International Organizations: UN, OECD, IMF, World Bank; not a member of: Arab League, OIC, NAM, ICC membership revoked

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Dominant military power in the Middle East; key US ally; influence through technology exports; complex relationships with neighbours
Neighbouring Countries: Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, Egypt (peace treaties with Egypt and Jordan)
Strategic Importance: Only democracy in the Middle East (by self-definition); key US security partner; major technology hub; nuclear deterrent; disputed territories status
Important Maritime Routes: Mediterranean Sea, Red Sea (via Eilat), Gulf of Aqaba
Important Geography: Coastal plain, Judean and Samarian hills, Negev Desert, Jordan Valley, Sea of Galilee, Dead Sea (lowest point on Earth)

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Significant natural gas fields (Tamar, Leviathan) making Israel energy independent; growing solar energy; limited oil
Natural Resources: Natural gas, potash (Dead Sea), copper, phosphates, bromine, magnesium, salt
Technology Sector: World-leading — cybersecurity (Check Point, Wiz, Palo Alto Networks roots), AI, fintech, medical devices, agritech, autonomous vehicles
Major Companies: Check Point, Mobileye (Intel), Wix, Nice Systems, Teva Pharmaceuticals, ICL Group, Bank Hapoalim, Elbit Systems, IAI

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High — 97% literacy; world-class universities (Hebrew University, Technion, Weizmann Institute)
Internet Penetration: ~92% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.915 (Very High, ranked 25th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 63/100 (ranked 34th)
Press Freedom Status: Mostly free — ranked ~98th globally; concerns over government pressure, anti-democratic judicial reforms
Human Rights Overview: Strong democratic institutions but significant concerns over treatment of Palestinians in occupied territories, settlement expansion, discrimination against Arab citizens, and asylum policy

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Security (Iran nuclear threat), normalization with Arab states (Abraham Accords), settlement expansion, judicial reform, countering de-legitimization (BDS)
Foreign Policy Overview: Self-reliance on security; US alliance as cornerstone; normalization with Gulf states; opposition to Iran nuclear program; managing Palestinian conflict via security coordination
Relations With:
  USA: Unbreakable ally — $3.8 billion annual military aid; intelligence cooperation; diplomatic cover at UN
  China: Growing trade and investment; technology cooperation; but cautious on China-Middle East engagement
  Russia: Complex — coordination on Syria de-confliction; concerns over Russia-Iran ties; large Russian-speaking population
  India: Strong and growing — defence cooperation, technology, agriculture, diaspora, strategic convergence
  Pakistan: No diplomatic relations; tensions over Kashmir and Palestine solidarity
  EU: Largest trading partner; but tensions over settlement policy and human rights
  Middle East: Peace treaties with Egypt and Jordan; Abraham Accords with UAE, Bahrain, Morocco; no relations with Saudi Arabia yet; hostility with Iran, Syria, Hezbollah, Hamas
  Neighbouring Countries: Relative stability on Egyptian and Jordanian borders; ongoing threats from Syria and Lebanon (Hezbollah)

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: USA, India (growing), UAE (Abraham Accords), Bahrain, Morocco, Egypt (peace), Jordan (peace), UK, Germany
Major Rivals: Iran, Lebanon (Hezbollah), Syria, Qatar (Al Jazeera), Palestinians (Hamas)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: National security, Iran nuclear threat prevention, technology leadership, Jerusalem status, settlement expansion, normalization with Arab world, maintaining Jewish majority
Current Major Challenges: Iran nuclear and proxy threat; Palestinian conflict (two-state solution collapse); internal political polarization (judicial reform); international isolation (ICC, UN votes); Houthi missile threats

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Israel has the highest number of engineers per capita in the world.
• Israel has the most startups per capita globally (Startup Nation).
• The Dead Sea is the lowest point on Earth (~430 meters below sea level).
• Israel's Iron Dome is the world's most tested missile defence system.
