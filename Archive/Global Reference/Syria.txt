SYRIAN ARAB REPUBLIC

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Syria remains shattered by over a decade of civil war, with the Assad regime backed by Russia and Iran controlling most territory. The country is a central theatre of regional proxy conflicts and one of the world's worst humanitarian crises.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Damascus
Largest City: Aleppo (pre-war)
Official Name: Syrian Arab Republic
Government Type: Unitary semi-presidential republic (authoritarian)
Head of State: President Bashar al-Assad
Head of Government: Prime Minister Hussein Arnous
Official Languages: Arabic
Population: ~23 million (pre-war: 21 million); ~6 million refugees abroad
Area: 185,180 km²
Currency: Syrian Pound (SYP)
GDP (Nominal): ~$20 billion (estimated, 2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$870

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Oil and gas (declining), agriculture, textiles, chemicals, cement, food processing
Major Exports: Crude oil (limited), phosphates, textiles, fruits, vegetables, cotton, olive oil, soap
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, wheat, rice, pharmaceuticals, electronics, chemicals, iron and steel
Major Trade Partners: Russia, Iran, China, UAE, Turkey, Iraq, Lebanon

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Pre-war military of ~170,000, significantly reduced; sustained by Russian and Iranian support. Conscription-based. Russian bases (Tartus naval, Khmeimim airbase). Iranian militias and Hezbollah operate in Syria.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). Israel conducted airstrike on alleged nuclear reactor (2007).
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: Not on Council (last served 2022-23)
Major International Organizations: UN, Arab League (re-admitted 2023), OIC, NAM; suspended from: Arab League (2011-2023)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Significantly reduced — dependent on Russia and Iran for survival; isolated in Arab world (but slowly reintegrating)
Neighbouring Countries: Turkey (north), Iraq (east), Jordan (south), Israel (southwest), Lebanon (west)
Strategic Importance: Central to Middle Eastern geopolitics; Russia's key Middle Eastern ally; Iran's land bridge to Hezbollah (Lebanon); Arab-Israeli conflict player
Important Maritime Routes: Mediterranean Sea (limited coastline)
Important Geography: Fertile coastal plain, Syrian Desert, Euphrates River, Golan Heights (occupied by Israel), Aleppo and Damascus urban centres

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Declining oil production (pre-war ~400,000 bpd, now ~25,000); significant natural gas (some production); most energy infrastructure damaged
Natural Resources: Oil, natural gas, phosphates, chrome, manganese, asphalt, iron ore, salt
Technology Sector: Minimal — pre-war IT small; most infrastructure destroyed; brain drain
Major Companies: Syrian Petroleum Company (state), SyriaTel (telecom)

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: Pre-war ~86% literacy; severely impacted by war (many schools destroyed, displaced)
Internet Penetration: ~50% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.557 (Low, ranked 157th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: Not ranked
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — state-controlled media; independent journalists arrested or exiled
Human Rights Overview: Catastrophic — over 500,000 killed in civil war; chemical weapons used against civilians; torture in detention; forced disappearances; displacement of half the population; war crimes documented by UN

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Regime survival, reconstruction, Arab League reintegration, sanctions relief, regaining territorial control, countering ISIS remnants
Foreign Policy Overview: Aligned with Russia and Iran; anti-Israel; anti-Western; seeking rehabilitation in Arab world; normalization with Turkey under discussion
Relations With:
  USA: Hostile — sanctions (Caesar Act); no diplomatic relations; US forces in Kurdish-controlled northeast
  China: Growing engagement — Belt and Road interest; reconstruction potential; diplomatic support
  Russia: Primary ally — military intervention (2015) saved regime; naval and air bases; diplomatic protection at UN
  India: Limited engagement; India supports territorial integrity; limited trade; concerns over terrorism
  Pakistan: Limited engagement; diplomatic solidarity in OIC
  EU: Sanctions; no reconstruction funding; humanitarian aid only; no normalization
  Middle East: Re-admitted to Arab League (2023); UAE leading normalization; Saudi Arabia cautious; Turkey hostile but negotiating; Israel periodic airstrikes
  Neighbouring Countries: Iraq (trade and border); Turkey (hostile but negotiating); Jordan (border security cooperation); Lebanon (Hezbollah involvement); Israel (Golan Heights occupation, airstrikes)

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: Russia, Iran, Hezbollah (Lebanon), Iraqi Shia militias
Major Rivals: USA, Israel, Turkey, Syrian opposition groups, ISIS, Kurdish forces (SDF)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Regime survival, territorial reunification, reconstruction, sanctions relief, Arab reintegration, counter-terrorism
Current Major Challenges: Economic collapse (hyperinflation, currency devaluation); war-torn infrastructure; sanctions; reconstruction funding (estimated $400 billion needed); displacement; ISIS remnant insurgency; drug trafficking (Captagon)

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• The Syrian civil war is one of the deadliest conflicts of the 21st century (500,000+ killed).
• Chemical weapons were used in the Ghouta attack (2013) and Khan Sheikhoun (2017).
• Damascus is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world.
• Syria's economy has shrunk by over 60% since 2011.
