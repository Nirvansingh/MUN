REPUBLIC OF INDIA

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
India is the world's most populous country and fifth-largest economy, emerging as a major global power in technology, space, and defence. It plays a pivotal role in South Asian geopolitics and is increasingly central to US and Western Indo-Pacific strategy.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: New Delhi
Largest City: Mumbai
Official Name: Republic of India
Government Type: Federal parliamentary democratic republic
Head of State: President Droupadi Murmu
Head of Government: Prime Minister Narendra Modi
Official Languages: Hindi (official), English (subsidiary official), 22 scheduled languages
Population: ~1.44 billion
Area: 3,287,263 km² (7th largest)
Currency: Indian Rupee (INR)
GDP (Nominal): ~$4.1 trillion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$2,850

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Information technology, telecommunications, pharmaceuticals, agriculture, automotive, steel, textiles, financial services, space technology
Major Exports: Refined petroleum, diamonds, pharmaceuticals, vehicles, machinery, iron and steel, textiles, software services
Major Imports: Crude oil, gold, electronics, machinery, chemicals, coal, plastics
Major Trade Partners: USA, China, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Russia, Indonesia, Iraq, Singapore

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Third-largest military globally (~1.45 million active personnel). Major naval power in the Indian Ocean. Growing defence indigenization (Make in India). Significant nuclear weapons capability.
Nuclear Weapons Status: De facto nuclear weapon state (not NPT signatory). ~172 warheads.
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: Non-permanent member (most recently 2021-22); seeks permanent membership
Major International Organizations: UN, G20, BRICS, SCO, QUAD, Commonwealth, ISA, IORA, ASEAN (dialogue partner), WTO, IMF, World Bank

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Dominant power in South Asia; growing influence in Indo-Pacific, Central Asia, and Africa
Neighbouring Countries: Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Afghanistan (via disputed Kashmir region)
Strategic Importance: Controls Indian Ocean sea lines of communication; key member of Quad; nuclear-armed state; largest democracy; critical counterweight to China
Important Maritime Routes: Indian Ocean, Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea, Strait of Malacca (eastern access), Persian Gulf (energy imports)
Important Geography: Himalayan mountain border (north), Thar Desert (west), fertile Gangetic plains, Deccan Plateau, extensive coastline (7,517 km)

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Third-largest energy consumer globally; growing renewable sector (solar, wind); significant coal reserves (4th largest); expanding nuclear capacity; large oil importer
Natural Resources: Coal, iron ore, bauxite, manganese, mica, titanium, diamonds, natural gas, limestone
Technology Sector: World-leading IT services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro); major space program (ISRO); growing semiconductor design; digital public infrastructure leader (UPI, Aadhaar)
Major Companies: Reliance Industries, TCS, HDFC Bank, Infosys, ICICI Bank, Bharti Airtel, State Bank of India, Tata Motors, L&T, Adani Group

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: ~77% literacy; world-class engineering (IITs) and management (IIMs) institutions
Internet Penetration: ~55% of population (~800 million users)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.644 (Medium, ranked 134th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 39/100 (ranked 85th)
Press Freedom Status: Mixed — ranked ~161st globally; concerns over government pressure on independent media
Human Rights Overview: Constitutional protections but faces challenges in religious freedom (communal violence), caste discrimination, women's safety, and freedom of expression

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Self-reliant India (Atmanirbhar Bharat), economic growth, infrastructure, technology leadership, border security with China, global recognition as a major power
Foreign Policy Overview: Multi-aligned — strategic partnerships with US, Russia, Japan, Australia (Quad), Israel, UAE, France; balancing China; focus on neighbourhood first policy; active in Global South
Relations With:
  USA: Growing strategic partnership — defence (COMCASA, LEMOA), technology (iCET), Quad, diaspora ties
  China: Border tensions (Galwan 2020); economic interdependence vs. strategic rivalry in South Asia and Indian Ocean
  Russia: Traditional ally; major arms supplier; energy cooperation; growing trade despite Western sanctions
  India: —
  Pakistan: Hostile relations; Kashmir dispute; cross-border terrorism; nuclear rivalry; limited trade
  EU: Growing trade and investment; digital partnership; technology cooperation
  Middle East: Strong ties with UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Iran; energy security; diaspora (8 million+ workers)
  Neighbouring Countries: Neighbourhood First policy — development assistance; China competition for influence; tensions with Pakistan and China

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: Quad members (USA, Japan, Australia), France, UAE, Israel, Russia (strategic partner)
Major Rivals: Pakistan, China (primary strategic competitor)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Economic growth, border security (China, Pakistan), Indian Ocean dominance, energy security, technology leadership, UNSC permanent seat, counter-terrorism
Current Major Challenges: Unemployment and underemployment; agricultural distress; border infrastructure gaps; communal polarization; bureaucratic inefficiency; climate change adaptation

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• India is the world's largest democracy.
• India has the second-largest English-speaking population after the US.
• ISRO is the only Asian space agency to reach Mars on the first attempt (Mangalyaan).
• India's UPI system processes over 10 billion digital transactions per month.
