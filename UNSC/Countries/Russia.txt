NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Bypassing Western maritime and financial sanctions to export oil, gas, and grain.
• Securing and developing the Northern Sea Route (NSR) as a sovereign transit corridor.
• Protecting black sea fleet operations and maintaining trade gateways in the Baltic.
• Countering Western economic blockades by redirecting trade to Asia, BRICS, and Africa.
• Maintaining strategic influence over energy transit routes to Europe and Central Asia.
• Opposing the enforcement of Western oil price caps and shipping bans.
• Protecting domestic agriculture and mineral exports from international disruption.
• Expanding naval presence in the Eastern Mediterranean and Arctic regions.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Accuses Western states of breaking global supply chains through illegal, unilateral sanctions. Promotes alternative, non-Western logistics routes.
• Maritime Security: Emphasizes sovereign control over territorial waters. Rejects Western-led security operations as attempts to police global trade lanes.
• Free Trade: Criticizes Western protectionism and weaponization of the SWIFT system. Advocates for de-dollarized trade among sovereign states.
• Sanctions: Declares all sanctions not authorized by the UNSC as illegal, null, and void.
• International Cooperation: Focuses on cooperation within BRICS, SCO, and bilateral agreements to build secure transport links.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Largest territory in the world, controlling the strategic Northern Sea Route.
✓ Major global exporter of oil, natural gas, wheat, fertilizers, and critical metals.
✓ World's largest fleet of nuclear-powered icebreakers, securing Arctic trade.
✓ Strong cyber warfare and electronic jamming capabilities to defend networks.
✓ P5 veto power, allowing Russia to block any unfavorable UNSC resolutions.
✓ Strategic naval bases in the Black Sea, Baltic Sea, Russian Far East, and Syria.
✓ Deep partnerships with major non-Western energy consumers (China, India).

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Severe economic isolation from Western financial markets and technologies.
✗ Dependency of shipping logistics on a "shadow fleet" of older, unregulated vessels.
✗ High military expenditures and resource strain from the ongoing conflict in Ukraine.
✗ Vulnerability of Black Sea naval assets to modern automated drone attacks.
✗ Sanction-induced delays in importing advanced high-tech components.
✗ Infrastructure bottlenecks in redirecting energy exports from West to East.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Western sanctions are the primary cause of global supply chain disruptions.
• The Northern Sea Route is a sovereign transport corridor subject to Russian regulation.
• Undersea infrastructure attacks like Nord Stream require independent, transparent investigations.
• Russian agricultural and energy exports are critical for global food and energy security.
• Military operations in the Black Sea are conducted to defend national security.
• Russia respects freedom of navigation but rejects attempts to militarize international choke points.
• Western oil price caps violate free-market principles and WTO rules.
• Alternative payment systems are necessary to counter Western financial monopoly.
