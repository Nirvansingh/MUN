AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE
==================================================

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: YELLOW (High Fertility OECD Outlier & Foreign Caregiver Destination)

Why?
• Highest Total Fertility Rate in the entire OECD: ~2.9 to 3.0 children per woman (nearly double the OECD average of 1.5)
• Concurrently maintains high female labor force participation (~70%), defying the conventional trade-off between fertility and employment
• Heavy reliance on international live-in caregivers for eldercare and disability: Hosts over 60,000 foreign caregivers, predominantly Filipina women operating under bilateral state agreements
• Diverse societal care dynamics: Secular high-tech dual-earner households versus Ultra-Orthodox (Haredi) communities where women are frequently the sole breadwinners while husbands study Torah
• Severe childcare costs for children under 3 and major wartime family displacements and trauma care burdens

Hot Topics
• The Bilateral Labor Agreement (BLA) with the Philippines for home care workers: Government-to-government recruitment eliminating placement fees
• Pronatalist state policies: Universal state-funded in vitro fertilization (IVF) treatments up to two live children
• Childcare affordability: High cost of private infant care ("Maoz") before free public preschool starts at age 3
• Gender wage gap (~23%) driven by long working hours in the tech sector and career interruptions
• Wartime care burdens: Thousands of mobilized reservist families leaving mothers to manage solo caregiving under emergency conditions

Expected Debate Frequency: Medium-High

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Asserts that high fertility and female economic empowerment can coexist through pro-family social norms and advanced technological productivity.
• Highlights state support for fertility: Free, state-funded IVF treatment for women up to age 45 until they have two children (world's most generous).
• Promotes statutory maternity leave: 15 weeks fully paid at 100% of previous earnings by the National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi), expandable to 26 weeks.
• Showcases government-to-government (G2G) recruitment under the Bilateral Agreement with the Philippines, which successfully eliminated illegal agency recruitment fees for caregivers.
• Free public preschool education guaranteed for all children aged 3 to 6.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Protect national demographic vitality and maintain high fertility rates across all population sectors.
• Attract and retain skilled foreign caregivers (especially from the Philippines) to care for elderly citizens and disabled veterans with dignity.
• Defend its international human rights record against geopolitical criticism in UN forums.
• Enhance female participation in high-productivity STEM and defense technology sectors.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ Highest fertility rate in the OECD (~2.9) combined with robust female labor force participation (~70%)
✓ Free, state-funded IVF treatments up to age 45 covered under the national healthcare basket
✓ 15 weeks of fully paid maternity leave at 100% wage replacement via Bituach Leumi
✓ Government-to-Government (G2G) Bilateral Agreement with the Philippines: Completely eliminated predatory recruitment fees for caregivers
✓ Free universal public preschool for all children aged 3 and above

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Infant Care Affordability Deficit: For children aged 0 to 3, state-supervised daycare slots are severely limited; private daycare costs consume up to 35% of a mother's salary
✗ 23% Gender Wage Gap: Persistent pay gap driven by the high-tech sector's extreme overtime culture, which penalizes mothers who cannot work 10-12 hour days
✗ Strict Visa Ties for Foreign Caregivers: Migrant caregivers are legally tied to specialized care visas, restricting job mobility outside of nursing/care
✗ Internal Divides: Ultra-Orthodox women face low average wages in underpaid clerical work, while Arab-Israeli women face low labor participation (~40%) due to local infrastructure deficits
✗ Wartime Care Shock: Long-term military reserve mobilizations have left hundreds of thousands of mothers single-handedly carrying family care and trauma burdens

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• ~2.9: Total fertility rate in Israel (highest in OECD, where average is 1.5).
• 60,000+: Foreign home care workers (predominantly from the Philippines) caring for seniors and disabled citizens.
• 100%: Salary replacement during 15 weeks of statutory maternity leave.
• 23%: Gender wage gap in Israel.
• 0: Placement fees legally permitted under the G2G bilateral caregiver agreement with the Philippines.

PHILIPPINES-ISRAEL RELATIONS
----------------------------
• Outstanding, historic bilateral partnership: Tens of thousands of Filipino caregivers work in Israel, deeply respected for their dedication. During the October 2023 attacks, several Filipina caregivers heroically refused to abandon their elderly Israeli wards.
• The Philippines and Israel share a model G2G recruitment system that serves as a global benchmark for eliminating predatory recruitment fees.

GSL TALKING POINTS
------------------
• "Israel demonstrates that a society does not have to accept demographic extinction to have an advanced economy. With universal maternal healthcare and free preschool from age 3, family growth and women's participation can align."
• "The G2G caregiver partnership between the Philippines and Israel proves that government-to-government agreements can wipe out illegal placement fees and guarantee fair wages for foreign care workers."

RESOLUTION IDEAS
----------------
• Model Resolution Clause on Government-to-Government (G2G) recruitment mechanisms for care workers to completely eliminate private broker debt.
• Universal coverage of maternal reproductive and fertility healthcare within statutory national health systems.
