================================================================
UNHRC SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO: AI & PRIVACY
================================================================

This document details four debate-ready solutions for resolutions addressing digital privacy and artificial intelligence.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 1: INDEPENDENT DATA PROTECTION AUTHORITIES (DPAS)
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Mandating the establishment of national, politically independent watchdogs with binding enforcement powers to monitor both public and private data processing.
• Key Elements:
  - Authority to issue fines to corporations and state bodies.
  - Mandatory audit power over government security databases.
  - Grievance redressal channels for citizens to report privacy breaches.
• Bloc Support: Strongly supported by Western and Privacy blocs.
• Opposed by: Sovereignty bloc (who reject external or non-executive audits of state security databases).

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 2: GLOBAL AI STANDARDS REGISTRY
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Establishing a voluntary, UN-backed international registry where developers of frontier AI models log safety parameters, training data sizes, and human rights impact assessments.
• Key Elements:
  - Managed under the OHCHR and ITU.
  - Focuses on identifying algorithmic biases before models are exported globally.
  - Offers certification marks for privacy-compliant AI systems.
• Bloc Support: Broad support, though Western nations prefer voluntary registries over binding licensing.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 3: GLOBAL DIGITAL SANDBOXES FOR THE GLOBAL SOUTH
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Creating regional, secure computing environments where developing nations can test, refine, and deploy AI models with technical support from developed states and tech companies.
• Key Elements:
  - Funded through private-public partnerships and international development banks.
  - Focuses on public service applications (healthcare, agriculture, transport).
  - Ensures data privacy compliance templates are built-in.
• Bloc Support: High support from Developing and Global South blocs; key compromise area for Western states.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SOLUTION 4: BIOMETRIC SAFETY PROTOCOLS IN LAW ENFORCEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Drafting international guidelines and restrictions on the deployment of facial recognition technology (FRT) in police investigations and public surveillance.
• Key Elements:
  - Requiring prior, independent judicial warrants for targeted FRT operations.
  - Complete ban on real-time biometric scanning during peaceful public assemblies.
  - Mandating third-party bias auditing of law enforcement software.
• Bloc Support: Strongly supported by civil-society-aligned nations; opposed or heavily qualified by centralized/sovereign security blocs.
