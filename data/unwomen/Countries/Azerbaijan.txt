AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE
==================================================

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: YELLOW (South Caucasus Energy Producer & Traditional Care Model)
Delegate: Heerva Rajpara

Why?
• Historical Pioneer in the Muslim World: First Muslim-majority democracy to grant women full voting rights (in 1918, ahead of the US and UK)
• Statutory Paid Maternity Leave: 126 calendar days (70 days prenatal, 56 days postnatal) at 100% of average earnings funded by the State Social Protection Fund
• Relatively High Female Labor Force Participation (~62%), but with heavy vertical and horizontal occupational segregation (women concentrated in lower-paying state education, healthcare, and subsistence agriculture)
• Deep Traditional Domestic Care Burden: Strong cultural expectation that women bear sole responsibility for cooking, cleaning, and multi-generational in-law eldercare
• The Sex-Selective Abortion Legacy: Historically recorded one of the world's most skewed sex ratios at birth (up to 116 boys per 100 girls in the 2000s, now improving to ~112) driven by cultural son preference

Hot Topics
• The COP29 Climate Summit in Baku (November 2024) and the intersection of climate resilience with rural female care burdens
• State Social Protection Fund reforms: Expanding targeted social assistance (TSA) to rural families
• Skewed sex ratio at birth and public campaigns combating son preference
• Banned occupations list for women: The Labor Code historically restricted women from over 600 professions (reforms underway)
• Childcare accessibility in rural regions outside the capital city of Baku

Expected Debate Frequency: Medium

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Highlights proud historical legacy: Equal political rights established in 1918 under the Azerbaijan Democratic Republic.
• Showcases 126 days of fully paid statutory maternity leave funded by the State Social Protection Fund, preventing employer discrimination.
• Highlights the State Committee for Family, Women and Children Affairs (SCFWCA) leading inter-agency gender mainstreaming and anti-violence campaigns.
• Promotes the "State Program on the Elimination of Sex-Selective Abortions" to restore natural gender balance at birth.
• Emphasizes the COP29 presidency in Baku, prioritizing women's leadership in green transitions and climate-resilient community infrastructure.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Showcase Azerbaijan as a modern, progressive, and secular bridge between the Islamic world and Europe.
• Leverage the COP29 global platform to attract green energy and rural development investments that alleviate female domestic labor.
• Preserve social cohesion and family values while modernizing the labor code to remove historical gender restrictions.
• Maintain constructive ties with both Western institutions and the Non-Aligned Movement (NAM).

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ 126 days of fully paid statutory maternity leave covered 100% by state social insurance
✓ Equal constitutional rights and early franchise pioneer (1918)
✓ High female literacy rate (over 99%) and gender parity in primary and secondary schooling
✓ Proactive state initiatives reducing the skewed sex ratio at birth through healthcare awareness and ultrasound regulations
✓ Expanding targeted social assistance (TSA) reaching hundreds of thousands of low-income families

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ 40%+ Gender Wage Gap: Men earn significantly more due to concentration in high-paying oil and gas extraction, while women are concentrated in public health and education
✗ Heavy Unpaid Care Divide: Azerbaijani women perform an average of 4.5 hours of unpaid domestic care daily, compared to less than 1 hour for men
✗ Rural Childcare Deficit: Kindergartens and nurseries are scarce in rural agrarian regions, forcing mothers to rely on extended family or stay home
✗ The Son Preference Legacy: Deep-seated traditional preference for sons over daughters has distorted sex ratios at birth for decades
✗ Banned Professions Legacy: Labor Code Article 241 historically barred women from hundreds of industrial and transportation jobs (currently undergoing gradual revision)

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• 126 Days: Paid statutory maternity leave at 100% salary.
• 1918: Year women were granted full voting rights in Azerbaijan.
• 40%+: Gender wage gap driven by the dominance of the male-heavy hydrocarbon sector.
• ~112: Boys born per 100 girls (improving from 116, but still higher than natural 105 biological ratio).

PHILIPPINES-AZERBAIJAN RELATIONS
--------------------------------
• Both nations share a commitment to secular governance, national sovereignty, and progressive social protection programs.
• Can collaborate on resolutions linking climate adaptation funds to rural domestic water and energy infrastructure.

GSL TALKING POINTS
------------------
• "Azerbaijan proved over a century ago that progress in the Muslim world is fully compatible with women's rights. Today, we must extend that vision to the care economy."
• "We must invest in rural childcare and clean water infrastructure so that women outside major industrial capitals enjoy the same economic freedom as urban professionals."

RESOLUTION IDEAS
----------------
• International climate adaptation financing specifically earmarked for rural household water and clean energy access to reduce female time poverty.
• Multilateral programs combating son preference and promoting the equal economic valuation of girl children.
