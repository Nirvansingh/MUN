================================================================
SAUDI ARABIA
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
Capital: Riyadh
Government: Unitary Islamic Absolute Monarchy
Current Leader: King Salman bin Abdulaziz / Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman
Population: ~36 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent voice)
Major International Organizations: UN, G20, OPEC+, OIC, Arab League, GCC, BRICS (Invited)

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• United States
• United Arab Emirates
• Bahrain
• Egypt
• United Kingdom
• France
• Pakistan
• China (Strategic Partner)

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• Iran (Complex Diplomacy/Regional Rival)
• Yemen (Houthi Authorities)

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Protecting Red Sea shipping lanes vital for oil and cargo exports.
• Securing the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait from Houthi missile and drone threats.
• Diversifying the national economy under the Vision 2030 program.
• Developing mega-ports (Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdullah Port).
• Maintaining global oil market stability through OPEC+ coordination.
• Promoting the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC).
• Expanding trade and investment links with China and Indo-Pacific partners.
• Protecting critical energy and desalination infrastructure from regional attacks.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Focuses on logistics hub development and regional corridors (IMEC). Seeks to locate key manufacturing supply chains domestically under Vision 2030.
• Maritime Security: Supports freedom of navigation. Participates in security coalitions (CMF) but balances Red Sea deployments to protect Yemen peace talks.
• Free Trade: Strongly supports open, rules-based free trade, particularly for oil and petrochemicals.
• Sanctions: Does not enforce unilateral Western sanctions. Maintains OPEC+ energy coordination with Russia.
• International Cooperation: Coordinates via GCC, OPEC, Arab League, G20, and UN.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Red Sea shipping disruptions and Saudi-Houthi peace negotiations.
• Promotion and development of the IMEC trade corridor.
• OPEC+ oil production decisions and energy market stability.
• Port infrastructure modernization and digitalization under Vision 2030.
• Security of the Bab-el-Mandeb and Gulf of Aden transit routes.
• Cyber defense of oil facilities and maritime database networks.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ World's leading crude oil exporter, holding immense financial reserves (PIF).
✓ Strategic coastline on both the Red Sea and Persian Gulf.
✓ Central node of the GCC and key leader of the Arab League.
✓ Major investor in Global South infrastructure projects.
✓ Highly modern port infrastructure (Jeddah is a top global hub).
✓ Close defense partnership with the United States.
✓ Expanding diplomatic ties with China, Russia, and India.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High dependency of national budget on crude oil export revenues.
✗ Vulnerability of oil terminals and refineries to regional drone/missile attacks.
✗ Geopolitical exposure to conflicts in Yemen, Bab-el-Mandeb, and Strait of Hormuz.
✗ Limited domestic food production, relying on agricultural imports.
✗ High dependency on foreign technology and specialized logistics workforce.
✗ Tension between US security ties and OPEC+ Russia alignment.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Saudi Arabia seeks regional stability to protect global energy lanes.
• Vision 2030 builds secure, modern transport infrastructure for global trade.
• OPEC+ decisions are based on oil market stability, not politics.
• Saudi Arabia coordinates with international partners to secure shipping.
• Regional disputes must be resolved through diplomatic dialogue, not force.
• Saudi port safety measures comply with the highest international standards.
• Undersea cable security is a vital national security interest.
• Saudi Arabia opposes actions that escalate regional maritime conflicts.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does Saudi Arabia balance security ties with the US and OPEC+ cooperation with Russia?
Q2. What measures protect Red Sea ports from regional conflict spillover?
Q3. How does Saudi Arabia address domestic food security vulnerabilities during supply chain shocks?
Q4. Does the development of IMEC bypass and damage Egypt's Suez Canal revenues?
Q5. What checks prevent Saudi maritime systems from cyber attacks?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Iran: What actions will Iran take to ensure its allies stop targeting Red Sea commercial shipping?
• Egypt: How can we deepen joint port security to protect Suez Canal traffic?
• USA: How will the US support regional logistics corridors like IMEC?
• India: How can we accelerate bilateral trade agreements to secure oil transit?
• Yemen (Houthi Authorities): When will you halt attacks on civilian shipping lanes?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• UN resolutions that jeopardize Yemen peace negotiations.
• Resolutions that restrict sovereign control of national oil production.
• Any framework that bypasses GCC coordination on regional security.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: UAE, Bahrain, Egypt, GCC members, United States, G20 partners.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Iran (preserve diplomatic normalization).
• Possible compromises: Support joint international funding for Red Sea port security.
• Main objectives: Secure energy corridors, defend OPEC+ autonomy, promote Vision 2030.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high composure, authority, and diplomatic weight.
✓ Frame Saudi Arabia as a global economic leader and a force for regional stability.
✓ Emphasize the success of Vision 2030 in modernizing global logistics.
✓ Support G20 and OPEC+ decisions, framing them as market-stabilizing.
✓ Deflect security queries by highlighting active diplomatic tracks.
