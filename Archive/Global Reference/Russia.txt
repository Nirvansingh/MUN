RUSSIAN FEDERATION

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Russia is the world's largest country by land area and a major military power with the largest nuclear arsenal. It remains a decisive player in global security issues, particularly in Eastern Europe, Central Asia, and the Middle East.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Moscow
Largest City: Moscow
Official Name: Russian Federation
Government Type: Federal semi-presidential republic
Head of State: President Vladimir Putin
Head of Government: Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin
Official Languages: Russian
Population: ~143 million
Area: 17,098,246 km² (largest country)
Currency: Russian Ruble (RUB)
GDP (Nominal): ~$2.2 trillion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$15,500

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Oil and gas, defense, mining, metallurgy, machinery, chemicals, agriculture, space technology
Major Exports: Crude oil, natural gas, coal, refined petroleum, wheat, steel, fertilizers, precious metals
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, pharmaceuticals, electronics, clothing, chemicals
Major Trade Partners: China, India, Turkey, Kazakhstan, Belarus, UAE, Egypt

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Second-largest military budget (~$120 billion). Largest nuclear arsenal globally. Extensive land forces with combat experience in Ukraine. Significant cyber warfare and electronic warfare capabilities.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Recognized nuclear weapon state (NPT). ~5,889 warheads (largest arsenal globally).
UN Membership: Founding member (1945); inherited Soviet seat in 1991
UNSC Status: P5 (permanent member with veto power)
Major International Organizations: UN, BRICS, SCO, CSTO, G20, Eurasian Economic Union, CIS

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Dominant in post-Soviet states; significant influence in Middle East (Syria, Iran); growing engagement in Africa and Arctic
Neighbouring Countries: Norway, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Belarus, Ukraine, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, China, Mongolia, North Korea
Strategic Importance: Controls vast energy resources (largest natural gas exporter); Arctic strategic position; key player in Eurasian security; controls vital energy pipelines to Europe
Important Maritime Routes: Northern Sea Route (Arctic), Baltic Sea, Black Sea (via Ukraine), Pacific ports (Vladivostok)
Important Geography: Ural Mountains (Europe-Asia divide), Siberia (resource-rich), Arctic coastline, Volga River system, Lake Baikal

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Largest natural gas reserves globally; second-largest coal reserves; significant oil production (third-largest); major nuclear power producer
Natural Resources: Oil, natural gas, coal, diamonds, gold, platinum, nickel, copper, timber, uranium, rare earth metals
Technology Sector: Growing but constrained by sanctions — strengths in cybersecurity, space technology, nuclear engineering; limited semiconductor industry (dependent on imports)
Major Companies: Gazprom, Rosneft, Lukoil, Sberbank, Rosatom, Norilsk Nickel, Yandex, Kaspersky

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High literacy rate (~99%); strong STEM education tradition
Internet Penetration: ~88% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.822 (Very High, ranked 52nd)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 28/100 (ranked 137th)
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — state-controlled major media; independent outlets labelled "foreign agents"
Human Rights Overview: Severe restrictions on political opposition, LGBTQ+ rights, and media freedom; concerns over treatment of minorities; authoritarian governance under Putin

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Ukraine war objectives, countering NATO expansion, maintaining energy revenues under sanctions, strategic partnership with China, asserting influence in post-Soviet space
Foreign Policy Overview: Revisionist — challenges US-led international order; military intervention in Ukraine; support for Assad in Syria; deepening ties with China, Iran, and India; uses energy exports as geopolitical leverage
Relations With:
  USA: Adversarial — Ukraine war, sanctions, arms control breakdown, cyber conflict
  China: Strategic partnership — aligned against US hegemony; energy exports; military cooperation
  Russia: —
  India: Traditional ally; major arms customer; growing trade and energy cooperation despite Western pressure
  Pakistan: Warming relations; military and economic cooperation; counter-terrorism coordination
  EU: Severely strained — Ukraine war; energy decoupling; sanctions and counter-sanctions
  Middle East: Key player — supports Assad (Syria); engages with Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Turkey
  Neighbouring Countries: Assertive influence — military intervention in Ukraine; leverages energy and military ties in Central Asia; tensions with Baltic states and Georgia

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: China (strategic partner), Belarus (union state), Iran (military partner), North Korea, Syria, Armenia (CSTO)
Major Rivals: USA, NATO members, Ukraine, Japan, EU

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Security buffer zone in Eastern Europe, maintaining energy revenues, preserving nuclear parity with US, influence in post-Soviet states, Arctic dominance
Current Major Challenges: Ukraine war attrition; unprecedented Western sanctions; demographic decline; economic isolation; technology restrictions; long-term energy transition reducing fossil fuel demand

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Russia spans 11 time zones — the most of any country.
• Russia has the largest stockpile of nuclear weapons in the world.
• The Trans-Siberian Railway is the longest railway line on Earth (~9,289 km).
• Russia supplies ~40% of EU's natural gas (pre-Ukraine war levels).
