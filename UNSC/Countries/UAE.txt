UAE
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: United Arab Emirates
Capital: Abu Dhabi
Government: Federal Constitutional Monarchy
Current Leader: President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan
Population: ~10 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Frequent voice)
Major International Organizations: UN, GCC, OPEC, Arab League, BRICS, G20 (Guest), WTO, IMO

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• Saudi Arabia
• United States
• India
• China
• Egypt
• France
• United Kingdom
• GCC Member States

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• Iran (Complex Diplomacy/Territorial Dispute over Islands)

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Protecting shipping lanes through the Strait of Hormuz and Bab-el-Mandeb.
• Expanding DP World's global portfolio of port operations and logistics hubs.
• Promoting the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC).
• Maintaining oil market stability and OPEC+ coordination.
• Securing technology and semiconductor supply chains for domestic industries.
• Acting as the primary global re-export hub via Jebel Ali Port.
• Balancing strategic ties between Western nations, China, and Russia.
• Defending critical offshore oil and maritime infrastructure.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Positions itself as a critical global logistics hub (Jebel Ali). Promotes the IMEC corridor and global port investments (DP World) to secure supply chains.
• Maritime Security: Supports freedom of navigation and UNCLOS. Participates in regional coalitions but balances involvement to avoid regional escalation.
• Free Trade: Strong advocate for open free trade, signing Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements (CEPA) globally.
• Sanctions: Does not enforce unilateral Western sanctions. Maintains commercial and oil market coordination under OPEC+.
• International Cooperation: Coordinates via GCC, OPEC, Arab League, BRICS, and UN.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Strait of Hormuz safety and regional maritime navigation.
• Development of the IMEC trade corridor linking India to Europe.
• Global port operations and logistics management by DP World.
• Jebel Ali Port expansion and digital customs automation.
• Balancing G7 sanctions compliance with local free zone business models.
• Security of the Bab-el-Mandeb and Gulf of Aden shipping lanes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Jebel Ali is the largest and most modern port in the Middle East.
✓ DP World operates over 80 marine and inland terminals globally.
✓ Massive financial reserves and sovereign wealth funds (ADIA).
✓ Strategic position at the crossroads of East and West trade.
✓ Strong cybersecurity defense systems protecting transport networks.
✓ Active member of BRICS and GCC, offering high diplomatic leverage.
✓ Close technology partnership with India and China.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High dependency on imported food and water resources.
✗ Direct strategic exposure to Strait of Hormuz security shocks.
✗ Geopolitical pressure from Western allies to comply with Russian sanctions.
✗ Vulnerability of oil terminals to regional drone/missile attacks.
✗ High dependency on foreign specialized labor in the logistics sector.
✗ Tension between US security ties and OPEC+ Russia alignment.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Jebel Ali Port and DP World support global trade efficiency.
• UAE trade policies are open, non-discriminatory, and comply with WTO rules.
• Gulf security requires regional coordination and dialogue, not confrontation.
• OPEC+ decisions are based on oil market stability, not geopolitics.
• The UAE coordinates with international partners to secure shipping lanes.
• National cybersecurity strategies protect transport networks from sabotage.
• The UAE seeks peaceful resolutions to regional maritime disputes.
• Undersea cable security is a vital national security interest.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does the UAE prevent its free zones from being used to bypass Russian oil sanctions?
Q2. What measures protect DP World's global ports from regional security escalations?
Q3. How does the UAE balance its US alliance with its participation in OPEC+ and BRICS?
Q4. Does the UAE's port expansion in East Africa raise security concerns?
Q5. How does the UAE address the environmental impact of port construction?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Iran: How can we cooperate to ensure the safety of transit through the Strait of Hormuz?
• India: How can our CEPA agreement strengthen semiconductor supply chain resilience?
• USA: How will the US support partners in securing regional trade routes?
• Egypt: How can we coordinate to secure Red Sea commercial transit routes?
• Russia: What steps are being taken to stabilize oil supply lines during conflicts?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Resolutions that restrict the operation of DP World ports globally.
• Unilateral maritime restrictions that disrupt free zone trade.
• Any challenge to OPEC+ autonomy in energy pricing.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: Saudi Arabia, GCC members, Egypt, India, G20 states, China.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Iran (preserve diplomatic normalization).
• Possible compromises: Support international third-party de-mining in trade zones.
• Main objectives: Promote IMEC, protect DP World assets, resist unilateral sanctions.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high professionalism, focusing on logistics and trade growth.
✓ Highlight the global footprint of DP World as a contributor to trade efficiency.
✓ Frame the UAE as a neutral, modern logistics bridge between East and West.
✓ Support OPEC+ and G20 decisions, framing them as market-stabilizing.
✓ Deflect sanctions queries by emphasizing compliance with UN-mandated rules.
