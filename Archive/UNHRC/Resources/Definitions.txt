This document provides clear definitions of key terms to prevent semantic disputes during debate and resolution drafting.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. SURVEILLANCE & COMMUNICATION TERMS
----------------------------------------------------------------

• Metadata: Data that provides information about other data. In telecommunications, this includes numbers dialed, duration of calls, location coordinates, IP addresses, and timestamps, but excludes the actual voice/text content.
• Mass Surveillance: The systematic monitoring of a population or large group of citizens without individual, prior judicial warrants or reasonable suspicion.
• Targeted Interception: The focused monitoring of specific communications belonging to identified individuals suspected of serious crimes or security threats, based on legal authorization.
• End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): A system of communication where only the communicating users can read the messages. The service provider or state agencies cannot access the decryption keys.
• Backdoor: A method, often secret, of bypassing normal authentication or encryption checks in a software system or device.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. AI & BIOMETRIC TERMS
----------------------------------------------------------------

• Biometrics: Unique physical or behavioral characteristics of an individual (e.g., face, fingerprint, voice, iris) that can be processed electronically for identity verification.
• Facial Recognition Technology (FRT): Algorithmic software designed to map and identify human facial features from images or video streams.
• Algorithmic Profiling: The automated processing of personal data to analyze or predict an individual's behavior, preferences, location, or reliability.
• Data Minimization: A privacy principle requiring that data collection be limited strictly to what is necessary for a specific, declared transaction or service.
• Deep Synthesis (Deepfakes): The use of artificial intelligence to generate realistic but synthetic audio, video, or image content.
