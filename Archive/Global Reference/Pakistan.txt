ISLAMIC REPUBLIC OF PAKISTAN

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Pakistan is a nuclear-armed South Asian power with significant strategic importance due to its location at the crossroads of South Asia, Central Asia, and the Middle East. It maintains a complex relationship with neighbouring India and Afghanistan and is a key player in regional security dynamics.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Islamabad
Largest City: Karachi
Official Name: Islamic Republic of Pakistan
Government Type: Federal parliamentary Islamic republic
Head of State: President Asif Ali Zardari
Head of Government: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif
Official Languages: Urdu (national), English (official)
Population: ~245 million
Area: 881,913 km² (33rd largest)
Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR)
GDP (Nominal): ~$375 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$1,550

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Textiles, agriculture, cement, steel, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, IT services, leather goods
Major Exports: Textiles (garments, cotton yarn), rice, leather, sports goods, surgical instruments, chemicals
Major Imports: Crude oil, petroleum products, machinery, plastics, edible oil, chemicals, iron and steel, electronics
Major Trade Partners: China, UAE, USA, Saudi Arabia, UK, Germany, Afghanistan, Kuwait

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Sixth-largest military globally (~650,000 active personnel). Significant nuclear arsenal. Extensive experience in counter-terrorism operations. Major defence industrial base.
Nuclear Weapons Status: De facto nuclear weapon state (not NPT signatory). ~170 warheads — fastest-growing nuclear arsenal globally.
UN Membership: Founding member (1947)
UNSC Status: Non-permanent member (most recently 2025-26)
Major International Organizations: UN, OIC, SCO, SAARC, Commonwealth, G20 (not a member), IMF, World Bank, ADB

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Major power in South Asia; significant influence in Afghanistan; key player in Islamic world; bridge to Central Asia
Neighbouring Countries: India (east), Afghanistan (west), Iran (southwest), China (northeast)
Strategic Importance: Controls key supply routes to Afghanistan; nuclear-armed state; Gwadar Port (CPEC hub); proximity to Persian Gulf
Important Maritime Routes: Arabian Sea, Strait of Hormuz (via Gulf), Gwadar deep-water port
Important Geography: Indus River system (agricultural backbone), Karakoram Highway (to China), Balochistan plateau, Khyber Pass (historical invasion route)

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Limited domestic energy — significant hydro, natural gas, coal (Thar); major energy importer; developing nuclear power
Natural Resources: Natural gas, coal, copper, gold (Reko Diq), salt, limestone, gemstones, iron ore
Technology Sector: Growing IT services sector; software exports; mobile penetration high; emerging startup ecosystem
Major Companies: Oil & Gas Development Corp, Pakistan State Oil, Habib Bank, MCB Bank, Engro, Lucky Cement, Pakistan Telecommunications

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: ~62% literacy; significant gender disparity in rural areas; growing university enrollment
Internet Penetration: ~35% of population (~90 million users)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.540 (Low, ranked 164th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 27/100 (ranked 140th)
Press Freedom Status: Mixed — ranked ~150th globally; harassment and censorship of journalists reported
Human Rights Overview: Constitutional protections but concerns over religious freedom (blasphemy laws), minority rights, women's rights, and restrictions on civil society

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Economic stabilization, IMF program compliance, counter-terrorism, Afghanistan engagement, strengthening CPEC, improving relations with Gulf states
Foreign Policy Overview: Historically US-aligned during Cold War; shifting towards China as primary strategic partner (CPEC); complex relations with Afghanistan; rivals India; maintains nuclear deterrence focus
Relations With:
  USA: Fluctuating — security cooperation disrupted by terrorism concerns; limited trust; IMF and aid dependent
  China: All-weather ally — CPEC ($62 billion investment), military cooperation, strategic convergence against India
  Russia: Gradually warming — defence and energy cooperation; traditional ties with India complicate relationship
  India: Hostile — Kashmir dispute, cross-border terrorism allegations, nuclear rivalry, limited people-to-people contact
  Pakistan: —
  EU: Trade and development partnership; GSP+ status; political pressure on democracy and human rights
  Middle East: Strong ties with Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar; diaspora remittances; growing engagement with Iran
  Neighbouring Countries: Strained relations with India and Afghanistan (Taliban); strong ties with China; managed border with Iran

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: China (all-weather ally), Saudi Arabia, UAE, Turkey, Malaysia
Major Rivals: India (primary rival), Afghanistan (cross-border tensions)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Kashmir issue resolution, nuclear deterrence, economic security, energy security, counter-terrorism, Afghanistan stability, CPEC completion, water security
Current Major Challenges: Economic crisis (inflation, debt); political instability; IMF program conditionality; climate vulnerability (2022 floods); water scarcity; terrorism resurgence; energy shortages

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Pakistan is the only nuclear-armed Muslim-majority country.
• The Karakoram Highway is the highest paved international road in the world.
• Pakistan has the second-largest salt mine in the world (Khewra Salt Mine).
• Pakistan's textile industry accounts for ~60% of total exports.
