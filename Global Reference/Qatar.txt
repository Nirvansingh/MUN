STATE OF QATAR

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Qatar is a small but immensely wealthy Gulf state with outsized diplomatic influence and the world's largest natural gas reserves. It has positioned itself as a key mediator in regional and international conflicts and a global hub for sports, education, and media.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Doha
Largest City: Doha
Official Name: State of Qatar
Government Type: Unitary Islamic absolute monarchy
Head of State: Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani
Head of Government: Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani
Official Languages: Arabic
Population: ~2.9 million (over 85% expatriates)
Area: 11,586 km²
Currency: Qatari Riyal (QAR)
GDP (Nominal): ~$230 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$80,000 (among highest globally)

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Natural gas (world's largest LNG exporter), oil, petrochemicals, construction, financial services, aviation (Qatar Airways), real estate, tourism (post-2022 World Cup)
Major Exports: Liquefied natural gas (LNG), crude oil, refined petroleum, fertilizers, plastics, polymers, steel
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, aircraft, electrical equipment, pharmaceuticals, food products, chemicals, iron and steel
Major Trade Partners: China, Japan, South Korea, India, UK, Germany, USA, UAE

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Small but well-funded military (~12,000 active personnel). Modern US-standard equipment. Hosts Al Udeid Air Base (US Central Command forward HQ). Expanding defence industry.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). No nuclear program.
UN Membership: 1971
UNSC Status: Non-permanent member (most recently 2022-23)
Major International Organizations: UN, GCC, Arab League, OIC, OPEC (left 2019), WTO, IMF, World Bank, G20 (not a member)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Mediation power — hosted talks on Afghanistan (Taliban political office), Gaza ceasefire, Lebanon, Iran-US, Sudan, Russia-Ukraine (prisoner swaps)
Neighbouring Countries: Saudi Arabia (only land border — south); maritime borders with UAE, Bahrain, Iran
Strategic Importance: World's largest LNG exporter; Al Udeid Air Base (US CENTCOM); global mediation hub; media influence (Al Jazeera); sovereign wealth fund (~$500+ billion)
Important Maritime Routes: Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz (export route)
Important Geography: Arabian Desert peninsula; flat terrain; extensive natural gas field (North Field/South Pars with Iran); limited fresh water (desalination dependent)

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: World's third-largest natural gas reserves (North Field — shared with Iran); largest LNG exporter; significant oil; massive solar energy investment
Natural Resources: Natural gas, oil, steel (raw materials), limestone
Technology Sector: Emerging — Qatar Science & Technology Park; AI and digital transformation investment; fintech hub aspirations
Major Companies: QatarEnergy (state), Qatargas, RasGas, Qatar Petroleum, Qatar Airways, Ooredoo, Qatar National Bank, Industries Qatar, Qatar Foundation

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High literacy (~97%); Education City (branch campuses of top global universities); large investment in research
Internet Penetration: ~99% of population (among highest globally)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.855 (Very High, ranked 40th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 63/100 (ranked 33rd)
Press Freedom Status: Not free — Al Jazeera independent in coverage but red lines on Qatari leadership; self-censorship expected
Human Rights Overview: Significant concerns — migrant worker conditions (kafala system, though reformed post-World Cup); no democratic representation; limited freedom of expression; women's rights improving but still restricted; LGBTQ+ criminalized

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Economic diversification (post-gas), mediation diplomacy, energy security (LNG expansion), global brand (sports, education), regional stability, investment overseas
Foreign Policy Overview: Activist mediation — hosts Taliban political office; brokers Hamas-Israel talks; mediates Iran-US communications; supports Muslim Brotherhood linked groups (contrary to Saudi/UAE); Al Jazeera as soft power tool
Relations With:
  USA: Major security partner — Al Udeid base (CENTCOM); strong economic ties; diplomatic ally despite differences on some issues
  China: Largest LNG customer; growing investment; Belt and Road engagement
  Russia: Pragmatic engagement — energy coordination (OPEC+); mediation role in Ukraine; not aligned with sanctions
  India: Strong energy partner (LNG); trade growing; diaspora (~800,000); food security cooperation
  Pakistan: Close ties — energy supply; diaspora; military cooperation; financial support
  EU: Major LNG supplier post-Ukraine war; growing energy partnership; human rights concerns
  Middle East: Complex — rival Saudi Arabia and UAE (blockade 2017-21, now reconciled); supports Gaza (Hamas political office); engages Iran; hosts Afghan Taliban; Turkey ally (military base in Qatar)
  Neighbouring Countries: Reconciliation with Saudi Arabia and UAE (2021 Al Ula summit); close Turkey ties; balancing Iran proximity

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: USA (security), Turkey (strategic), UK, France, India, Pakistan
Major Rivals: Saudi Arabia (historical), UAE (historical), Bahrain (historical), Houthis

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: LNG market dominance, economic diversification, mediation role, global soft power, Al Udeid base security, regional stability, investment returns
Current Major Challenges: Post-gas economic transition; regional isolation risk; climate change (extreme heat, water); Iran tensions; migrant worker reforms implementation; regional rivalry with UAE/Saudi

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Qatar has the world's highest GDP per capita (~$80,000).
• Qatar is the world's largest exporter of liquefied natural gas (LNG).
• Al Udeid Air Base is the largest US military base in the Middle East.
• Qatar hosted the 2022 FIFA World Cup, the first in the Middle East.
