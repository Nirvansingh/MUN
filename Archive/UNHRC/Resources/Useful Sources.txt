This document lists the most reliable sources and organizations to reference when looking for up-to-date data during committee.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTERGOVERNMENTAL BODIES
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR):
  - Website: https://www.ohchr.org
  - Key Publications: The Right to Privacy in the Digital Age Report series, Special Rapporteur on Privacy mandates.
• International Telecommunication Union (ITU):
  - Website: https://www.itu.int
  - Key Publications: Global Cybersecurity Index (GCI), Facts and Figures on digital connectivity.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. CIVIL SOCIETY & ADVOCACY GROUPS (NGOs)
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Privacy International (PI):
  - Website: https://privacyinternational.org
  - Focus: Leading NGO investigating state surveillance, data brokers, and biometric tracking globally.
• Access Now:
  - Website: https://www.accessnow.org
  - Focus: Tracks global internet shutdowns (KeepItOn coalition), digital rights violations, and encryption policy.
• Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF):
  - Website: https://www.eff.org
  - Focus: Leading US-based digital rights group focusing on surveillance self-defense and technological liberty.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. ACADEMIC & INDEPENDENT RESEARCH HUBS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• The Citizen Lab (Munk School of Global Affairs, University of Toronto):
  - Website: https://citizenlab.ca
  - Focus: Leading researchers exposing spyware deployments (Pegasus) and state-sponsored digital espionage.
• Center for Internet and Society (CIS):
  - Focus: Analyzes the intersection of technology, policy, and human rights in developing economies.
