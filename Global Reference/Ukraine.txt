UKRAINE

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Ukraine is a European country at the centre of the largest armed conflict in Europe since WWII, following Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022. It is a key security battleground between Russia and the West, with immense implications for European and global order.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Kyiv
Largest City: Kyiv
Official Name: Ukraine
Government Type: Unitary semi-presidential republic
Head of State: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy
Head of Government: Prime Minister Denys Shmyhal
Official Languages: Ukrainian
Population: ~38 million (pre-war: 44 million; large displacement)
Area: 603,628 km² (largest country entirely in Europe)
Currency: Ukrainian Hryvnia (UAH)
GDP (Nominal): ~$180 billion (2025, wartime)
GDP Per Capita: ~$4,800

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Agriculture (grain, sunflower oil), steel, mining, defence, IT services, energy transit, chemicals, machinery
Major Exports: Sunflower oil, grain (wheat, corn, barley), iron and steel, ores, machinery, IT services, honey
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, refined petroleum, natural gas, pharmaceuticals, electronics, plastics, clothing
Major Trade Partners: EU (Poland, Germany, Netherlands, Romania), China, Turkey, India, USA, Egypt

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Strong, battle-hardened military (~800,000+ active personnel, wartime). Large-scale NATO-standard equipment integration. Advanced drone warfare capabilities. Extensive combat experience.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). Relinquished nuclear weapons in 1994 (Budapest Memorandum).
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: Not on Council
Major International Organizations: UN, OSCE, Council of Europe, WTO, IMF, World Bank; candidate for: EU (since 2022), NATO (aspirant)

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Key European security actor; central to Russia-West confrontation; growing influence through grain exports and digital diplomacy
Neighbouring Countries: Russia (east/north), Belarus (north), Poland (west), Slovakia (west), Hungary (west), Romania (southwest), Moldova (southwest)
Strategic Importance: European energy transit hub; world's largest grain exporter (pre-war); buffer between Russia and NATO; key to European security order; war has global food and energy implications
Important Maritime Routes: Black Sea (blockaded by Russia), Sea of Azov, Danube River (grain export route)
Important Geography: Carpathian Mountains (west), Dnieper River (divides east-west), Donbas industrial region, Crimean peninsula (occupied), fertile steppes (world's best agricultural land)

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Significant natural gas reserves; coal (Donbas); growing renewable energy; hydroelectric; pre-war energy transit hub for Russian gas to Europe
Natural Resources: Iron ore, coal, manganese, natural gas, oil, salt, sulphur, graphite, titanium, timber, fertile agricultural land
Technology Sector: Growing — IT outsourcing hub (top 20 globally); defence tech (drones, electronic warfare); software development; startup ecosystem (Grammarly, GitLab roots)
Major Companies: Naftogaz (state energy), Metinvest (steel), Kernel (agriculture), DTEK (energy), EPAM Ukraine, SoftServe, Grammarly (Ukrainian-founded)

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: Very high — 99% literacy; strong STEM education tradition
Internet Penetration: ~85% of population (despite war)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.772 (High, ranked 87th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 33/100 (ranked 104th)
Press Freedom Status: Mixed — martial law restrictions but generally independent media; concerns over censorship during war
Human Rights Overview: War has caused massive violations — civilian casualties, torture in occupied territories, forced displacement, deportation of children to Russia; pre-war concerns over corruption and minority rights

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: War victory, territorial integrity restoration, NATO membership, EU accession, sanctions on Russia, international military and financial support
Foreign Policy Overview: Pro-Western integration; EU and NATO membership as strategic goals; defence against Russian aggression; global diplomatic campaign for support; food security diplomacy
Relations With:
  USA: Largest military and financial donor; strategic partnership; intelligence cooperation; bipartisan support (with some erosion)
  China: Complex — largest pre-war trade partner; concerns over China-Russia alignment; but limited direct hostility
  Russia: War — no diplomatic relations; full-scale invasion; occupied territories (Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson)
  India: Cautious — India maintains economic ties with Russia; limited military cooperation; Ukraine seeks Indian mediation and humanitarian support
  Pakistan: Limited engagement; Pakistan historically closer to Russia; Ukraine seeks support at UN
  EU: Candidate country (since 2022); massive financial and military support; progressive integration; solidarity from Eastern Europe
  Middle East: Engaging Gulf states (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar) for mediation and investment; Israel limited support; Iran arms Russia (drones)
  Neighbouring Countries: Strong support from Poland, Romania, Baltic states; Hungary and Slovakia more cautious; Moldova managing war spillover; Belarus complicit with Russia

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: USA, UK, Poland, EU, NATO members, Australia, Japan, Canada
Major Rivals: Russia (primary adversary), Belarus (complicit), Iran (arms supplier to Russia), North Korea (arms supplier to Russia)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: War victory, territorial integrity, NATO membership, EU integration, reconstruction, energy security, grain export routes, Western security guarantees
Current Major Challenges: War attrition; manpower and ammunition shortages; infrastructure destruction; economic survival; refugee return; corruption reform; NATO membership political barriers; Russian occupation

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Ukraine is the world's largest exporter of sunflower oil.
• Ukraine has the world's largest reserves of manganese ore.
• The Chernobyl nuclear disaster (1986) occurred in northern Ukraine.
• Ukraine is the second-largest country in Europe by area after Russia.
