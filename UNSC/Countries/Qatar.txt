================================================================
QATAR
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: State of Qatar
Capital: Doha
Government: Unitary Constitutional Monarchy
Current Leader: Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani
Population: ~2.7 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent Voice)
Major International Organizations: UN, GCC, OIC, Arab League, WTO, G77

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• United States (Major Non-NATO Ally)
• Turkey
• Iran (Pragmatic Relations)
• United Kingdom
• France
• Saudi Arabia
• United Arab Emirates
• GCC Member States

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• None (Maintains neutral mediator status)

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) export corridors in the Persian Gulf.
• Protecting shipping lanes through the Strait of Hormuz and Bab-el-Mandeb.
• Acting as a neutral mediator to resolve regional maritime and trade conflicts.
• Expanding global sovereign wealth investments through the Qatar Investment Authority (QIA).
• Promoting national food security through diversified supply chains.
• Developing Hamad Port as a major regional shipping and logistics hub.
• Supporting political stability in the Middle East to secure trade lanes.
• Defending critical offshore gas fields (North Field) from security threats.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Prioritizes securing global LNG supply chains. Promotes Hamad Port connectivity and alternative shipping routes.
• Maritime Security: Supports freedom of navigation. Promotes diplomatic dialogue to secure shipping lanes rather than military coalitions.
• Free Trade: Strong advocate for open energy trade and WTO compliance.
• Sanctions: Opposes unilateral economic sanctions, especially those affecting energy transit.
• International Cooperation: Coordinates via GCC, OIC, Arab League, and UN.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Global LNG supply chain security during regional conflicts.
• Mediation of Red Sea shipping tensions and regional conflicts.
• Expansion of Hamad Port and its trade connections.
• Safety of shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and Gulf of Aden.
• Energy transition and long-term gas supply contracts to Europe.
• Food security diversification and national logistics resilience.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ World's leading exporter of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG).
✓ Vast financial reserves and global investments (QIA).
✓ Trusted neutral mediator role, maintaining dialogue with all major actors.
✓ Modern port infrastructure (Hamad Port) with high efficiency.
✓ Strategic alliance with the US (Major Non-NATO Ally status).
✓ Close defense and economic cooperation with Turkey.
✓ Host to major international diplomatic forums.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High dependency on imported food and raw materials.
✗ Small military force, relying on foreign security guarantees.
✗ Geographic exposure to Strait of Hormuz shipping bottlenecks.
✗ Vulnerability of energy export terminals to regional escalations.
✗ Economic susceptibility to changes in global energy prices.
✗ Dependency on a single export commodity (gas/oil).

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Qatar's LNG exports are crucial for global energy stability and supply chains.
• Neutral mediation is the most effective tool to resolve maritime security disputes.
• Energy contracts are commercial agreements and must be protected from politics.
• Hamad Port operations comply with the highest international security standards.
• Qatar supports freedom of navigation and UNCLOS.
• Unilateral sanctions harm developing states and worsen global supply chains.
• Regional security must be built on dialogue and respect for sovereignty.
• Food security must be protected through unrestricted access to agricultural imports.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does Qatar ensure its financial systems are not used to support groups that disrupt maritime trade?
Q2. How does Qatar balance its US alliance with its close diplomatic ties with Iran?
Q3. What measures protect Qatar's offshore gas fields from regional conflict spillover?
Q4. Does Qatar's LNG diplomacy give it disproportionate leverage over European energy security?
Q5. How does Qatar address the environmental impact of its massive LNG extraction?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Iran: How can we ensure that Hormuz transit remains uninterrupted during tensions?
• USA: How can the US support diplomatic tracks to resolve Red Sea shipping issues?
• Germany: How can we strengthen long-term LNG supply contracts to secure your energy chain?
• Russia: What steps are being taken to guarantee agricultural fertilizer supplies to Global South markets?
• Egypt: How can we coordinate to secure Red Sea commercial transit routes?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• UN resolutions that criticize its mediator role or diplomatic tracks.
• Sanctions that target energy exports or transit channels.
• Unilateral military interventions in the Persian Gulf.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: Turkey, GCC members, United States, Western allies, G77 states.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Iran (preserve shared gas field cooperation).
• Possible compromises: Support international funding for maritime safety research.
• Main objectives: Secure LNG transit, promote diplomatic mediation, resist energy sanctions.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high diplomatic poise, highlighting Qatar's role as a mediator.
✓ Frame Qatar's LNG exports as a stabilizing force for global energy security.
✓ Focus on terms like "diplomatic dialogue," "neutral mediation," and "energy security."
✓ Highlight the efficiency and security of Hamad Port.
✓ Coordinate arguments closely with Turkey and GCC partners.
