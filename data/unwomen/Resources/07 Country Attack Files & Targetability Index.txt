DETAILED DOSSIERS ON THE 8 RED TARGETS
==================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------
1. UNITED STATES OF AMERICA (USA)
---------------------------------------------------------------
• Delegate: Saanj Kakadiya
• Core Weakness: Zero statutory federal paid leave; astronomical childcare costs; non-ratification of CEDAW and ILO C189.
• Attack Angle: Point out the hypocrisy of preaching women's empowerment globally while leaving American mothers to return to work within 14 days of childbirth.
• Devastating Fact: The US is the only industrialized country on earth with 0 weeks of statutory paid leave, and over 70,000 childcare centers face closure due to the "childcare cliff."
• Killer Quote for Speech: "The United States lectures this committee on economic freedom, yet denies its own mothers the basic freedom of paid maternity leave, forcing families to spend more on infant care than on university tuition."

---------------------------------------------------------------
2. JAPAN
---------------------------------------------------------------
• Delegate: Mannat Bhatiya
• Core Weakness: Lowest G7 gender equality ranking (#118); 54% of working women trapped in non-regular jobs; 41 minutes of male care time.
• Attack Angle: Japan's demographic aging crisis cannot be solved by artificial corporate slogans while men contribute less than an hour a day to domestic care and women are forced out of regular careers.
• Devastating Fact: Over 100,000 mid-career workers (mostly women) resign every year due to eldercare demands (Kaigo Risyoku).
• Killer Quote for Speech: "Womenomics in Japan has become a hollow slogan when over half of Japanese working women are relegated to non-regular employment with zero pension security, while fathers contribute just 41 minutes of domestic care."

---------------------------------------------------------------
3. SOUTH KOREA
---------------------------------------------------------------
• Delegate: Hiya Himanshu Mehta
• Core Weakness: World-record 0.72 fertility collapse; 31.1% gender wage gap (OECD worst for 27 years); proposal to pay Filipina caregivers below minimum wage.
• Attack Angle: Expose the attempt to import cheap foreign domestic labor to subsidize 60-hour corporate workweeks while shortchanging caregivers on basic wages.
• Devastating Fact: South Korea's fertility rate has crashed to 0.72 despite spending $280 billion on cash handouts, because money cannot fix a corporate culture that forces women to quit their jobs upon marriage.
• Killer Quote for Speech: "South Korea cannot solve its fertility collapse by attempting to import Filipino care workers at sub-minimum wages. Human care cannot be devalued to patch over broken corporate work cultures."

---------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAUDI ARABIA
---------------------------------------------------------------
• Delegate: Akshita Jindal
• Core Weakness: Exclusion of 1.5 million domestic workers from 2021 labor reforms; persistent exit-visa requirement; passport confiscation.
• Attack Angle: Saudi female labor participation surged by outsourcing domestic chores onto vulnerable foreign women trapped under sponsorship restrictions.
• Devastating Fact: Saudi Arabia assumed the Chair of the UN Commission on the Status of Women while denying 1.5 million domestic workers the right to leave their place of employment without an employer's exit permit.
• Killer Quote for Speech: "Empowering one woman by exploiting another is not gender progress. If Saudi Arabia wishes to lead the Commission on the Status of Women, it must abolish exit visas and guarantee domestic workers equal protection under the national labor code."
