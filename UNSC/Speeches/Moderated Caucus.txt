UNSC MODERATED CAUCUS TALKING POINTS
================================================================

Use these modular talking points to quickly prepare speeches for specific moderated caucuses in the Security Council.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TOPIC 1: RED SEA MARITIME SAFETY & COALITION patrols
----------------------------------------------------------------
Suggested Motion: "Motion for a moderated caucus of 10 minutes with a 30-second speaking time on the deployment of defensive coalitions in the Red Sea."

WESTERN MARITIME BLOC ARGUMENTS:
• Houthi missile and drone attacks on merchant shipping violate international law and represent an act of regional terrorism.
• Operations like Prosperity Guardian are purely defensive, necessary to protect civilian lives and keep global trade lanes open.
• The UNSC must coordinate targeted interdictions to halt the illicit flow of weapons to proxy groups.

SOVEREIGN BLOC ARGUMENTS:
• Western-led military coalitions in the Red Sea escalate regional conflicts and fail to address the root political causes.
• These patrols represent a unilateral attempt to establish naval hegemony in a strategic transit zone without UN authorization.
• True security can only be achieved through regional dialogue and respect for the territorial waters of coastal states.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TOPIC 2: REGULATING THE RUSSIAN SHADOW FLEET
----------------------------------------------------------------
Suggested Motion: "Motion for a moderated caucus of 12 minutes with a 45-second speaking time on the security risks of the shadow fleet."

SANCTIONS-ENFORCING BLOC ARGUMENTS:
• The Russian shadow fleet of older, un-insured, and poorly maintained tankers presents an imminent environmental risk of oil spills in busy transit zones.
• These vessels bypass G7 oil price caps using flags of convenience, undermining the rule of law and international accountability.
• We call on coastal states to deny transit access to tankers lacking verified commercial P&I insurance.

ANTI-SANCTIONS BLOC ARGUMENTS:
• Unilateral Western sanctions are the direct cause of the shift in shipping logistics and are illegal under WTO rules.
• Attempts to block Russian agricultural and energy exports threaten global energy stability and cause inflation in developing nations.
• Commercial operators have the right to select flags of convenience and insurance pools free from political coercion.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TOPIC 3: DEFENDING UNDERSEA CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Suggested Motion: "Motion for a moderated caucus of 15 minutes with a 45-second speaking time on protecting undersea cables and energy pipelines."

COOPERATIVE SECURITY ARGUMENTS:
• Undersea communication cables carry 95% of global internet traffic. Sabotage of these networks would cause global financial collapse.
• We call for a UN-led Undersea Cable Sentinel Network to share real-time sonar and satellite data on suspicious maritime vessel movements.
• Sovereign states must coordinate cybersecurity and physical defense protocols for critical energy pipelines in international waters.

SOVEREIGN BLOC ARGUMENTS:
• Infrastructure security remains the sole responsibility of the coastal state within its EEZ and territorial waters.
• We reject attempts by foreign military alliances to police our undersea assets under the guise of cooperative security.
• Independent, transparent UN-led investigations are needed to identify the state actors behind the Nord Stream pipeline sabotage.
