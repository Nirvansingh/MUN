================================================================
UNSC WORKING PAPER CLAUSES: MARITIME SECURITY & TRADE
================================================================

This document provides sample preambular and operative clauses for use during the initial drafting phase of working papers in the Security Council.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SAMPLE PREAMBULAR CLAUSES (Context and framing)
----------------------------------------------------------------

• Reaffirming that freedom of navigation, as established under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS), is essential for global trade and international security,

• Deeply concerned by the escalating security threats in the Gulf of Aden, Bab-el-Mandeb, and Red Sea transit corridors, which disrupt global supply chains and force ships to route around the Cape of Good Hope,

• Acknowledging the severe economic impact of shipping disruptions on transit revenue-dependent states, specifically noting the loss of revenue for the Suez Canal Authority,

• Emphasizing the vulnerability of critical maritime infrastructure, including undersea communication cables and offshore energy terminals, to sabotage and cyber interception,

• Recognizing that stable global food and fertilizer supply chains are critical to preventing humanitarian crises in import-dependent nations,

----------------------------------------------------------------
SAMPLE OPERATIVE CLAUSES (Action mandates)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Encourages member states to coordinate information sharing through regional maritime intelligence hubs to detect and prevent piracy, smuggling, and threats to merchant transit;

2. Recommends that coastal and transit states implement the IMO's International Ship and Port Facility Security (ISPS) Code to strengthen port cybersecurity defenses;

3. Calls upon states to participate in regional maritime search-and-rescue (SAR) exercises to build trust and improve disaster response capabilities along busy sea lanes;

4. Invites developing nations to seek financial and technical assistance from international development banks to modernize port infrastructure and cargo screening systems;

5. Encourages the expansion of grain transit corridors in the Black Sea and Danube regions through coordinated de-mining initiatives.
