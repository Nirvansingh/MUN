• Indigenous care disparities: Addressing the chronic underfunding of child welfare and family support on First Nations reserves
• Gender wage gap in the private sector (hovering around 11% to 13%) and persistent occupational segregation
• Shortage of trained eldercare workers in long-term care homes exposed during the COVID-19 pandemic

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Asserts that care infrastructure is essential economic infrastructure, as vital to national GDP growth as roads and bridges.
• Showcases the $30 billion 5-year federal investment in the Canada-Wide Early Learning and Child Care (ELCC) system, which has already halved childcare fees across all provinces and achieved $10-a-day childcare in several territories and provinces.
• Promotes the Feminist International Assistance Policy (FIAP), financing care economy initiatives and time-use research globally.
• Champions the Caregiver Immigration Pilots, granting direct Permanent Residency to qualified foreign caregivers upon arrival.
• Advocates for universal international reporting on the valuation of unpaid care and domestic labor in national GDP accounting.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Champion progressive multilateral standards on the care economy and gender equality on the international stage.
• Attract skilled international healthcare and early childhood care professionals to offset domestic demographic aging.
• Demonstrate the tangible economic return on investment of public childcare (every dollar invested generates $1.50 to $2.80 in broader economic returns).
• Maintain constructive bilateral ties with labor-sending partner states, particularly the Philippines.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ Historic $10-a-day Child Care Plan: Reduced out-of-pocket childcare fees by 50% or more nationwide, saving families thousands of dollars annually
✓ Record Female Labor Force Participation: Reached an all-time high of over 85% for core working-age women (aged 25-54) driven by affordable childcare
✓ Progressive Parental Leave: Up to 18 months of job-protected parental leave with dedicated "use-it-or-lose-it" bonus weeks for fathers/second parents
✓ Permanent Residency for Caregivers: Announced in June 2024 that caregivers will receive permanent resident status immediately upon arrival under new pilot programs
✓ Ratified ILO Convention No. 156 (Workers with Family Responsibilities) and signatory to all core gender equality conventions

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Childcare Space Deficit: The dramatic fee reduction to $10/day caused demand to skyrocket, but severe shortages of Early Childhood Educators (ECEs) due to low wages have left tens of thousands of families stuck on multi-year waiting lists
✗ The Long-Term Care Crisis: Severe staffing shortages in nursing homes; during the pandemic, military personnel had to be deployed into privatized eldercare facilities due to catastrophic staffing neglect
✗ Backlog in Caregiver Applications: Thousands of migrant caregivers endured years of processing delays, prolonged family separation, and tied-visa vulnerabilities under predecessor programs
✗ Persistent Private Gender Wage Gap: Women working full-time earn approximately 88 cents for every dollar men earn, with Indigenous and racialized women experiencing wider gaps
✗ Indigenous Child Welfare Inequalities: First Nations children remain grossly overrepresented in provincial foster care systems due to structural underinvestment in community family care

UNPAID CARE WORK SITUATION
--------------------------
• Canadian women spend an average of 3.8 hours daily on unpaid domestic work and caregiving, compared to 2.4 hours for men.
• More than 8 million Canadians provide informal care to an adult family member or child with long-term healthcare needs.

CHILDCARE & ELDERCARE INFRASTRUCTURE
------------------------------------
• The $10-a-day ELCC rollout is a historic breakthrough, but retention of ECE workers is challenged by wages averaging less than $20/hour in some provinces.
• Long-term eldercare is governed provincially, with significant quality variations between public and for-profit private operators.

DOMESTIC & MIGRANT WORKER POLICIES
----------------------------------
• Deep historical partnership with the Philippines: Hundreds of thousands of Filipino-Canadians trace their origins to caregiver migration programs.
• Significant progress: Canada eliminated the predatory "live-in" requirement of the old LCP and recently committed to granting Permanent Residency upon arrival.

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• $10 / Day: National target for average licensed childcare fees across Canada.
• 85.7%: All-time record high labor force participation for Canadian women aged 25 to 54.
• $30 Billion: Federal investment allocated to universal early learning and childcare over 5 years.
• June 2024: Date Canada announced direct Permanent Residency upon arrival for incoming qualified home care workers.

IMPLEMENTATION GAPS & HYPOCRISY
-------------------------------
• Celebrates affordable $10/day childcare in political speeches while thousands of working mothers cannot actually access a space due to severe nursery shortages.
• Touts generous parental leave while weekly Employment Insurance (EI) payment caps (~$668/week) make it financially difficult for lower-income families to take extended leave.
• Praises foreign caregivers as "heroes" while legacy immigration files faced multi-year bureaucratic processing delays.

BEST POINTS OF INFORMATION (POIs) TO ASK CANADA
-----------------------------------------------
1. Space Shortage & Wage POI: "While Canada's $10-a-day childcare policy is laudable, how does Canada plan to solve the acute shortage of tens of thousands of Early Childhood Educators who earn low wages, leaving families languishing on years-long waiting lists?"
2. Caregiver Family Reunification POI: "Canada has announced direct Permanent Residency for incoming caregivers, but what immediate concrete measures are being taken to clear the remaining backlog of foreign caregivers who have suffered years of family separation under previous programs?"
