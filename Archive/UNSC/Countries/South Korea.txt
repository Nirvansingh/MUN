NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing maritime shipping lanes vital for its export-oriented economy.
• Maintaining advanced semiconductor and battery supply chain dominance.
• Protecting key industrial exports (automotive, electronics, shipbuilding).
• Defending against North Korean maritime provocations and missile tests.
• Securing energy corridors from the Middle East through the Indian Ocean.
• Promoting rules-based trade and WTO dispute settlement functions.
• Protecting critical port networks and industrial databases from cyber attacks.
• Strengthening technology cooperation with G7 and Indo-Pacific partners.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Key player in high-tech supply chains (semiconductors, batteries). Promotes de-risking while maintaining pragmatic economic ties with China.
• Maritime Security: Strongly supports freedom of navigation and UNCLOS. Deploys naval units (Cheonghae Unit) in counter-piracy.
• Free Trade: Strong advocate for bilateral and multilateral FTAs (RCEP, KORUS, WTO).
• Sanctions: Coordinates with US/G7 on Russian export controls and enforces sanctions against North Korea.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via G20, APEC, WTO, IMO, and UN to protect shipping.

• Shipping safety in the East and South China Seas.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ World-leading semiconductor, electronics, and battery manufacturer.
✓ World-dominant commercial shipbuilding industry, constructing advanced carriers.
✓ Highly capable, modern navy with advanced destroyer fleets.
✓ Broad network of free trade agreements covering major global markets.
✓ Top-tier cyber defense capacity protecting industrial sectors.
✓ Strong strategic alliance with the United States.
✓ Leading role in APEC and G20 technology tracks.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Extreme dependency on imported energy and raw materials.
✗ Direct geopolitical threat from North Korea, requiring high defense focus.
✗ Economic vulnerability to trade disputes between the US and China.
✗ Shrinking domestic workforce due to historically low birth rates.
✗ Dependency on key rare earths imported from China.
✗ Regional maritime boundaries remain contested.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Technology alliances are defensive and protect supply chain stability.
• South Korea's naval deployments protect global commercial routes from piracy.
• Digital export controls are necessary to prevent military diversion of technology.
• Port security measures comply with international guidelines.
• Stability in the Taiwan Strait is vital for South Korean trade transit.
• South Korea seeks peaceful, denuclearized stability in the Korean Peninsula.
• Trade agreements promote mutual economic benefit and WTO compliance.
• Industrial cyber security is a prerequisite for safe global trade.

RED LINES

• Resolutions that restrict technology exports without security reasons.
• Any framework that permits North Korean maritime provocations.
• Restrictions on naval access to international transit lanes.
