CASE STUDY 1: THE RED SEA HOUTHI ATTACKS (2023-PRESENT)
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Following geopolitical conflict in Gaza, Yemeni Houthi forces launched drone and missile attacks on commercial vessels transiting the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, declaring a blockade on Israel-bound shipping.
• Impact:
  - Forced major shipping firms (Maersk, MSC) to divert ships around Africa.
  - Caused a 40% reduction in Suez Canal transit revenues, severely affecting Egypt's economy.
  - Led to the establishment of US-led Operation Prosperity Guardian and EU Operation Aspides.
• Debate Use: Cite to argue for international task forces to protect trade lanes and coordinate navigation security.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: NORD STREAM PIPELINE SABOTAGE (2022)
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: Underwater explosions ruptured three of the four Nord Stream natural gas pipelines in the Baltic Sea within Denmark and Sweden's EEZ, releasing massive amounts of methane.
• Impact:
  - Destroyed direct gas delivery routes from Russia to Germany, forcing a rapid energy supply chain shift to LNG imports.
  - Highlighted the extreme vulnerability of undersea critical energy and data infrastructure.
• Debate Use: Cite to demand coordinated UN-led protection for undersea cables and pipelines in international waters.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: THE BLACK SEA GRAIN INITIATIVE (2022-2023)
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: A UN and Turkey-brokered agreement between Russia and Ukraine to permit the safe transit of agricultural exports from three Ukrainian Black Sea ports.
• Impact:
  - Allowed over 32 million metric tons of grain to reach global markets, reducing global food prices by 20%.
  - Terminated by Russia in July 2023, who cited obstacles to its own agricultural and fertilizer exports.
• Debate Use: Cite to prove that neutral mediation and UN-brokered trade corridors are effective alternatives to military escorts.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: THE SUEZ CANAL BLOCKAGE (2021)
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Description: The ultra-large container ship Ever Given ran aground and blocked the Suez Canal for six days, halting over 400 ships.
• Impact:
  - Blocked an estimated $9.6 billion worth of trade daily.
  - Demonstrated the high risk of single choke point dependencies.
• Debate Use: Cite when arguing for trade corridor diversification and investment in alternate routes (such as Central Asian rail links).
