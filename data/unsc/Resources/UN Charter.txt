This document reviews how the Articles of the United Nations Charter apply to naval deployments, self-defense at sea, and trade sanctions.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. ARTICLE 2(4) - PROHIBITION OF THE USE OF FORCE
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Text Summary: Member states must refrain from the threat or use of force against the territorial integrity or political independence of any state.
• Maritime Application:
  - Applicable to military blockades, firing upon civilian vessels, or unauthorized naval incursions in territorial waters.
  - Reaffirms that international sea lanes must remain corridors of peace.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. ARTICLE 39 - DETERMINATION OF THREATS TO PEACE
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Text Summary: The Security Council determines the existence of any threat to the peace, breach of the peace, or act of aggression and recommends or decides measures to restore order.
• Maritime Application:
  - The Council has declared systematic piracy (Somalia) and attacks on shipping (Red Sea) as breaches of international peace, triggering Security Council resolutions.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. ARTICLE 41 - NON-MILITARY SANCTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Text Summary: Authorizes the Security Council to decide on measures not involving the use of armed force, including complete or partial interruption of economic relations and communications.
• Maritime Application:
  - Formulates the legal basis for UNSC-mandated trade embargoes, cargo restrictions, and financial transaction blocks.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. ARTICLE 42 - MILITARY ENFORCEMENT MEASURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Text Summary: Should non-military measures prove inadequate, the Security Council may take such action by air, sea, or land forces as may be necessary to maintain or restore peace.
• Maritime Application:
  - Provides the legal basis for UN-authorized naval blockades or military interventions to enforce embargoes (e.g., historical Gulf War interceptions).

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. ARTICLE 51 - INHERENT RIGHT OF SELF-DEFENSE
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Text Summary: Nothing in the Charter shall impair the inherent right of individual or collective self-defense if an armed attack occurs against a member state.
• Maritime Application:
  - Cited by states to justify firing upon hostile drones/missiles targeting commercial vessels (Operation Prosperity Guardian).
  - Subject of intense debate over whether "armed attacks" can apply to non-military commercial shipping.
