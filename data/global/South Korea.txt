REPUBLIC OF KOREA (SOUTH KOREA)

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
South Korea is a high-tech democracy and the world's 12th-largest economy, a key US ally, and a cultural powerhouse. It remains technically at war with North Korea and is central to East Asian security dynamics.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Seoul
Largest City: Seoul
Official Name: Republic of Korea
Government Type: Unitary presidential constitutional republic
Head of State: President Yoon Suk Yeol
Head of Government: Prime Minister Han Duck-soo
Official Languages: Korean
Population: ~52 million
Area: 100,210 km²
Currency: South Korean Won (KRW)
GDP (Nominal): ~$1.9 trillion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$36,000

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Semiconductors, electronics, automotive, shipbuilding, steel, petrochemicals, machinery, entertainment (K-pop, film), cosmetics
Major Exports: Semiconductors, vehicles, machinery, refined petroleum, ships, steel, plastics, cosmetics, entertainment
Major Imports: Crude oil, natural gas, electronics, machinery, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, food products, optical equipment
Major Trade Partners: China, USA, Vietnam, Japan, Australia, Saudi Arabia, Germany, Indonesia

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Highly modern military (~555,000 active personnel). Powerful navy and air force. US Forces Korea (28,500 troops) stationed under Mutual Defense Treaty. Mandatory conscription. Advanced cyber warfare capabilities.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). Under US nuclear umbrella. Growing domestic debate on nuclear weapons.
UN Membership: 1991 (simultaneous with DPRK)
UNSC Status: Non-permanent member (most recently 2024-25)
Major International Organizations: UN, OECD, G20, WTO, APEC, ASEAN+3, MIKTA, IMF, World Bank, IPEF

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Major economic power in East Asia; middle power diplomacy (global pivot state); active in ASEAN, Pacific Islands, and UN peacekeeping
Neighbouring Countries: North Korea (DMZ border — only land border)
Strategic Importance: US alliance linchpin in East Asia; front line of North Korea deterrence; global technology and supply chain hub; world-class semiconductor industry
Important Maritime Routes: Sea of Japan (East Sea), Yellow Sea, Korea Strait, South China Sea (trade route)
Important Geography: Korean Peninsula (southern half); mountainous terrain (70%); heavily fortified DMZ; Busan port

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Very limited domestic energy — world's second-largest LNG importer; significant nuclear power capacity (24 reactors); growing renewables
Natural Resources: Limited — some coal, tungsten, graphite, molybdenum; heavily dependent on imports for minerals
Technology Sector: World leader — semiconductors (Samsung, SK Hynix), electronics, 5G/6G, AI, robotics, shipbuilding, display technology
Major Companies: Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix, Hyundai Motor, LG Electronics, POSCO, Kia, Naver, Kakao, Korean Air, Celltrion

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: Very high — 99% literacy; world-class education system; highest tertiary education rate in OECD (~70%)
Internet Penetration: ~98% of population (fastest internet speeds globally)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.925 (Very High, ranked 23rd)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 62/100 (ranked 35th)
Press Freedom Status: Mostly free — ranked ~47th globally; concerns about political pressure on media
Human Rights Overview: Strong democratic institutions; concerns over gender equality, working conditions, LGBTQ+ rights, and freedom of expression (National Security Law restrictions)

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: North Korea deterrence, US alliance strengthening, supply chain security, technology competitiveness, Indo-Pacific engagement, demographic crisis response
Foreign Policy Overview: US alliance cornerstone; Japan partnership (despite historical tensions); China economic interdependence; active middle power diplomacy; global pivot state
Relations With:
  USA: Core ally — Mutual Defense Treaty; USFK stationed; nuclear umbrella; technology and supply chain alliance
  China: Largest trading partner but strategic competitor; THAAD tensions; supply chain decoupling pressure
  Russia: Strained — sanctions over Ukraine; North Korea arms cooperation concern; limited trade
  India: Growing strategic partnership; defence cooperation; Indo-Pacific alignment; semiconductor supply chain collaboration
  Pakistan: Limited engagement; development assistance focus
  EU: Strategic partnership; trade and technology agreement; cooperation on Indo-Pacific
  Middle East: Energy dependence on Gulf; engaging with UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel (defence, technology)
  Neighbouring Countries: Hostile towards North Korea; improving Japan relations (2023 summit); balancing China

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: USA (core ally), Japan (security coordination), UK, Australia, NATO partners
Major Rivals: North Korea (primary adversary), China (strategic competitor)

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: North Korea deterrence, US alliance, technology leadership, export-driven economy, semiconductor supply chain dominance, Indo-Pacific stability
Current Major Challenges: Demographic crisis (world's lowest birth rate ~0.72); North Korean nuclear threat; China economic dependence vs. security concerns; housing costs; youth unemployment; technology competition

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• South Korea has the world's fastest average internet speed.
• South Korea's birth rate is the lowest in the world (~0.72 children per woman).
• Samsung Electronics is the world's largest semiconductor manufacturer.
• The DMZ is one of the most heavily fortified borders on Earth.
