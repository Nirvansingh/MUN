================================================================
INDIA
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: Republic of India
Capital: New Delhi
Government: Federal Parliamentary Democratic Republic
Current Leader: Prime Minister Narendra Modi
Population: ~1.43 Billion
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent Member)
Major International Organizations: UN, BRICS, SCO, Quad, G20, IORA, WTO, IMO

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• United States
• France
• Japan
• South Korea
• Russia (Strategic/Energy Partner)
• United Arab Emirates
• Egypt
• Australia

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• China
• Pakistan

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing trade and energy routes across the Indian Ocean Region (IOR).
• Countering China's maritime encirclement strategy ("String of Pearls").
• Acting as the "Net Security Provider" in the Indian Ocean.
• Safeguarding key transit choke points (Strait of Malacca, Bab-el-Mandeb).
• Diversifying manufacturing supply chains through the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme.
• Securing commercial access to landlocked Central Asia via Chabahar Port (Iran).
• Maintaining pragmatic energy imports from Russia despite Western sanctions.
• Fighting maritime piracy and narcotics trafficking in the Arabian Sea.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Focuses on "Atmanirbhar Bharat" (Self-Reliant India) and joining resilient supply chain initiatives (e.g., Supply Chain Resilience Initiative - SCRI with Japan and Australia).
• Maritime Security: Promotes "SAGAR" (Security and Growth for All in the Region). Deploys naval assets for counter-piracy and merchant vessel protection in the Arabian Sea.
• Free Trade: Supports open, fair, and inclusive trade. Emphasizes WTO reforms that protect the agricultural and food security interests of developing nations.
• Sanctions: Rejects unilateral economic sanctions not authorized by the UN. Continues purchasing energy from Russia to protect domestic consumer markets.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via G20, BRICS, Quad, and the Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA).

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Chinese naval and research vessel presence in the Indian Ocean.
• Indian Navy counter-piracy operations and escorting of merchant ships in the Gulf of Aden.
• Strategic expansion of Chabahar Port to bypass Pakistan for Central Asian trade.
• Participation in the Quad and its impact on regional maritime security.
• Building domestic semiconductor manufacturing to secure technology supply lines.
• Balancing G7 energy price cap compliance with national import needs.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Strategic central position commanding major Indian Ocean sea lanes.
✓ Modern, expanding blue-water navy with multiple aircraft carrier groups.
✓ Major global exporter of pharmaceuticals, refined petroleum, and IT services.
✓ Strong diplomatic relationships with both Western powers and Russia.
✓ Leading role in IORA and regional maritime security coordination.
✓ Rapidly developing port infrastructure and digital customs clearance.
✓ Self-sufficiency in food grains, protecting against global supply shocks.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High dependency on imported crude oil (over 80% of consumption).
✗ Infrastructure bottlenecks in domestic shipping and logistics networks.
✗ Geopolitical pressure from Western allies regarding energy trade with Russia.
✗ Border conflicts with Pakistan and China, diverting military resources.
✗ Dependency on China for active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and electronics.
✗ Lower port container turnaround times compared to East Asian hubs.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Energy purchases from Russia are necessary to shield Indian consumers from inflation.
• SAGAR is an inclusive security model that respects the sovereignty of all IOR states.
• India's naval patrols in the Arabian Sea are defensive and counter-piracy focused.
• Trade regulations are targeted to protect vulnerable domestic farmers and small businesses.
• Quad cooperation is focused on humanitarian relief, disaster response, and maritime safety.
• Chabahar Port development provides peaceful trade corridors for landlocked states.
• India opposes the weaponization of international payment networks.
• Regional disputes must be resolved through bilateral dialogue, not force.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does India justify importing Russian oil while co-sponsoring global peace declarations?
Q2. Why is India expanding its naval Quad cooperation if it claims to remain non-aligned?
Q3. What measures protect Indian Ocean maritime networks from local pollution and spills?
Q4. How do India's domestic import tariffs align with its advocacy for global free trade?
Q5. What steps prevent bilateral disputes with Pakistan from halting regional trade corridors?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• China: How does China ensure its naval research vessels in the IOR respect local EEZs?
• USA: How can the US support technology transfer to help developing nations secure their shipping?
• Russia: What steps are being taken to guarantee agricultural fertilizer supplies to Global South markets?
• Pakistan: What measures will Pakistan take to secure maritime lanes in the Arabian Sea?
• Egypt: How can we deepen partnerships to protect Suez Canal shipping?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Resolutions that restrict sovereign states from choosing their trade and energy partners.
• Any UN mandate that permits foreign military presence in India's Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ).
• Binding environmental trade restrictions that penalize developing nations.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: US, France, Japan, Australia, UAE, Egypt, IORA states.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Russia, China (focus on maritime safety coordination).
• Possible compromises: Support shared logistics database standards for counter-piracy.
• Main objectives: Secure the Indian Ocean, defend strategic autonomy in trade, promote technology transfer.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Emphasize India's role as the voice of the Global South and a responsible regional leader.
✓ Use SAGAR as your primary talking point for maritime security.
✓ Confidently defend energy purchases by framing them as domestic economic survival.
✓ Highlight India's active naval contributions to protecting merchant shipping.
✓ Refer to Quad as a developmental and safety forum, not a military bloc.
