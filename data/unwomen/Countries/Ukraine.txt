AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE
==================================================

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: YELLOW (Wartime Care Economy & Resilience Model)
Delegate: Mishti Gupta

Why?
• Acute armed conflict context: Ongoing defense against full-scale Russian military aggression has transformed the national care economy into a frontline of survival
• Complete displacement of families: Over 6 million Ukrainian refugees abroad (predominantly women and children) and millions internally displaced (IDPs)
• Solo Caregiving Burden: With millions of men mobilized on the frontline, Ukrainian women bear 100% of childcare, eldercare, and community survival duties
• The Veteran Care Crisis: Tens of thousands of severely wounded soldiers and amputees returning from the front, with the long-term rehabilitation burden falling almost entirely on female family members
• Destruction of Care Infrastructure: Over 1,500 healthcare facilities and hundreds of kindergartens damaged or destroyed by Russian missile strikes; rolling power blackouts make basic cooking, heating, and hygiene care grueling

Hot Topics
• Gendered impact of wartime displacement: Female-headed households managing care under constant aerial bombardment
• Reconstruction of care infrastructure: Building modern, bomb-sheltered kindergartens and inclusive community rehabilitation centers
• Mental health and psychological trauma care for children, displaced mothers, and war widows
• European Union accession reforms: Aligning Ukrainian labor law with EU Work-Life Balance directives despite martial law
• Pre-war reliance on Ukrainian women as domestic caregivers in Italy, Poland, and Western Europe

Expected Debate Frequency: High / Respected Resilience State

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Highlights the extraordinary resilience of Ukrainian women who keep the civilian economy, schools, hospitals, and households running while men defend the frontlines.
• Asserts that care infrastructure must be recognized as essential national security infrastructure: Rebuilding bomb-sheltered kindergartens is necessary to enable displaced mothers to work.
• Promotes post-war modernization: In 2021, Ukraine enacted Law No. 1401-IX granting equal rights to fathers to take 14 days of paid paternity leave and share parental leave.
• Demands international accountability and reparations for the systematic destruction of maternity wards, pediatric hospitals, and power grids by Russian forces.
• Calls for international donor funding for community-based mental health and veteran rehabilitation centers to relieve the unpaid care burden on war widows and families.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Secure international financial and technical grants to rebuild destroyed schools, daycares, and pediatric clinics with modern bomb shelters.
• Facilitate the return of Ukrainian refugee mothers and children from abroad by ensuring safe, accessible childcare and employment.
• Harmonize national labor and gender equality standards with European Union directives to advance EU membership.
• Establish state-funded veteran rehabilitation systems so that wounded soldiers do not become an uncompensated lifetime burden on female relatives.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ Extraordinary female civic mobilization: Women lead volunteer logistics, community care networks, and municipal civil defense
✓ 2021 Parental Leave Reform: Codified equal rights for fathers to take 14 days of fully paid paternity leave and share parental leave up to age 3
✓ First Lady Olena Zelenska's National Mental Health Program ("How Are You?"): Providing community trauma support to millions of mothers and children
✓ Accelerating EU labor harmonization: Eliminating historical Soviet-era protective restrictions on female employment
✓ 126 days of statutory fully paid maternity leave (100% wage replacement via the Social Insurance Fund)

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Catastrophic Physical Destruction: Thousands of nurseries, schools, and hospitals destroyed, leaving mothers without safe daytime care
✗ The Veteran Care Burden: Lack of formal state long-term care facilities forces wives and mothers into full-time, unpaid medical nursing for wounded veterans
✗ Massive Demographic Collapse: Fertility rate dropped below 1.0; millions of young mothers and children living abroad as refugees
✗ Infrastructure Collapse: Destruction of power plants forces mothers to manage child and elderly care without electricity, running water, or heating for hours daily
✗ 18.6% Pre-War Gender Wage Gap: Persists and risks widening due to economic displacement and single-mother poverty

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• 6+ Million: Ukrainian refugees in Europe (over 85% women and children).
• 1,500+: Healthcare and medical facilities damaged or destroyed by Russian strikes.
• 126 Days: Statutory fully paid maternity leave.
• 2021: Year Ukraine enacted equal statutory paternity leave rights (Law 1401-IX).

PHILIPPINES-UKRAINE RELATIONS
-----------------------------
• Strong mutual respect: The Philippines has consistently voted in the UN General Assembly to uphold Ukraine's territorial integrity and sovereignty.
• Both nations recognize the deep sacrifices of women sustaining family structures during national crises and economic disruption.

GSL TALKING POINTS
------------------
• "In Ukraine, the care economy is not a theoretical concept—it is a frontline of national survival. While soldiers defend our freedom, it is our women who nurse the wounded, shelter the children, and keep our society alive under bombardment."
• "We urge the international community: post-war reconstruction must not merely rebuild concrete bridges and factories. We must build bomb-safe kindergartens, accessible eldercare homes, and dignified mental health centers."

RESOLUTION IDEAS
----------------
• Model Resolution Clause on "Care Infrastructure Protection in Conflict Zones": Classifying early childhood and maternal healthcare centers as strictly protected civilian humanitarian infrastructure under international law.
• International financing for community-based veteran rehabilitation and trauma centers to prevent the privatization of war care onto female relatives.
