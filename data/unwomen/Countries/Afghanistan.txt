AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE
==================================================

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: RED (Extreme Agenda-Based Target / Human Rights Emergency)

Why?
• Systemic institutionalization of "gender apartheid" by the de facto Taliban authorities since August 2021
• Absolute elimination of female economic and social mobility: women are officially banned from working in virtually all sectors, including NGOs, government, and the United Nations
• Banned from secondary and university education (beyond Grade 6), permanently locking millions of girls into domestic confinement
• Women are confined to the home, bearing 100% of the domestic and care burden with zero rights, autonomy, or economic resources
• Extreme maternal mortality crisis (620+ deaths per 100,000 live births) exacerbated by bans on female medical training

Hot Topics
• Over 50 de facto edicts stripping women and girls of all public, economic, and educational life
• Mandatory confinement to the home; women cannot travel beyond 72 km without a male chaperone (mahram)
• Collapse of the care economy infrastructure: ban on female NGO workers has crippled humanitarian food and medical delivery
• Surge in child and forced marriages (up by over 25%) as families resort to desperate economic coping strategies
• International non-recognition of the Taliban regime versus humanitarian engagement obligations

Expected Debate Frequency: High / Universally Condemned

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA (DE FACTO TALIBAN TALKING POINTS)
-------------------------------------------------------------
• Claims that female confinement protects women's dignity, safety, and honor in full accordance with Islamic Sharia law and Afghan tribal traditions.
• Asserts that women's primary, divinely ordained role is motherhood, domestic care, and raising righteous children within the sanctity of the household.
• Claims that women are permitted to work in strictly segregated, culturally appropriate sectors, specifically basic female primary education and female-only healthcare.
• Denounces Western concepts of gender equality, female economic mobility, and UN conventions as foreign cultural imperialism designed to corrupt Islamic society.
• Demands the unfreezing of Afghanistan's central bank foreign reserves and an end to international sanctions to address the humanitarian starvation crisis.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Achieve diplomatic recognition from regional powers (China, Russia, Central Asian states) and the United Nations without conceding on female human rights.
• Unfreeze over $7 billion in central bank assets held in the US and international financial institutions.
• Maintain internal ideological cohesion among hardline clerical factions by enforcing strict moral edicts.
• Rebuff international human rights scrutiny at the UN by claiming religious and sovereign exemption.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ De facto administration has established security and ended major nationwide combat operations across rural provinces
✓ Eradication of major opium poppy cultivation across large swathes of the country
✓ Maintained localized primary schools for girls up to Grade 6 in several provinces
✓ Allowed limited female healthcare workers to operate in segregated maternal health wards
✓ Elimination of widespread administrative bribery in local border customs and checkpoints

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Institutionalized Gender Apartheid: Over 50 decrees stripping women of every recognized civil, political, economic, and social right
✗ Complete Ban on Secondary & Higher Education: Only country on earth that bans girls from attending secondary school and university
✗ Total Economic Disenfranchisement: Banned from working in civil service, private offices, NGOs, UN agencies, and beauty salons (which employed over 60,000 women)
✗ Care Confinement: Women are legally reduced to unpaid domestic laborers without legal recourse against domestic violence or forced marriage
✗ Healthcare Catastrophe: Banning women from attending medical universities guarantees that within a few years, no female doctors or midwives will exist to treat female patients
✗ Catastrophic Poverty & Starvation: Over 23 million Afghans require urgent humanitarian assistance; female-headed households face catastrophic acute malnutrition

UNPAID CARE WORK SITUATION
--------------------------
• In Afghanistan today, the care economy is a system of state-enforced domestic imprisonment. Women perform 100% of household, eldercare, and childcare labor under threat of physical violence, with zero economic independence.
• Denied access to public parks, gyms, public baths (hammams), and public transit without a male guardian (mahram).

CHILDCARE & ELDERCARE INFRASTRUCTURE
------------------------------------
• Public childcare and social security do not exist.
• Healthcare infrastructure has collapsed: rural clinics lack electricity, clean water, and female personnel, leading to one Afghan woman dying in pregnancy or childbirth every two hours.

DOMESTIC & MIGRANT WORKER POLICIES
----------------------------------
• Women are banned from domestic or international travel without a mahram.
• No legal protections exist for domestic workers; independent human rights commissions have been dismantled.

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• 80%+: Percentage of school-age Afghan girls (1.4 million) deliberately excluded from education by de facto edicts.
• $1 Billion+: Estimated annual loss to the Afghan economy resulting from the exclusion of women from the workforce (~5% of GDP).
• 620: Maternal mortality ratio per 100,000 live births (highest in Asia, among the worst globally).
• 1 in 3: Afghan women who face catastrophic food insecurity.

IMPLEMENTATION GAPS & HYPOCRISY
-------------------------------
• Claims to protect women under Sharia while denying them the foundational Islamic right to seek knowledge and literacy.
• Demands international aid to feed its population while banning the female humanitarian aid workers who are required to reach starving women and children.
• Claims that restrictions on female education are "temporary technical delays," yet enters its fourth year of maintaining absolute school bans.

BEST POINTS OF INFORMATION (POIs) TO ASK AFGHANISTAN
----------------------------------------------------
1. Gender Apartheid POI: "Can the delegate representing Afghanistan explain how a society can sustain its care economy when it officially bans women from secondary education, universities, and public employment, reducing half its population to forced domestic confinement?"
   - Expected Taliban Defence: Claim that women's sacred role is in the home according to Islamic law and that Western models are unsuitable.
   - Philippine Counter: "Every major Islamic organization and Muslim-majority nation—from Indonesia to Saudi Arabia—has condemned your bans on female education as completely un-Islamic. Confinement is not culture; it is state-sponsored oppression."

2. Medical Care Collapse POI: "If young women are banned from attending universities and nursing schools, who will provide medical care, deliver babies, and treat female patients in five years? Is it your official policy to let Afghan mothers die in childbirth rather than let a woman hold a university degree?"

3. Humanitarian Aid Ban POI: "With millions of Afghan families on the brink of starvation, why did your authorities ban women from working in NGOs and the UN, sabotaging the very humanitarian aid distribution that feeds your people?"

RESOLUTION IDEAS
