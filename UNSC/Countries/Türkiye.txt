NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Enforcing the Montreux Convention strictly to regulate transit through the Turkish Straits.
• Promoting the Middle Corridor (Trans-Caspian East-West-Middle Corridor) trade route.
• Protecting Turkey's maritime rights in the Aegean and Eastern Mediterranean ("Blue Homeland").
• Mediating regional conflicts (e.g., Black Sea grain corridor negotiations).
• Securing and diversifying energy imports (natural gas pipelines, nuclear projects).
• Expanding domestic defense industry exports, including unmanned systems (UAVs) and warships.
• Supporting economic and transport connectivity with Central Asian Republics.
• Countering terrorism along national borders to protect transit stability.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Focuses on positioning Turkey as a central logistics hub linking Asia and Europe. Promotes the Middle Corridor to bypass Russian and Iranian routes.
• Maritime Security: Strictly implements the Montreux Convention, regulating Black Sea transit. Opposes external naval deployments in the Black Sea.
• Free Trade: Supports free trade, maintaining a customs union with the European Union while seeking trade diversification.
• Sanctions: Does not enforce unilateral Western sanctions on Russia, maintaining energy and commercial trade.
• International Cooperation: Cooperates via BSEC, OIC, ECO, NATO, and UN channels.

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Sovereign control over the Turkish Straits (Bosporus, Dardanelles) under Montreux.
✓ Strategic geopolitical bridge between Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.
✓ Powerful military and modern, expanding navy (Milgem Project).

✓ Active mediator role, trusted by both Ukraine and Russia.
✓ Custom Union agreement with the EU, securing trade access.
✓ Diversified energy transit hub (host to major gas pipelines).

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Domestic inflation and currency volatility affecting trade costs.
✗ Dependency on imported energy and raw materials.
✗ Border conflicts with Syria and Iraq, straining national resources.
✗ Ongoing maritime boundary disputes with Greece, causing regional tension.
✗ Geopolitical balancing act between NATO commitments and Russia relations.
✗ Logistics routes vulnerable to regional escalation in the Middle East.

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Turkish Straits regulations under the Montreux Convention are non-negotiable.
• The Blue Homeland doctrine protects legitimate maritime rights under international law.
• Turkey's mediation is vital for securing global grain supply chains.
• Turkey opposes unilateral sanctions that disrupt global trade and food security.
• Transit corridor investments are commercial agreements that benefit global trade.
• Naval deployments in the Aegean are defensive and protect national sovereignty.
• Turkey supports the territorial integrity of Ukraine.
• Economic relations with Russia comply with Turkey's national interests and international law.
