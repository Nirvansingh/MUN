================================================================
UNSC CLOSING SPEECH TEMPLATES
================================================================

These templates help delegates summarize their positions and call for action during the closing sessions of the Security Council.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 1: P5 & WESTERN MARITIME ALLIANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

As this council concludes its deliberations, the delegation of [Country Name] wishes to emphasize the collective achievement of our bloc. The draft resolution we have finalized reinforces the fundamental rules of international law, specifically the UNCLOS framework.

By establishing cooperative inspection regimes for un-insured shadow fleet tankers and authorizing joint naval patrols to protect commercial transit routes, we have demonstrated that this council can act decisively. We have sent a clear message: illegal blockades and maritime piracy will not be tolerated.

However, resolution text is only the beginning. The safety of global supply chains depends on the physical execution of these security measures. We call upon all member states to fulfill their commitments, protect seafarers, and keep the trade routes of the world open. Thank you."

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 2: SOVEREIGN & EURASIAN BLOC
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] is pleased that this council has resisted attempts to validate unilateral Western economic warfare. While we support maritime safety, we will continue to oppose illegal, non-UN sanctions that disrupt global energy and agricultural supply lines.

The final drafts of our working papers have successfully preserved the principle of national sovereignty. Any maritime security operations or infrastructure defense must respect the territorial waters and EEZ jurisdictions of coastal states. 

Russia, China, and our partners will continue to develop independent, secure trade corridors—such as the Northern Sea Route and the Middle Corridor—outside of Western control. We will defend our technological and economic sovereignty. Thank you."

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 3: TRANSIT & DEVELOPING BLOC
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] welcomes the emphasis placed in the final drafts on protecting food and energy security for the Global South. For developing countries, trade stability is not a strategic game; it is a matter of economic survival.

The funding commitments secured for port cybersecurity and the protection of grain transit corridors are critical steps. We thank the council for focusing on peaceful, non-military de-escalation in strategic choke points.

We urge all parties to implement the agreements in good faith, ensuring that commercial shipping lanes remain safe corridors of peace and development. Thank you."
