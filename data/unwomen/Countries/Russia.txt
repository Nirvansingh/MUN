AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: YELLOW (Demographic Pronatalist & State-Subsidized Care Model)

Why?
• Extensive Soviet-era legacy of state-supported public nursery and kindergarten infrastructure
• Generous "Maternity Capital" (Materinskiy Kapital) state cash grants for first, second, and third children to stimulate birth rates
• High statutory maternity leave: 140 days fully paid (100% of average earnings) plus parental leave up to 3 years with job security
• Deep ideological pivot toward state-sponsored "traditional family values," contrasting with Western liberal gender frameworks
• Controversial 2017 decriminalization of first-time domestic battery ("battery within the family") reducing legal penalties for domestic abuse

Hot Topics
• "Maternity Capital" subsidies: demographic effectiveness versus long-term female economic mobility
• The 2017 domestic violence decriminalization and its devastating impact on women's physical safety in the domestic sphere
• Demographic decline exacerbated by wartime casualties and male mobilization
• The "Banned Professions List" restricting women from certain industrial, maritime, and transport occupations (reduced from 456 to ~100)
• Conservative state ideology promoting women primarily as mothers and guardians of traditional spiritual values

Expected Debate Frequency: High / Complex Geopolitical Faultlines

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Asserts that protecting motherhood, childhood, and traditional family values is a foundational constitutional obligation under the 2020 Constitutional Amendments.
• Touts the "Maternity Capital" program, providing direct financial subsidies of over 630,000 rubles (~$7,000) for the first child and over 830,000 rubles for the second, usable for housing, education, or maternal pensions.
• Highlights generous statutory leave: 70 days prenatal and 70 days postnatal leave at 100% pay, plus partially paid parental leave until the child reaches 1.5 years and job-guaranteed unpaid leave until 3 years.
• Defends the traditional division of domestic roles as natural and culturally rooted, rejecting Western gender terminology.
• Emphasizes state-guaranteed free public kindergartens and pediatric polyclinics.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Reverse acute demographic decline (fertility rate around 1.4) to maintain national labor supply and military recruitment pools.
• Champion "traditional spiritual-moral values" globally, building diplomatic coalitions with conservative and developing states against Western progressive frameworks.
• Defend state sovereignty against international human rights bodies regarding domestic violence and reproductive laws.
• Maintain female workforce participation in critical sectors (education, healthcare, civil service) while incentivizing high birth rates.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ Fully paid statutory maternity leave of 140 days (100% of earnings) covered entirely by the Social Insurance Fund
✓ "Maternity Capital" (Materinskiy Kapital) providing substantial direct cash certificates for every newborn child
✓ Extensive network of free state-funded public kindergartens ("Detskiy Sad") with near-universal enrollment for ages 3 to 7
✓ Job protection guaranteeing mothers can return to their exact position after taking parental leave up to 3 years
✓ High proportion of women in senior executive, financial, and managerial positions compared to the global average

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ 2017 Decriminalization of Domestic Violence: Reclassified first-time domestic battery causing minor bruising as an administrative infraction rather than a criminal offense, fostering impunity in the home
✗ Severe Gender Wage Gap (~24% to 27%): Concentrated in lower-paying public-sector care professions (teaching, nursing) while men dominate high-paying extraction and defense industries
✗ Pronatalist Ideological Pressure: State rhetoric actively discourages young women from pursuing higher education and corporate careers before childbirth
✗ Banned Professions List: Government still bars women from approximately 100 occupations deemed hazardous to maternal health
✗ 2.2:1 Care Disparity: Russian women spend over 4.3 hours daily on unpaid housework and childcare, compared to less than 2 hours for men
✗ Wartime Economic Distortion: High state spending on military operations diverts federal budget resources away from regional public healthcare and nursery renovations

UNPAID CARE WORK SITUATION
--------------------------
• Russian women bear the overwhelming burden of household chores, cooking, cleaning, and eldercare, deeply entrenched by cultural expectations.
• Grandmothers ("babushkas") provide an enormous, unquantified informal childcare subsidy, stepping in so young mothers can return to the workforce.

CHILDCARE & ELDERCARE INFRASTRUCTURE
------------------------------------
• High kindergarten availability for ages 3+, but infant nurseries (under age 1.5) face severe shortages in rapidly growing suburban areas.
• Eldercare infrastructure is underdeveloped; state nursing homes ("internaty") are frequently underfunded, leaving families to manage chronic eldercare at home.

DOMESTIC & MIGRANT WORKER POLICIES
----------------------------------
• Large population of Central Asian female migrant workers (from Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan) working as domestic cleaners, cooks, and hospital aides.
• Central Asian care workers face precarious legal status, frequent police checks, and lack of social insurance.

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• 140 Days: Fully paid statutory maternity leave (70 days before birth, 70 days after).
• 630,000+ Rubles: Value of Maternity Capital certificate for the first child.
• 27%: Gender wage gap between male and female workers in Russia.
• ~1.4: Total fertility rate (well below replacement level despite billions in subsidies).

IMPLEMENTATION GAPS & HYPOCRISY
-------------------------------
• Celebrates the "sanctity of motherhood" while failing to protect mothers from domestic violence under the weakened 2017 battery law.
• Offers large cash payments for childbirth, but fails to close the 27% gender wage gap or provide sufficient nursery facilities for infants under 1.5 years.
• Condemns Western labor practices while relying on undocumented Central Asian women to perform menial hospital and sanitation care.

BEST POINTS OF INFORMATION (POIs) TO ASK RUSSIA
-----------------------------------------------
1. Domestic Safety & Care POI: "Can the honorable delegate of Russia explain how a society can foster women's economic and social mobility within the care economy when the 2017 decriminalization of domestic violence reclassified domestic battery as a minor administrative infraction, leaving millions of female caregivers legally unprotected in their own homes?"
   - Expected Russia Defence: Claim that the law targets trivial disputes, while criminal code provisions still apply for serious bodily harm, protecting family privacy from state intrusion.
   - Philippine Counter: "The European Court of Human Rights and the UN CEDAW Committee repeatedly documented that reclassifying battery created systemic impunity. A woman cannot achieve economic independence or provide secure care when she faces violence at home without criminal protection."

2. Gender Wage Gap POI: "Despite Soviet-era infrastructure, Russia's gender wage gap hovers near 27%, with women heavily segregated in low-paying public care and teaching jobs. What structural wage reforms is Russia implementing to value public care workers?"

3. Banned Occupations POI: "Why does Russia continue to maintain a statutory list of banned occupations restricting women from certain transport and engineering careers under the paternalistic pretext of protecting maternal health?"

LIKELY ATTACKS AGAINST THE PHILIPPINES FROM RUSSIA

We maintain zero banned professions for women, and our laws strictly criminalize all domestic violence under the Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act (RA 9262)."

GSL TALKING POINTS
------------------
• "Financial incentives and maternity capital cannot buy demographic growth if women are not granted physical safety, equal workplace opportunities, and a fair distribution of unpaid domestic labor."

RESOLUTION IDEAS
----------------
• International recognition of legal protections against domestic violence as an indispensable foundation of the care economy.
• Multilateral guidelines for the complete abolition of "banned profession lists" that restrict women's occupational mobility under the guise of reproductive protection.
