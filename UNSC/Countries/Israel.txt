================================================================
ISRAEL
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: State of Israel
Capital: Jerusalem
Government: Parliamentary Democracy
Current Leader: Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu
Population: ~9.8 Million
UNSC Status: Non-Permanent Member (Eligible/Frequent voice)
Major International Organizations: UN, OECD, WTO, IMO

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR ALLIES
------------
• United States
• United Kingdom
• Germany
• Italy
• France
• Canada
• Greece
• Cyprus
• Western Nations

---------------------------------------------------------------

MAJOR RIVALS
------------
• Iran
• Palestine
• Syria
• Yemen (Houthi Authorities)

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing maritime trade routes to Eilat and Haifa ports.
• Defending offshore natural gas platforms (Leviathan, Tamar) from missile threats.
• Countering Iranian maritime weapons transfers to regional proxies.
• Promoting the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) to bypass hostile zones.
• Maintaining advanced technology and security supply chains.
• Defending against Houthi maritime blockades targeting Israel-bound shipping.
• Protecting critical undersea internet cables in the Mediterranean.
• Enhancing security cooperation with the US Sixth Fleet and regional partners.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Promotes trade corridor diversification (IMEC). Focuses on securing high-tech supply chains and energy self-sufficiency.
• Maritime Security: Strongly advocates for international military action against maritime terrorism. Supports joint patrols to secure lanes.
• Free Trade: Supporter of open, rules-based free trade, particularly for technology and security services.
• Sanctions: A leading proponent of international sanctions against Iran, the IRGC, and regional proxies.
• International Cooperation: Coordinates primarily with the United States and European allies to secure transport lanes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Houthi shipping blockades targeting vessels bound for Eilat.
• Security of natural gas rigs in the Mediterranean.
• Counter-terrorism operations in international waters.
• Security of undersea internet links in the Eastern Mediterranean.
• Trade corridor development (IMEC) linking Israel to India and Europe.
• Cybersecurity of port management databases.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Advanced technological economy, leading in cybersecurity and defense tech.
✓ Highly modern navy (Israeli Navy) with advanced missile corvette fleets.
✓ Domestic natural gas resources, providing energy independence.
✓ Strong strategic alliance and intelligence sharing with the United States.
✓ High level of cyber resilience protecting critical port infrastructure.
✓ Strategic position linking Mediterranean and Red Sea routes.
✓ Leading private sector innovation in shipping logistics.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ Small geographic size, heavily dependent on maritime imports for food and goods.
✗ High geopolitical isolation in the region, facing active security threats.
✗ Port of Eilat highly vulnerable to Houthi missile and drone blockades.
✗ High defense expenditures, putting pressure on national budgets.
✗ Vulnerability of offshore gas platforms to regional conflict escalation.
✗ Internal political division affecting long-term strategic plans.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Israel's maritime defense operations protect civilian lives and assets from terrorism.
• Houthi shipping blockades are illegal violations of freedom of navigation under international law.
• Offshore energy platforms are sovereign assets protected under international security norms.
• Israel coordinates with international partners to secure shipping lanes.
• Cybersecurity measures protect supply chain databases from state-sponsored threats.
• Israel supports open, rules-based free trade and WTO rules.
• Defense operations comply strictly with international humanitarian law.
• Regional security requires dismantling Iranian-backed proxy networks.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does Israel's conflict in Gaza affect maritime security in the Red Sea?
Q2. What measures protect Mediterranean shipping from collateral damage during conflicts?
Q3. How do Israel's actions align with international law regarding blockades?
Q4. Does the security of offshore natural gas platforms risk regional escalation?
Q5. What checks prevent Israeli cyber technologies from targeting foreign maritime systems?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• Iran: When will Iran cease funding maritime piracy and Houthi attacks?
• USA: How can the US ensure Houthi blockades on Israel-bound ships are dismantled?
• Turkey: How does Turkey justify trade restrictions that violate bilateral agreements?
• Egypt: How can we cooperate to secure Red Sea commercial transit routes?
• Greece: How can we expand energy security cooperation in the Eastern Mediterranean?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Resolutions that restrict Israel's right to defend its offshore energy assets.
• Any UN mandate that validates Houthi maritime blocks.
• Resolutions that link maritime security exclusively to regional conflicts.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: United States, Western nations, Greece, Cyprus.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: Gulf partners (maintain diplomatic tracks under Abraham Accords).
• Possible compromises: Support international monitoring of Mediterranean shipping lanes.
• Main objectives: Secure Eilat shipping, condemn Houthi blockades, protect gas assets.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Speak with high conviction, focusing on counter-terrorism and security.
✓ Frame Houthi shipping blocks as a global threat to maritime trade, not just an Israeli issue.
✓ Highlight Israel's contribution to cybersecurity defense for logistics.
✓ Coordinate arguments closely with the United States.
✓ Reference international maritime law and UNCLOS to condemn shipping attacks.
