KINGDOM OF SAUDI ARABIA

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Saudi Arabia is the world's leading oil exporter and a dominant power in the Islamic world and Middle East. It is undergoing a historic economic and social transformation under Vision 2030, aiming to diversify beyond oil.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Riyadh
Largest City: Riyadh
Official Name: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
Government Type: Unitary Islamic absolute monarchy
Head of State: King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud
Head of Government: Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman (de facto ruler)
Official Languages: Arabic
Population: ~36 million
Area: 2,149,690 km² (12th largest)
Currency: Saudi Riyal (SAR)
GDP (Nominal): ~$1.2 trillion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$33,000

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Oil and gas, petrochemicals, refining, construction, finance, tourism (growing), mining, logistics
Major Exports: Crude oil, refined petroleum, polymers, fertilizers, chemicals, plastics
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, electrical equipment, pharmaceuticals, food products, metals, clothing
Major Trade Partners: China, India, USA, UAE, Japan, South Korea, Germany, Egypt

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Well-funded military (~225,000 active personnel). Significant defence spending (~7% of GDP, 3rd highest globally). Modern equipment from US, UK, Europe. Active in Yemen conflict.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). Developing civilian nuclear program with uranium enrichment ambitions.
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: Non-permanent member (most recently 2022-23)
Major International Organizations: UN, OPEC (leader), G20, OIC, GCC, Arab League, IMF, World Bank, WTO

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Dominant power in Arabian Peninsula; leading OPEC member; influential in Islamic world (cradle of Islam, custodian of two holy mosques); major donor to Muslim countries
Neighbouring Countries: Jordan, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE, Oman, Yemen
Strategic Importance: World's largest oil exporter; controls key energy markets; strategic Red Sea coastline; custodian of Mecca and Medina; major investment power (PIF sovereign wealth fund ~$900 billion)
Important Maritime Routes: Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, Red Sea, Bab el-Mandeb Strait
Important Geography: Arabian Desert (largest sand desert), Rub' al Khali (Empty Quarter), Red Sea coral reefs, Asir Mountains

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: World's second-largest oil reserves (~17% of global); fourth-largest natural gas reserves; massive solar energy potential
Natural Resources: Oil, natural gas, gold, copper, phosphate, bauxite, iron ore, uranium
Technology Sector: Rapidly developing — NEOM smart city, futuristic projects (The Line), tech investment via PIF (Uber, Lucid, gaming), digital transformation
Major Companies: Saudi Aramco (most valuable oil company), SABIC, Al Rajhi Bank, ACWA Power, STC, Ma'aden, NEOM

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: ~97% literacy; massive investment in education and scholarships; new universities (KAUST, King Saud)
Internet Penetration: ~99% of population (among highest globally)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.875 (Very High, ranked 35th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 44/100 (ranked 53rd)
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — state-controlled media; strict censorship; journalists face arrest
Human Rights Overview: Significant reforms under MBS (women driving, entertainment, tourism) but severe restrictions on political dissent, LGBTQ+ rights (death penalty), freedom of expression, and labour rights (migrant workers, kafala system)

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Vision 2030 economic transformation, regional stability (Yemen peace), normalization with Israel, reducing US dependence, engaging China and Russia
Foreign Policy Overview: Historically US-aligned; increasingly multi-aligned (China, Russia); assertive regional policy under MBS; major humanitarian donor; oil diplomacy as foreign policy tool
Relations With:
  USA: Long-standing strategic ally; oil for security; recent tensions over Khashoggi, OPEC+, normalization push
  China: Largest oil customer; growing economic and security ties; Belt and Road participation
  Russia: OPEC+ coordination on oil prices; growing trade and diplomatic engagement; Syria coordination
  India: Strong energy and trade ties; labour diaspora (~2.5 million); strategic partnership growing
  Pakistan: Close historical ally; financial support; military cooperation; large diaspora
  EU: Major trade and investment partner; human rights concerns; clean energy transition discussions
  Middle East: Rivalry with Iran; supports Lebanon; Yemen intervention; competing with UAE; key role in Arab League
  Neighbouring Countries: Gulf leadership; competition with Qatar and UAE; border security with Yemen; support for Yemen government against Houthis

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: USA (traditional), UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Egypt, Pakistan (close ally)
Major Rivals: Iran (primary rival), Qatar (historical tensions), Turkey

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Oil market stability, economic diversification (Vision 2030), regional security, countering Iran, technology investment, human rights modernization, tourism growth
Current Major Challenges: Oil price volatility; climate transition risk; Yemen war quagmire; human rights international pressure; succession planning; water scarcity; unemployment among youth

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Saudi Arabia is the world's largest exporter of petroleum.
• The Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF) is one of the largest sovereign wealth funds globally.
• Women were granted the right to drive only in 2018.
• Saudi Arabia has no rivers — it is one of the driest countries on Earth.
