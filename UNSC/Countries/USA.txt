UNITED STATES
================================================================

BASIC INFORMATION
-----------------
Official Name: United States of America
Capital: Washington, D.C.
Government: Federal Presidential Constitutional Republic
Current Leader: President Donald J. Trump
Population: ~340 Million
UNSC Status: Permanent Member (P5)
Major International Organizations: UN, NATO, G7, OECD, APEC, WTO, IMO

---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Maintaining freedom of navigation in global maritime choke points (Bab-el-Mandeb, Taiwan Strait).
• Securing global supply chains against disruption by state and non-state actors.
• Defending critical maritime infrastructure, including undersea communication cables.
• Countering Iranian-backed Houthi threats to commercial shipping in the Red Sea.
• Enforcing international sanctions against Russia, Iran, and North Korea.
• Promoting "friend-shoring" to relocate supply chains to democratic partner nations.
• Maintaining the US dollar as the primary currency for global trade and energy contracts.
• Protecting domestic manufacturing and strategic technology sectors from foreign espionage.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Advocates for diversifying supply chains away from adversarial states (specifically China and Russia). Promotes friend-shoring and near-shoring to build resilience.
• Maritime Security: Strongly supports the execution of Freedom of Navigation Operations (FONOPs). Led the establishment of the defensive coalition Operation Prosperity Guardian in the Red Sea.
• Free Trade: Supports a rules-based trading system but utilizes targeted tariffs and export controls on critical technologies (e.g., advanced semiconductors) for national security.
• Sanctions: View sanctions as a crucial, peaceful tool to penalize violations of international law. Supports strict enforcement of maritime oil caps on Russia.
• International Cooperation: Actively cooperates through G7, NATO, and coalitions of willing states to secure trade lanes when UNSC consensus is blocked.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping and Operation Prosperity Guardian.
• Undersea communication cable vulnerability in the Baltic and Mediterranean.
• Russian "shadow fleet" bypassing oil price caps and risking maritime accidents.
• Supply chain vulnerabilities regarding critical minerals and rare earths from China.
• Taiwan Strait security and freedom of navigation.
• Impact of Panama Canal drought and routing shifts on global inflation.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ World's most powerful blue-water navy capable of global power projection.
✓ Network of military bases and bilateral security treaties near global choke points.
✓ Leading domestic producer of oil and natural gas, reducing import dependency.
✓ Dollar dominance provides leverage in international trade and financial monitoring.
✓ Strong private sector driving innovations in logistics and supply chain analytics.
✓ Leadership of major international security coalitions.
✓ Key trade partner for both European and Indo-Pacific economies.

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High dependency on foreign imports for rare earth elements and critical minerals.
✗ Domestic political divisions that can disrupt long-term foreign and trade policies.
✗ Military overstretch across multiple active global conflict zones.
✗ Susceptibility of domestic consumer markets to inflation from transport costs.
✗ Reliance on advanced semiconductor manufacturing concentrated in Taiwan.
✗ Domestic ports suffer from lower efficiency and modernization compared to Asian hubs.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Operation Prosperity Guardian is a defensive coalition designed to protect civilian lives.
• Freedom of navigation is a universal right under international law and UNCLOS.
• Targeted export controls and tariffs are necessary measures to protect national security.
• Sanctions target state actors violating international norms, not humanitarian goods.
• Undersea cable protection requires cooperative international security frameworks.
• Decoupling is not the goal; rather, the US seeks to de-risk and diversify.
• Security of the Taiwan Strait is vital for global economic stability and shipping.
• The US works to coordinate with regional partners to protect transit routes.

---------------------------------------------------------------

LIKELY QUESTIONS AGAINST THIS COUNTRY
-------------------------------------
Q1. How does the US justify unilateral sanctions that disrupt third-party global trade?
Q2. Why has the United States not ratified UNCLOS while claiming to defend its principles?
Q3. How does Operation Prosperity Guardian prevent escalation of conflict in the Red Sea?
Q4. Does the US policy of "friend-shoring" violate WTO principles of non-discrimination?
Q5. How do you respond to the impact of US export controls on global technology supply chains?

---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS THIS COUNTRY CAN ASK
------------------------------
Ask one strong diplomatic question each to:
• China: How can China justify its silence on Houthi attacks that disrupt global shipping?
• Russia: What steps is Russia taking to prevent its "shadow fleet" from causing maritime accidents?
• Iran: Will Iran cease providing targeting data to groups threatening Red Sea trade?
• Egypt: How can the UNSC assist Egypt in mitigating the loss of Suez Canal revenue?
• UAE: What measures are being taken to prevent illicit transshipment of sanctioned Russian oil?

---------------------------------------------------------------

RED LINES
----------
The country is unlikely to support:
• Restrictions on Freedom of Navigation Operations (FONOPs) in international waters.
• Any dilution of international sanctions on Russia, Iran, or North Korea.
• Proposals that give non-democratic states regulatory oversight of open-source logistics data.

---------------------------------------------------------------

NEGOTIATION STRATEGY
--------------------
• Countries to ally with: UK, France, Japan, South Korea, EU members, Canada, Australia.
• Countries to avoid confronting first: China (focus on shared interests in trade stability first).
• Possible compromises: Support funding for developing countries to modernize port security.
• Main objectives: Secure condemnation of Red Sea attacks, call out the Russian shadow fleet, and promote joint maritime patrols.

---------------------------------------------------------------

MUN TIPS
--------
✓ Use authoritative, leadership-focused language suitable for a P5 member.
✓ Frame your arguments around the defense of global economic stability and civilian shipping.
✓ Reference international law and maritime conventions, particularly UNCLOS, despite non-ratification.
✓ Highlight the defensive nature of coalitions like Operation Prosperity Guardian.
✓ Deflect criticisms of tariffs by pointing to national security and fair-trade practices.
