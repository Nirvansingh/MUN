This document provides formal preambular and operative clauses suitable for final draft resolutions in the Security Council, including Chapter VII mandates.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FORMAL PREAMBULAR CLAUSES
----------------------------------------------------------------

• Reaffirming its commitment to the sovereignty, territorial integrity, and political independence of all member states in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations,

• Acting under Chapter VII of the Charter of the United Nations in the interest of restoring international peace, security, and the safety of navigation,

• Condemning in the strongest terms all attacks, hostings, and illegal detentions of commercial vessels and their crews in the Red Sea, Strait of Hormuz, and adjacent waters,

• Expressing concern over the rise of the un-insured 'shadow fleet' of tankers carrying crude oil through international straits, noting the lack of P&I insurance and safety compliance,

• Reaffirming that all maritime security operations must comply strictly with the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) and regional treaties,

----------------------------------------------------------------
FORMAL OPERATIVE CLAUSES
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Decides to establish the United Nations Maritime Security Coordination Mechanism (UNMSCM) to facilitate real-time communication between naval coalitions, coastal states, and merchant vessel operators;

2. Authorizes member states, acting nationally or through regional organizations, to conduct inspections of merchant vessels suspected of violating arms embargoes or carrying illicit cargo in international waters, in accordance with international law;

3. Urges coastal states to enforce safety and environmental regulations under the MARPOL Convention, and to verify that all tankers transiting their Exclusive Economic Zones possess valid Protection and Indemnity (P&I) insurance;

4. Demands that all parties immediately cease attacks on civilian seafarers and merchant shipping, and calls for the immediate release of all unlawfully detained vessels and crews;

5. Decides to establish a Special Committee on Undersea Infrastructure Security (SCUIS) to compile best practices and coordinate joint monitoring of undersea communications and energy links;

6. Recommends that the World Trade Organization (WTO) and UNCTAD establish a Joint Working Group to assess the economic impact of unilateral trade and shipping blocks on food security in the Global South.
