REPUBLIC OF THE UNION OF MYANMAR

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
Myanmar (Burma) is a Southeast Asian country rich in natural resources, but it remains deeply unstable following the 2021 military coup, ongoing civil war, and severe humanitarian crisis. It is a key flashpoint in regional geopolitics.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Naypyidaw
Largest City: Yangon (formerly Rangoon)
Official Name: Republic of the Union of Myanmar
Government Type: Unitary military junta (State Administration Council)
Head of State: Prime Minister Min Aung Hlaing (military leader)
Head of Government: Prime Minister Min Aung Hlaing
Official Languages: Burmese
Population: ~55 million
Area: 676,578 km² (39th largest)
Currency: Burmese Kyat (MMK)
GDP (Nominal): ~$65 billion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$1,200

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Natural gas extraction, agriculture (rice, beans), mining (jade, rubies, gems), timber, garments, opium production
Major Exports: Natural gas, rice, garments, jade, rubies, timber, beans, pulses, fish
Major Imports: Machinery, vehicles, refined petroleum, electronics, cooking oil, pharmaceuticals, plastics, chemicals
Major Trade Partners: China, Thailand, India, Japan, Singapore, Indonesia, Bangladesh

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Tatmadaw (Myanmar military) ~150,000 active personnel. Ongoing civil war with multiple ethnic armed organizations and opposition PDF forces. Heavy reliance on airstrikes and artillery. Military controls major economic assets.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Non-nuclear weapon state (NPT). No known nuclear ambitions.
UN Membership: 1948
UNSC Status: Not on Council
Major International Organizations: UN, ASEAN (membership contested post-coup), BIMSTEC, NAM, WTO, IMF

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Limited — internationally isolated post-coup; ASEAN divided engagement; China dominates influence
Neighbouring Countries: China, India, Bangladesh, Thailand, Laos
Strategic Importance: Rich in natural resources (jade, rubies, natural gas, timber); strategic location between India and China; key Belt and Road projects (China-Myanmar Economic Corridor); India's Act East gateway
Important Maritime Routes: Bay of Bengal, Andaman Sea; strategically located near Strait of Malacca
Important Geography: Irrawaddy River basin; Himalayan foothills (north); extensive coastline; dense forests

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Significant natural gas reserves (major exporter to Thailand and China); limited oil; hydroelectric potential (but underdeveloped); deforestation for biomass
Natural Resources: Natural gas, jade (world's finest), rubies, sapphires, copper, lead, zinc, tin, tungsten, timber, fish
Technology Sector: Very limited — basic telecommunications; mobile penetration moderate; no significant tech industry
Major Companies: Myanmar Oil and Gas Enterprise (state), Myanmar Economic Holdings (military), Myanma Timber Enterprise (state)

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: ~76% literacy; higher education severely impacted by coup and COVID
Internet Penetration: ~44% of population; heavily restricted post-coup (frequent shutdowns)
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.585 (Medium, ranked 149th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: Not ranked (2024) — historically among most corrupt
Press Freedom Status: Very restricted — journalists arrested; independent media shut down post-coup
Human Rights Overview: Severe — military junta committing atrocities (Rohingya genocide case at ICJ); war crimes in civil war; mass arbitrary arrests; torture; forced displacement of 2+ million

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Regime survival, counter-insurgency, international legitimacy restoration, arms procurement, resource exports to China
Foreign Policy Overview: Internationally isolated post-coup; China and Russia provide diplomatic cover (UNSC vetoes); ASEAN divided; limited engagement with neighbours
Relations With:
  USA: Sanctions regime; arms embargo; condemnation of coup and genocide; limited humanitarian engagement
  China: Primary ally — provides arms, investment, diplomatic support; controls major economic assets
  Russia: Growing military cooperation — arms, nuclear energy talks; diplomatic support at UN
  India: Cautious engagement — border security concerns; development projects; balancing China influence
  Pakistan: Limited engagement; some defence ties
  EU: Sanctions; no diplomatic recognition of junta; humanitarian aid only
  Middle East: Limited — some trade with UAE and Saudi Arabia
  Neighbouring Countries: China dominates; India cautious; Thailand engages for border trade; Bangladesh strained (Rohingya crisis); Laos and Cambodia more accommodating

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: China (primary backer), Russia (military partner), North Korea (alleged arms ties)
Major Rivals: Ethnic armed organizations, PDF (people's defence forces), ASEAN critics

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Regime survival, resource revenue, arms procurement, fighting opposition forces, international legitimacy
Current Major Challenges: Civil war (multiple fronts); severe humanitarian crisis; economic collapse; international sanctions; Rohingya genocide case (ICJ); health system collapse; displacement crisis

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• Myanmar produces over 90% of the world's rubies.
• Myanmar's jade industry is worth billions, mostly controlled by the military.
• The Rohingya genocide case at the ICJ was brought by Gambia on behalf of OIC.
• Myanmar has over 135 officially recognized ethnic groups.
