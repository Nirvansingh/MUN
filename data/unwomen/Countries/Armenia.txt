AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE
==================================================

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: YELLOW (Democratic Reformer Facing Acute Displacement & Care Shocks)

Why?
• Acute humanitarian care crisis: Absorbed over 100,000 forcibly displaced ethnic Armenians from Nagorno-Karabakh in late 2023, placing extraordinary sudden demands on national childcare, maternal health, and trauma support systems
• Groundbreaking Childcare Reform (2020-2023): Reformed the monthly child care allowance so that mothers who return to work before their child turns 2 continue to receive the state benefit, eliminating the penalty for working mothers
• Statutory Paid Maternity Leave: 140 calendar days (70 days prenatal, 70 days postnatal) covered at 100% of average earnings through the state budget
• The "Khopon" (Seasonal Labor Migration) Phenomenon: High rates of male circular migration to Russia leave hundreds of thousands of rural Armenian women as de facto solo heads of household, managing all unpaid care, subsistence farming, and eldercare alone
• Rapid democratic modernization under the post-2018 reform agenda, balancing Western integration with deep cultural family traditions

Hot Topics
• Integration and trauma care for over 100,000 refugees displaced from Nagorno-Karabakh (over 50% women and children)
• The Child Care Allowance reform enabling working mothers to retain childcare benefits upon returning to employment
• Combating prenatal sex selection: Public campaigns and medical protocols cutting skewed birth sex ratios
• The "Khopon" seasonal migration leaving rural women with overwhelming double-burdens
• Stagnant female labor force participation (~50-52%) and 28% gender wage gap

Expected Debate Frequency: Medium / High Empathy State

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Highlights sweeping democratic and legal modernizations enacted since 2018 to dismantle Soviet-era gender barriers.
• Showcases the innovative Child Care Allowance: Providing monthly cash allowances for parents caring for children up to age 2, uniquely structured so that mothers do NOT lose this allowance if they return to employment.
• Offers 140 days of statutory maternity leave paid at 100% of previous earnings funded directly by the state budget.
• Demands international solidarity and long-term humanitarian funding to build housing, kindergartens, and psychological clinics for the 100,000+ displaced people from Nagorno-Karabakh.
• Actively implementing the National Strategy on Gender Equality and the Action Plan for the Elimination of Prenatal Sex Selection.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Secure multilateral financing for permanent housing, public kindergartens, and psychological rehabilitation for displaced refugee families.
• Accelerate integration with the European Union while preserving economic stability.
• Expand female participation in Armenia's booming high-tech and software engineering sectors.
• Alleviate rural poverty and improve community healthcare in border villages.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ 140 days of fully paid statutory maternity leave funded by the state budget
✓ Innovative childcare allowance reform: Mothers who return to work before child turns 2 continue to receive the monthly state allowance
✓ Rapid reduction in sex-selective abortions through medical counseling and community awareness campaigns
✓ Booming tech ecosystem actively encouraging women into STEM and software programming
✓ Successfully integrated 100,000+ displaced people with immediate access to public schools, healthcare, and maternal services

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Humanitarian Care Strain: The sudden arrival of over 100,000 displaced people in late 2023 has severely overcrowded schools and clinics
✗ 28.4% Gender Wage Gap: Substantial wage disparity between male and female workers
✗ The "Khopon" Double Burden: Seasonal migration of men to Russia leaves rural women carrying 100% of physical household and agricultural labor alone
✗ Shortage of Rural Daycare: Kindergartens in provincial regions often lack winter heating, modern sanitation, and full-day operating hours
✗ Deep-Rooted Patriarchal Family Norms: Cultural pressure on women to prioritize in-law eldercare and household maintenance over careers

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• 100,000+: Forcibly displaced persons integrated into Armenia within weeks in late 2023.
• 140 Days: Paid statutory maternity leave at 100% salary.
• 28.4%: Gender wage gap in Armenia.
• 2 Years: Age up to which parents receive the reformed monthly child care allowance.

PHILIPPINES-ARMENIA RELATIONS
-----------------------------
• Friendly and supportive: Both nations understand the profound human sacrifices of labor migration and the resilience of women who head households when men migrate for work.
• The Philippines can support Armenia's humanitarian care initiatives and champion its policy of allowing working mothers to retain childcare benefits.

GSL TALKING POINTS
------------------
• "Armenia has shown extraordinary compassion, opening its arms to over 100,000 displaced brothers and sisters within days. But true integration requires care infrastructure: kindergartens, trauma counselors, and maternal clinics."
• "We commend Armenia's progressive policy allowing working mothers to keep their childcare allowances upon returning to work. That is how a state removes the penalty on working women."

RESOLUTION IDEAS
----------------
• Model Resolution Clause on Non-Forfeiture of Childcare Subsidies: Ensuring that parents who re-enter the labor market do not immediately lose state childcare allowances.
• Dedicated multilateral humanitarian care pools for refugee and displaced mothers and children.
