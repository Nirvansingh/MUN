UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

================================================================
QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================
The United States is the world's largest economy and a dominant military power with significant global influence across all international institutions. It plays a central role in the UN Security Council as a P5 member and is a leading voice on technology, human rights, and international security.

================================================================
BASIC INFORMATION
================================================================
Capital: Washington, D.C.
Largest City: New York City
Official Name: United States of America
Government Type: Federal presidential constitutional republic
Head of State: President Joe Biden
Head of Government: President Joe Biden
Official Languages: None at federal level; English (de facto)
Population: ~340 million (2025)
Area: 9,833,520 km² (3rd largest)
Currency: United States Dollar (USD)
GDP (Nominal): ~$28.8 trillion (2025)
GDP Per Capita: ~$83,000

================================================================
ECONOMY
================================================================
Major Industries: Technology, financial services, healthcare, defense, aerospace, energy, agriculture, entertainment
Major Exports: Refined petroleum, crude oil, aircraft, vehicles, integrated circuits, pharmaceuticals
Major Imports: Vehicles, crude oil, computers, broadcasting equipment, pharmaceuticals
Major Trade Partners: Canada, Mexico, China, Japan, Germany, United Kingdom, South Korea

================================================================
MILITARY & SECURITY
================================================================
Military Overview: Largest military budget globally (~$920 billion). Operates the most capable navy, air force, and nuclear arsenal. Maintains ~750 military bases across 80+ countries.
Nuclear Weapons Status: Recognized nuclear weapon state (NPT). ~5,550 warheads (deployed + stockpiled).
UN Membership: Founding member (1945)
UNSC Status: P5 (permanent member with veto power)
Major International Organizations: UN, NATO, G7, G20, OECD, OAS, IMF, World Bank, WTO

================================================================
GEOGRAPHY & INFLUENCE
================================================================
Regional Influence: Dominant power in North and South America; extensive influence in Europe, East Asia, and Middle East
Neighbouring Countries: Canada (north), Mexico (south)
Strategic Importance: Controls key global sea lanes through naval power; hosts global financial system (USD reserve currency); leads NATO alliance
Important Maritime Routes: Atlantic Ocean, Pacific Ocean, Gulf of Mexico, Arctic routes, Panama Canal access
Important Geography: Two ocean borders provide strategic insulation; vast agricultural heartland; significant oil and gas reserves in Texas, Alaska, Gulf of Mexico

================================================================
ENERGY & RESOURCES
================================================================
Energy Resources: Largest producer of oil and natural gas globally; leading producer of renewable energy (wind, solar); extensive nuclear power capacity
Natural Resources: Coal, copper, gold, uranium, timber, rare earth minerals, natural gas, petroleum
Technology Sector: World leader — Silicon Valley, major AI and semiconductor companies (Apple, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Nvidia, Intel)
Major Companies: Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet (Google), Amazon, Nvidia, Meta, Tesla, Berkshire Hathaway, JPMorgan Chase, ExxonMobil

================================================================
SOCIETY
================================================================
Education Level: High — 90%+ adult literacy; ~50% hold tertiary degrees
Internet Penetration: ~93% of population
Human Development Index (HDI): 0.921 (Very High, ranked 20th)
Corruption Perceptions Index: 69/100 (ranked 24th)
Press Freedom Status: Mostly free; ranked ~45th globally by RSF — concerns over media polarization
Human Rights Overview: Strong constitutional protections but faces challenges in racial equality, mass incarceration, gun violence, and surveillance law reforms

================================================================
FOREIGN POLICY
================================================================
Current Leader Priorities: Democracy reinforcement abroad, climate action, strategic competition with China, managing Russia-Ukraine war, technology regulation, immigration reform
Foreign Policy Overview: Multilateralism (Biden admin); emphasis on alliances (NATO, Quad, AUKUS); strategic competition with China; support for Ukraine; engagement in Middle East peace processes
Relations With:
  USA: —
  China: Strategic competitor; intense rivalry in trade, technology, Taiwan, and South China Sea
  Russia: Adversarial — sanctions regime, support for Ukraine, arms control tensions
  India: Strategic partner; growing defence and technology cooperation (Quad, iCET)
  Pakistan: Fluctuating — security cooperation on Afghanistan, but tensions over terrorism and nuclear issues
  EU: Strong alliance; NATO and trade partnership; coordination on China and Russia
  Middle East: Key ally of Israel; engages with Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Gulf states; nuclear deal tensions with Iran
  Neighbouring Countries: Strong economic integration with Canada and Mexico (USMCA)

================================================================
ALLIANCES & RIVALRIES
================================================================
Major Allies: NATO members, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Israel, Saudi Arabia, India (strategic partner)
Major Rivals: China, Russia, Iran, North Korea

================================================================
NATIONAL INTERESTS & CHALLENGES
================================================================
Key National Interests: Maintain global economic and military leadership; protect critical infrastructure; secure supply chains; advance democracy and human rights; counter terrorism; address climate change
Current Major Challenges: Political polarization; national debt ($34+ trillion); China's rising influence; immigration policy; healthcare costs; gun violence; technology regulation (AI, social media)

================================================================
INTERESTING FACTS
================================================================
• The US has not had a federal official language.
• The US dollar is the world's primary reserve currency (~58% of global reserves).
• The US spends more on its military than the next 10 countries combined.
• Silicon Valley produces most of the world's leading AI and technology companies.
