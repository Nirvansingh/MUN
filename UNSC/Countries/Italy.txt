---------------------------------------------------------------

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Securing trade and shipping routes in the Mediterranean Sea ("Mare Nostrum").
• Protecting critical ports (Trieste, Genoa, Gioia Tauro) from cargo disruption.
• Securing maritime energy transit from North Africa (Algeria, Libya) and Azerbaijan.
• Protecting undersea energy pipelines and fiber-optic communication cables.
• Countering illegal smuggling and human trafficking across Mediterranean lanes.
• Ensuring maritime security in the Red Sea to protect national import volumes.
• Diversifying supplier supply chains within the European Single Market framework.
• Aligning with NATO and G7 defense strategies in the Mediterranean.

---------------------------------------------------------------

POSITION ON THE AGENDA
----------------------
• Supply Chains: Actively supports EU initiatives for supply chain diversification. Prioritizes energy supply chain security, replacing Russian gas with Mediterranean partners.
• Maritime Security: Strongly supports UNCLOS. Takes a leading command role in the European Union maritime coalition Operation Aspides in the Red Sea.
• Free Trade: Promotes open, rules-based free trade within the European Union and WTO frameworks.
• Sanctions: Fully supports EU-wide and G7 sanctions against Russia, Iran, and entities disrupting shipping.
• International Cooperation: Coordinates primarily through the EU, NATO, G7, and UN agencies.

---------------------------------------------------------------

HOT TOPICS
----------
• Command of EU's Operation Aspides in the Red Sea.
• Security of undersea energy and data cables in the Mediterranean.
• Transition to Algerian and North African natural gas supplies.
• Impact of Suez Canal shipping delays on Italian port container volume.
• Modernization and cybersecurity of Trieste and Genoa port hubs.
• Maritime border patrol and search-and-rescue policies.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STRENGTHS
---------
✓ Critical geographic position in the center of the Mediterranean Sea.
✓ Modern, capable navy (Marina Militare) with aircraft carrier assets.
✓ Command leadership in European maritime security operations.
✓ Major exporter of industrial machinery, automotive, and refined goods.
✓ High level of integration in the European Single Market.
✓ Advanced shipbuilding industry (Fincantieri) producing naval vessels.
✓ Close coordination with Mediterranean neighbors (France, Greece).

---------------------------------------------------------------

WEAKNESSES
----------
✗ High public debt, limiting rapid expansion of defense hardware.
✗ High dependency on maritime routes for energy and raw material imports.
✗ Exposure of domestic economy to political instability in North Africa (Libya).
✗ Coastal defense resources stretched by migration monitoring duties.
✗ Port container turnaround efficiency lags behind Northern European hubs.
✗ Susceptibility of domestic manufacturing to raw material logistics delays.

---------------------------------------------------------------

KEY DEFENCE POINTS
------------------
• Operation Aspides is a defensive, non-escalatory coalition protecting merchant transit.
• Mediterranean maritime security operations comply with international law and human rights.
• Energy partnerships in North Africa support mutual economic development and stability.
• Italy defends UNCLOS as the cornerstone of international maritime law.
• Port security measures comply with strict EU safety and environmental regulations.
• Italian port infrastructure is being modernized using EU recovery funds.
• Security of undersea data links is essential for global digital trade.
• Sanctions are target-oriented, designed to defend sovereignty and international law.

Q3. How does Italy secure its port databases from state-sponsored cyber disruptions?
