OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Asserts that motherhood and family care are the most sacred, dignified roles for women in an Islamic society, superior to capitalist labor exploitation.
• Cites the "Youthful Population Law" as progressive family policy offering marriage loans, subsidized housing, and maternal healthcare benefits.
• Highlights outstanding female educational achievements: women constitute over 60% of university students and 70% of STEM graduates.
• Denounces Western sanctions as "economic terrorism" that directly cripples Iran's healthcare and childcare infrastructure, harming ordinary mothers and children.
• Rejects UN scrutiny as politically motivated interference by Western imperialist powers attempting to enforce secular individualism.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Reverse demographic aging and population decline by increasing fertility rates through state incentives and legal restrictions on birth control.
• Evade and dismantle international unilateral sanctions that restrict access to medical imports and financial liquidity.
• Defend the constitutional legitimacy of its Islamic legal codes (Velayat-e Faqih) against multilateral human rights treaties.
• Build solidarity with non-aligned and anti-Western blocs (Russia, China, Global South) to resist diplomatic isolation.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ Exceptionally high female literacy rate (over 90%) and dominant female representation in higher education and STEM fields (over 60%)
✓ Highly advanced domestic medical and pharmaceutical sector providing universal maternal prenatal healthcare
✓ 9 months of fully paid maternity leave in the public sector with job retention guarantees
✓ Low infant mortality and broad access to clean water, electricity, and sanitation across rural villages
✓ Direct cash subsidies and housing loan preferences granted to families with three or more children under population laws

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Ranked #143 on WEF Gender Gap Index (ranked 144th in Economic Participation, 143rd in Political Empowerment)
✗ Extreme Economic Exclusion: Despite world-class education, female labor force participation is frozen at a dismal ~14%
✗ Legal Subordination: Civil Code Article 1117 explicitly allows husbands to legally prohibit wives from working; Article 1105 makes husband head of family
✗ State-Coerced Natality: The 2021 Population Law banned state distribution of contraceptives, eliminated prenatal screening subsidies, and increased penalties for abortion
✗ Violent Repression of Women's Protests: Documented executions, torture, sexual violence, and blinding of peaceful female protesters under the "Woman, Life, Freedom" crackdowns
✗ Complete Ban on CEDAW: One of only four countries refusing to ratify the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women

UNPAID CARE WORK SITUATION
--------------------------
• Iranian women perform an estimated 7 times more unpaid care and domestic work than men, spending an average of 4.5 hours daily on unpaid household duties.
• Women are culturally and legally viewed as the sole domestic caretakers, with civil law demanding total obedience ("tamkin") to the husband in exchange for maintenance ("nafaqa").

CHILDCARE & ELDERCARE INFRASTRUCTURE
------------------------------------
• Public daycare centers (Mahd-e Koudak) have declined due to austerity and inflation; private nurseries are unaffordable for working-class families.
• Eldercare rests exclusively on female family members, with minimal state nursing-home infrastructure due to cultural stigmas against institutionalizing parents.

DOMESTIC & MIGRANT WORKER POLICIES
----------------------------------
• Informal domestic cleaners and caregivers lack written employment contracts and are excluded from the Supreme Labor Council's formal wage oversight.
• Large population of Afghan refugee women performing underpaid, informal domestic and care labor without legal status or social security.

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• #143: Iran's ranking out of 146 nations on the WEF Global Gender Gap Index 2024.
• 14.4%: Female labor force participation rate (despite 60%+ university graduation rate).
• 7x: Disparity in time spent on unpaid care work by Iranian women compared to men.
• Article 1117: Civil Code provision giving husbands statutory veto power over wives' careers.
• December 2022: Historic date ECOSOC voted to expel Iran from the UN Commission on the Status of Women.

IMPLEMENTATION GAPS & HYPOCRISY
-------------------------------
• Boasts of educating its women while legally empowering husbands to ban their educated wives from ever entering an office.
• Claims to venerate motherhood while clubbing, imprisoning, and executing mothers and young women who demand basic bodily autonomy.
• Outlaws free family planning under population laws while galloping inflation leaves families unable to afford baby formula or diapers.
