This document provides quick-draft opening speech templates of varying lengths (30, 60, and 90 seconds) to adapt during the opening session of the Security Council.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DYNAMIC HOOK EXAMPLES FOR THE AGENDA
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use one of these hooks to start your opening speech:
• "When a missile strikes a container ship in the Red Sea, the shockwaves are felt in supermarkets in Europe, fuel pumps in Asia, and grain silos in Africa." (Trade Stability Focus)
• "Freedom of navigation is not a privilege granted by coastal states; it is a universal right established under UNCLOS." (Western Bloc Focus)
• "Sanctions are not legal tools of peace; they are instruments of economic coercion that fragment the global economy." (Sovereign Bloc Focus)

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 1: 30-SECOND SPEECH (approx. 70 words)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] calls upon this council to address the critical disruptions facing global maritime trade. Ongoing conflicts in the Red Sea and Black Sea threaten food and energy security, violating UNCLOS principles of free transit. We must condemn attacks on civilian merchant shipping, coordinate infrastructure cybersecurity, and protect supply chains. [Country Name] is ready to draft a balanced security framework. Thank you."

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 2: 60-SECOND SPEECH (approx. 130 words)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] stands before the Security Council to emphasize that maritime security is the lifeblood of global trade. 

Today, commercial shipping faces unprecedented threats near key choke points. Houthi attacks in the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait have forced ships to bypass the Suez Canal, raising shipping rates and carbon emissions. Unilateral, non-UN sanctions have forced the rise of an unregulated 'shadow fleet' of tankers, posing severe environmental risks to our shared oceans.

We must act collectively. [Country Name] calls for:
1. UN-coordinated patrols to secure vital maritime transit corridors.
2. Port inspections to enforce insurance and safety standards on the shadow fleet.
3. Enhanced protection for undersea communication and energy infrastructure.

We urge the Council to focus on rules-based stability and free trade. Thank you."

----------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 3: 90-SECOND SPEECH (approx. 200 words)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Thank you, Mr. President.

The delegation of [Country Name] addresses the Security Council at a time when geopolitical conflicts threaten to fragment the global trading system. 

Over 80% of global trade is transported by sea, passing through narrow choke points like the Straits of Hormuz, Malacca, and Bab-el-Mandeb. When these corridors are closed or threatened, the global economy suffers. Food prices rise, energy supplies are choked, and developing nations are hit the hardest.

To restore trade stability, we must address three critical areas:
First, freedom of navigation. We must coordinate defensive maritime safety operations under the UNCLOS framework. Commercial shipping must be protected from military targeting by state and non-state actors.
Second, infrastructure protection. Undersea fiber-optic cables and energy pipelines are critical civilian assets. We call for shared intelligence protocols to prevent physical sabotage.
Third, opposing protectionism. Decoupling and unilateral tech blocks fragment global supply chains. We must support multilateral trade rules under the WTO.

[Country Name] urges the P5 and all member states to find common ground. We must adopt a resolution that secures maritime corridors, regulates the shadow fleet, and protects global free trade.

Thank you."
