AGENDA RELEVANCE SCORE
==================================================

Importance to Committee: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Targetability: YELLOW (African Care Benchmark with Deep Structural Inequalities)

Why?
• African leader in domestic worker labor legislation: Ratified ILO Convention 189 and in 2022 equalized the domestic worker minimum wage to 100% of the national minimum wage
• Constitutional Court ruling in the Mahlangu case (2020): Struck down the exclusion of domestic workers from the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act (COIDA)
• Child Support Grant (CSG): Reaches over 13 million vulnerable children, paid overwhelmingly directly to female primary caregivers
• Immense structural challenges: Extreme female unemployment (~36%), triple burden of poverty, inequality, and unemployment, and severe rates of gender-based violence (GBV)
• Deep unpaid care burden: Millions of grandmothers ("Gogos") shoulder the unpaid care of orphaned children and sick family members affected by HIV/AIDS

Hot Topics
• Equalization of the domestic worker minimum wage to 100% of the National Minimum Wage (R27.58/hour in 2024)
• Implementation of the Mahlangu ruling: Registering domestic workers for workplace injury and death compensation (COIDA)
• The Child Support Grant (CSG) as a frontline buffer against extreme child poverty
• Extreme female unemployment and high informal economy proportion
• National Strategic Plan on Gender-Based Violence and Femicide (GBVF)

Expected Debate Frequency: High / Influential African Leader

OFFICIAL POSITION ON AGENDA
---------------------------
• Asserts that care work must be recognized as a constitutional right, anchored in Section 27 (healthcare, food, water, and social security) of the South African Constitution.
• Highlights the historic equalization of the domestic worker minimum wage to 100% of the national minimum wage under the National Minimum Wage Act.
• Promotes the Child Support Grant (CSG), providing monthly cash transfers to over 13 million children via female caregivers.
• Champions the expanded Parental Leave provisions under the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (10 days parental leave for fathers/partners).
• Demands global financial support and debt relief for developing nations to build public care, sanitation, and healthcare infrastructure.

NATIONAL INTERESTS
------------------
• Champion African developmental priorities and the 5R Framework on the international stage.
• Address catastrophic female unemployment without destabilizing fragile small business employment.
• Attract international climate and development financing to upgrade water and energy infrastructure in informal townships and rural homelands.
• Protect progressive constitutional labor standards against deregulation pressures.

STRENGTHS (PROS)
----------------
✓ Ratified ILO Convention No. 189 (Domestic Workers Convention) in 2013
✓ Equalized domestic worker minimum wage to 100% of the national minimum wage rate in 2022
✓ Landmark Constitutional Court victory (Mahlangu v Minister of Labour, 2020) including domestic workers in workplace injury and death compensation (COIDA)
✓ The Child Support Grant (CSG) reaching over 13 million children, proven to increase school attendance and maternal nutrition
✓ Four months of statutory maternity leave protected under the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA) with UIF benefits

WEAKNESSES (CONS)
-----------------
✗ Catastrophic Female Unemployment: Formal female unemployment exceeds 36% (expanded rate over 45%), trapping millions of women in poverty
✗ Domestic Worker Job Losses: The equalization of minimum wages, while progressive, caused hundreds of thousands of middle-class households to lay off domestic cleaners
✗ Severe Gender-Based Violence (GBV) Crisis: Femicide rates are five times higher than the global average, posing constant physical danger to female caregivers
✗ Chronic Infrastructure Failures: "Load shedding" (rolling electricity blackouts) and water shortages in townships drastically multiply female unpaid domestic labor
✗ The "Gogo" Care Burden: Millions of elderly grandmothers survive on meager state pensions while single-handedly caring for grandchildren orphaned by HIV/AIDS

IMPORTANT STATISTICS
--------------------
• 100%: Ratio of domestic worker minimum wage to the national minimum wage as of 2022.
• 13+ Million: Children supported by the monthly Child Support Grant (CSG).
• 36.1%: Official female unemployment rate in South Africa (among the highest in the world).
• 5x: South Africa's femicide rate relative to the global average.
• 2020: Year the Constitutional Court struck down domestic worker exclusion from COIDA (Mahlangu case).

PHILIPPINES-SOUTH AFRICA RELATIONS
----------------------------------
• Natural allies on domestic worker rights: Both nations are pioneers in their respective continents in ratifying ILO Convention 189 and legally protecting domestic workers.
• Can collaborate on establishing universal compensation frameworks for workplace injuries in domestic service.

GSL TALKING POINTS
------------------
• "South Africa has proven that constitutional courts and progressive legislation can overturn decades of apartheid-era exploitation, bringing domestic workers into full equality under the law."
• "However, progressive laws alone cannot overcome structural poverty if basic municipal infrastructure—water, electricity, and childcare—is absent. We must invest in public services to liberate women from time poverty."

RESOLUTION IDEAS
----------------
• Model Resolution Clause on Workplace Injury Compensation for Domestic Workers (based on the Mahlangu precedent).
• International commitments expanding direct child cash transfers to female primary caregivers in developing economies.
