This document provides formal, technical preambular and operative clauses suitable for final draft resolutions in the UNHRC.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FORMAL PREAMBULAR CLAUSES
----------------------------------------------------------------

• Reaffirming its resolutions HRC 34/7 of 23 March 2017 and HRC 48/4 of 7 October 2021 on the right to privacy in the digital age,

• Reclaiming the sovereign right of each state to regulate domestic communications and security systems to protect territorial integrity, counter-terrorism, and maintain public order in compliance with international law,

• Stressing that the sale, export, and deployment of dual-use surveillance technologies without human rights audits pose an imminent threat to the safety of journalists, human rights defenders, and civil society actors,

• Recognizing with concern that algorithmic bias in machine learning models can result in discriminatory practices in judicial sentencing, housing applications, and public welfare allocation,

----------------------------------------------------------------
FORMAL OPERATIVE CLAUSES
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Requests the Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR) to compile a comprehensive, biennial report on the human rights implications of generative AI and deep synthesis technologies, with a focus on mitigating political disinformation;

2. Urges member states to mandate Human Rights Impact Assessments (HRIAs) for all high-risk AI applications deployed in law enforcement, border control, and public administration before national licensing is granted;

3. Declares that the deployment of automated, real-time facial recognition in public spaces should be suspended until international human rights standards and independent oversight mechanisms are established;

4. Calls upon states to adopt legal frameworks requiring private tech companies to obtain explicit, opt-in consent before processing biometric data, and to guarantee users the right to delete their profiles;

5. Recommends that the UN establish a Global Digital Sandboxes Network to assist developing nations in testing AI models under secure, privacy-compliant conditions before public deployment;

6. Urges states to implement domestic legislation prohibiting the purchase of personal database registries from unregulated commercial data brokers by state security agencies without prior judicial warrants.
